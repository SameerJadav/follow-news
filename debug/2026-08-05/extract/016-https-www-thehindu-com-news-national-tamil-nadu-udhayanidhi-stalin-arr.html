Udhayanidhi Stalin arrest updates: Leader of Opposition says statement wasn't intended as double entendre

He accused the TVK government of filing the case to distract the public from the issues faced by farmers he raised.

Updated - August 05, 2026 01:15 am IST

The Hindu Bureau
SEE ALL Remove

DMK leader and the Leader of the Opposition in the Tamil Nadu Assembly Udhayanidhi Stalin speaks to the media in Tiruchirappalli on August 4, 2026. | Photo Credit: ANI

This LIVE is now closed

Leader of Opposition and DMK youth wing leader Udhayanidhi Stalin, who was arrested and brought to Thanjavur on a complaint alleging he made misogynistic remarks seemingly directed at a film actor, was released on Station Bail on Tuesday (August 4, 2026) night after being subjected to questioning at the Sengipatti Police Station.

Also read | Propriety in public discourse: On Udhayanidhi Stalin’s double entendre remarks

Earlier, amidst high drama and tension, Mr. Udhayanidhi Stalin was brought to Thanjavur by road, after being arrested in Chennai. He was questioned for over an hour at the Sengipatti Police Station in connection with the case booked against him on charges of making offensive remarks against a woman actor during the DMK’s demonstration on the Cauvery issue in Thanjavur on Monday (August 3, 2026).

Mr. Udhayanidhi Stalin walked out of the station at around 8.15 p.m. to a boisterous welcome from the assembled cadres. He waved to the crowd standing through the car’s sunroof and accepted their greetings.

A petition seeking anticipatory bail was filed before the Madras High Court in the morning. During the hearing in the afternoon, Justice G.K. Ilanthiraiyan directed the police to release Mr. Udhayanidhi Stalin today on station bail after questioning. He also directed the Leader of the Opposition to cooperate with the investigation and appear for inquiry as and when required. He is being interrogated at Sengipatti police station in Thanjavur district. 

  • August 04, 2026 23:45
    TVK govt. will crack down on insults against women, says Aadhav Arjuna

    Those who speak in a derogatory manner against women, irrespective of whether they are Opposition leaders, publishers of “yellow magazine”, or those operating fake accounts on social media platforms, would be identified and arrested, said TVK general secretary (election campaign management) and Minister for Public Works and Sports Development Aadhav Arjuna on Tuesday.

    Speaking at the meeting of MLAs of TVK and its alliance partners at the party headquarters in Panaiyur on the outskirts of Chennai ahead of the Budget session of the Assembly, Mr. Arjuna said this remains the TVK’s foremost policy and “just as corruption was being eliminated,” the culture of insulting women should also be eradicated.

    He said Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay had never indulged in personal criticism. “Our leader [Vijay] had referred to former Chief Minister M.K. Stalin as ‘Stalin Uncle’ once during a speech in Madurai, purely in the context of a relationship, and had stopped using the expression thereafter.”

    -R. Sivaraman
