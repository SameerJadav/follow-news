Will monitor NEET overhaul steps, take those extra miles: SC, Pg8

Supreme Court pledges rigorous oversight of NEET reforms, pushing for institutional overhaul and data security amidst paper leak concerns.

Key Highlights:

  • The Supreme Court announced it would closely monitor the Centre's overhaul of the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) process, particularly concerning paper leaks.
  • The Court cautioned that a mere shift to computer-based testing might not fully eliminate the risk of leaks.
  • The Centre assured the Supreme Court of its commitment to safeguarding the NEET process, stating it is going "10 extra miles".
  • The Supreme Court highlighted the recommendations of the K. Radhakrishnan Committee, formed in 2024, for reforming the National Testing Agency (NTA).
  • Prime Minister Narendra Modi recently announced the formation of fast-track courts to ensure swift justice in paper leak cases.

Detailed Insights:

  • The Supreme Court bench, including Justice P S Narasimha, emphasized the need for "complete and total institutionalisation" of the NEET process, moving beyond ad hoc solutions.
  • The Court specifically asked the Centre to detail actions taken regarding the K. Radhakrishnan Committee's recommendations on the NTA's governing board, transparency, and inclusion of domain experts.
  • Concerns were raised by the Court regarding the safety of data when NEET transitions to a digital mode, highlighting potential new avenues for leaks.
  • The K. Radhakrishnan Committee, established after the NEET-UG 2024 controversy, submitted 101 recommendations covering examination reforms, data security, and institutional restructuring of the NTA.
  • Key recommendations from the committee include minimizing outsourcing, implementing biometric and AI-based authentication, and using encrypted question papers.
  • The National Testing Agency (NTA), established in 2017, is an autonomous body under the Ministry of Education responsible for conducting major entrance examinations like NEET-UG and JEE (Main).
  • The Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024, passed in February 2024, aims to curb malpractices and impose stringent penalties for paper leaks and other unfair means in public examinations.

Key Concepts Involved:

  • National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET): A pre-medical entrance examination for admission to undergraduate medical and dental courses in India.
  • National Testing Agency (NTA): An autonomous organization under the Ministry of Education, India, responsible for conducting various entrance examinations for higher education.
  • K. Radhakrishnan Committee: A high-powered committee constituted in 2024 to recommend reforms for the National Testing Agency (NTA) following examination irregularities.
  • Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024: A legislation enacted to prevent and penalize malpractices in public examinations across India.
