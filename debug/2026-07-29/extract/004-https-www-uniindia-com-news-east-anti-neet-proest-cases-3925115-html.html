Withdrawal of 64 cases linked to NEET-UG protest initiated, 27 cases being withdrawn in Patna

Patna, July 28 (UNI) In compliance with the Bihar Government's decision and the directive issued by the Advocate General, the Public Prosecutor in Patna has filed applications before the competent court seeking withdrawal of prosecution in 27 cases registered in connection with the NEET-UG question paper leak protests.
During the nationwide protests against the alleged NEET-UG question paper leak scandal, including demonstrations in Delhi and several parts of Bihar, a total of 64 criminal cases were registered against protesters across the state on July 26, 2026. Of these, 27 cases were registered within Patna city.
In the state capital, 16 were registered at Kotwali Police Station, six at Gandhi Maidan Police Station, four at Secretariat Police Station, and one at Shastri Nagar Police Station.
Following the State Government's decision to withdraw these cases, the Advocate General issued directions to all District Magistrates to initiate the process for withdrawal of prosecution in the 64 cases.
Acting on these directions, District Magistrates requested their respective Public Prosecutors to move the courts for withdrawal.
Accordingly, separate applications have been filed under Section 360 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS).
One of the cases covered by the withdrawal applications is Gandhi Maidan Police Station Case No. 400/2026, registered against Tej Pratap Yadav, founder of the Jan Shakti Janata Dal. An application seeking withdrawal of prosecution in this case has also been submitted.
Once the court approves these applications, the withdrawal of prosecution will pave the way for the release of Tej Pratap Yadav and hundreds of other protesters currently lodged in jail in connection with the NEET-UG protest cases.
