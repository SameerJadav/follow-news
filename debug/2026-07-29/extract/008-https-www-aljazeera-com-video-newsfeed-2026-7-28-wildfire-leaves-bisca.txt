# Wildfire leaves Biscarrosse commercial district in ruins

A devastating wildfire in southwestern France has left parts of Biscarrosse’s commercial district in ruins, with business owners counting heavy losses. Some shops have reopened, but residents are on edge over reports of nearby flare-ups and lingering fire risks.
