There are no conflicting reports regarding the total number of students affected by the NEET UG 2026 paper leak. More than 2.27 million candidates appeared for the examination held on May 3, 2026, which was subsequently cancelled. One report also states that nearly 22 lakh students were affected.

Regarding the number of exam centers compromised, the provided information does not give a specific total number of compromised centers, nor are there conflicting claims about such a number. The investigation traced the alleged leak to Nashik, Maharashtra, with connections spreading to Sikar, Rajasthan, and involved a network across Rajasthan, Maharashtra, and other states. Individuals appointed as subject experts by the National Testing Agency (NTA) were found to have passed confidential questions through private coaching sessions. Students were also reportedly called to Pune a day before the exam to discuss questions in a private session.
