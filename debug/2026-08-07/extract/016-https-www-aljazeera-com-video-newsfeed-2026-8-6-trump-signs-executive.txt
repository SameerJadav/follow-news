# Trump signs executive orders banning so-called ‘birth tourism’

US President Donald Trump signed two executive orders to further limit who is eligible for birthright citizenship – a policy giving any child born in America automatic US citizenship. The US Supreme Court had already blocked his earlier effort in June.
