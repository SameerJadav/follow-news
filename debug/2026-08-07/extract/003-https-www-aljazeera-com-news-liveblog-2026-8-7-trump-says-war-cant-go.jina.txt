*     3m ago(00:07 GMT)    ## WATCH: Israeli air strikes hit southern Lebanon again as ceasefire talks stall

 Israeli forces have launched a second consecutive day of air strikes on southern Lebanon, targeting the town of Mansouri after an explosion killed two Israeli soldiers near Majdel Zoun.

Residents describe widespread destruction, damaged homes and renewed displacement as violence escalates despite ongoing US-backed ceasefire talks in Rome. Israel says its military operations are a response to violations of the ceasefire agreement, while Hezbollah has not claimed responsibility for the explosion.

The negotiations have shown little sign of progress, with Israel insisting on Hezbollah’s disarmament before any further troop withdrawals from southern Lebanon.

## A recap of recent developments

 

    *   US President Donald Trump expects the war on Iran to end “pretty soon,” telling reporters during questions on global and domestic energy prices that he does not think “they can go much longer.”
    *   Trump again dismisses claims of ammunition shortages, asserting that the US possesses “an unlimited supply” of weapons. Addressing reporters in Washington, he said that the country remains “in very good shape” with “literally massive amounts of ammunition.”
    *   Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian states that the US has applied “maximum” economic pressure, noting that Iran loses roughly 230 million cubic meters of gas daily as a result of US-Israeli attacks.
    *   Yemeni forces reported that Houthi strikes using suicide drones and ballistic missiles left at least 18 troops dead.
    *   The latest cycle of Rome talks between Lebanon and Israel has concluded, with the US State Department characterising the discussions as “fruitful”.
    *   Following a meeting in Amman, Jordan, several nations have condemned Israel’s escalating attacks in Gaza, while Hamas has urged the signatories to stop Israel “from sabotaging the agreement” in the region.
    *   UNICEF reports that at least 300 children have been killed in Gaza during the 300 days following the October 2025 “ceasefire,” averaging one child per day.
