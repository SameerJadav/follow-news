# Uproar in NY after ICE shoots man with child in the car

There are protests and outrage in New York after ICE agents shot a man after surrounding his vehicle. The victim had a five-year old child in the car at the time. New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani has called President Trump to demand ICE withdraw from the city.
