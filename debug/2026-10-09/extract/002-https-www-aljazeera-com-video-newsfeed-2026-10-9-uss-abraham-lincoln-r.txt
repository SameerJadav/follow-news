# USS Abraham Lincoln returns after record-breaking deployment

Families cheered and embraced their loved ones as the USS Abraham Lincoln returned home to San Diego after more than 300 days at sea to support the US-led war on Iran. The extended deployment led to supply shortages, facility breakdowns and reports of suicide attempts.
