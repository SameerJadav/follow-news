# Police use tear gas and water cannons on student protestors in Paris

Police fired tear gas and water cannons as demonstrators gathered in Paris to demand more funding, teachers, and better conditions in schools. The movement has entered its third week, with police tactics under scrutiny after several teenagers were seriously injured.
