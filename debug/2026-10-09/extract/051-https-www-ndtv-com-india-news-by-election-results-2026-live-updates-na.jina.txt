**By-Election Results 2026 LIVE:**Votes are being counted for [bypolls in five assembly constituencies](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/byelection-2026-live-updates-nandigram-rejinagar-nagaon-lok-sabha-dharapuram-madurantakam-west-bengal-tamil-nadu-assam-puducherry-voting-live-news-upd-12143555) in West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, and Puducherry, along with a Lok Sabha seat in Assam.

In Bengal, the [bypoll was held in Nandigram](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/mamatas-leg-was-not-broken-in-nandigram-suvendu-adhikari-announces-probe-into-mamata-banerjees-2021-injury-ahead-of-by-election-12137108), which was vacated by West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari after he chose to retain Bhabanipur where he had defeated former chief minister Mamata Banerjee, and in Rejinagar, which was vacated by Aam Janata Unnayan Party (AJUP) chief [Humayun Kabir](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/nandigram-madurantakam-dharapuram-high-turnout-for-byp-olls-in-3-states-puducherry-humayun-kabir-arrested-in-bengal-12147927) after he won both Rejinagar and Naoda in the 2026 assembly elections.

In Tamil Nadu, votes are being counted for [bypolls in the Madurantakam (SC) and Dharapuram (SC) Assembly constituencies](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/ahead-of-tamil-nadu-bypolls-vijay-alleges-deal-between-mk-stalins-dmk-e-palaniswami-12121876), which were won in the recent elections by AIADMK's Maragatham Kumaravel and P Sathyabama, respectively. The two leaders were among a group of rebel AIADMK legislators who backed Chief Minister Vijay's Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) government in a floor test.
