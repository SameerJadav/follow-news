## A recap of recent developments

 

    *   US President Donald Trump has rejected a news report claiming his administration offered Iran sanctions relief and access to frozen funds. Trump called the story a hoax on social media.
    *   Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says Tehran has held further indirect talks with the US through Qatari mediators in New York, with Washington’s formal response expected by Tuesday, “hopefully”.
    *   UAE President Mohamed bin Zayed (MBZ) hosted Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu over the weekend, the Gulf state’s official news agency has confirmed.
    *   Iraqi Airways has resumed flights between Najaf and Iran following efforts led by Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi and protests against the decision to suspend Iranian flights.
    *   Yemeni government forces claim more than 350 attacks on Houthi targets in the past 24 hours.
    *   Israeli demolitions in the occupied West Bank have displaced 147 Palestinians in one week, the UN’s OCHA says.
