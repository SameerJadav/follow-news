# Nepal hit by deadly floods, landslides, and avalanche

Heavy rain has triggered deadly floods and landslides across Nepal, killing at least 21 people and leaving five missing, according to the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority. An avalanche on Himlung Himal has left 12 local workers missing.
