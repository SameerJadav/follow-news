# El-Sayed wins Michigan Democratic Senate primary

Abdul el-Sayed has won Michigan’s Democratic senate primary, defeating Haley Stevens in one of the country’s most closely-watched races. The contest became a proxy battle over the Democratic Party’s future, including divisions over US support for Israel.
