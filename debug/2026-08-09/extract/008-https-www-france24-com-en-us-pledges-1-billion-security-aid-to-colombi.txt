# US pledges $1 billion security aid to Colombia as Trump-endorsed president takes office

 Americas 

Issued on: 08/08/2026 - 08:16 Modified: 08/08/2026 - 10:09

Share

The US State Department announced late Friday that President Donald Trump's ​administration plans to provide $1 billion in security assistance to Colombia as the country's new right-wing President Abelardo De La Espriella was sworn into office.De la Espriella, who calls himself "The Tiger", vowed to "defeat narco-terrorists" in his first presidential speech at a military garrison in Cali.
