Artist Jennifer Teresa Villanueva has captured her family's everyday life through her lens for eight years, revealing moments of love, care and sacrifice within their Chicago home. The personal project took root in 2018, sparked by her grandmother's hospitalization. It started as a way to better understand her grandmother's daily routines, and soon it blossomed into a heartfelt exploration of the immigrant experience. "I am interested in what happens when the camera enters a home where everyone already knows one another," Villanueva told NPR. "There is no distance between photographer and subject. I am photographing the people who raised me, and I am also photographing the home that raised me." See photos from the project and read more about Villanueva's work.
