# Trump campaigning in Republican strongholds amid slide in polls

 Americas 

Issued on: 20/10/2026 - 22:13 Modified: 20/10/2026 - 22:13

Share

With the midterm elections fast approaching, US President Donald Trump is stumping for candidates in states that he won by double digits in 2024. Despite what he says, it’s a sign of what Trump and the Republican party are fearing: with polls showing GOP support slip drastically even in some deep red states.
