Biometric verification is an important part of the NEET UG 2026 security system. It helps make sure that only genuine candidates are allowed to enter the exam hall, reducing the chances of impersonation or fraud.

For NEET UG 2026, candidates will go through fingerprint-based biometric verification before entering the exam centre. The candidate's fingerprint will be scanned at the entry point and will be matched with official NTA records. This mandatory identity verification for every candidate will make sure that only the registered candidates are appearing in the NEET UG exam.

To handle technical issues, NTA has also arranged backup systems this time. If NEET 2026 biometric verification does not work due to reasons like device failure, network problems, unclear fingerprints, or medical conditions, candidates will still be allowed to enter.

In such cases, a manual verification will be done using valid ID proof. Candidates will need to submit a written undertaking at the NEET exam centre in the presence of the Centre Superintendent or Observer. This ensures that the process remains fair and consistent for everyone. NTA has also specified that candidates will not be interrupted during the exam time (2 PM to 5 PM) for any biometric checks.
