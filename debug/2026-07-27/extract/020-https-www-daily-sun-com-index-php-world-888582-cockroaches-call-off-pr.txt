# ‘Cockroaches’ call off protests after talks with India govt, minister’s resignation

CJP spokespersons and Indian ministers address a joint press conference held at the Constitution Club of India in New Delhi on Saturday. Photo: Collected

The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) has called off protests “in good faith” and the government has agreed to drop all legal measures including lawsuits against the student demonstrators, both sides said at a joint press conference on Saturday afternoon.

At the press conference, cabinet members also said they would work on the students’ demand for Tk1 crore compensation for the families of those who took their leaves in anguish over the medical entry question paper leak – a grave tragedy that started off the protests.

The government has agreed to consider the remaining demands of the movement after the resignation of Dharmendra Pradhan as education minister, CJP Spokesperson Saurav Das said at the presser held at the Constitution Club of India in New Delhi.

The government will formally announce the withdrawal of legal action in writing on Tuesday, Saurav Das said, adding another round of talks will be held with the government in four weeks.

At the presser, Health Minister JP Nadda said that the central government has agreed not to take legal action against any of the protestors. Nadda also said that the Centre will soon fix the compensation amount for the families of the children who died by suicide after question leaks.
