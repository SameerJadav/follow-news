Nandan Nilekani is an Indian entrepreneur, best known as the co-founder and chairman of Infosys, a prominent IT services company.

His specific expertise and background relevant to leading a task force on examination reform stem from his extensive experience in leveraging technology for large-scale public infrastructure projects in India. He served as the founding chairman of the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) from 2009 to 2014, where he was instrumental in developing and implementing Aadhaar, India's biometric digital identity program. This role involved creating and scaling a technology platform for a vast population. Nilekani also chaired the Technology Advisory Group for Unique Projects (TAGUP), which provided recommendations for technology-based reforms in major government initiatives, including the Goods and Services Tax (GST) system. More recently, in January 2023, he was appointed co-chair of the G20 Task Force on Digital Public Infrastructure for Economic Transformation, Financial Inclusion, and Development.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced on July 26, 2026, that Nilekani would lead a high-powered task force to recommend measures for creating a leak-proof, technology-driven examination system. His appointment highlights the government's intention to centralize technology in the reform effort, drawing on his experience in building and scaling secure digital platforms for public use. The task force's objectives include integrating technology, strengthening question paper security, and implementing secure biometric and digital multi-factor identity verification to prevent impersonation.
