Shashikant Suthar, a chemistry teacher from Sikar, Rajasthan, initially reported or exposed the NEET UG 2026 paper leak. He discovered a "guess paper" PDF circulating in Telegram groups that contained nearly 140 questions matching the actual NEET-UG examination paper, including identical wording, sequence, and punctuation marks. Suthar then informed authorities, sending emails to the National Testing Agency (NTA), the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI), and the Ministry of Education.
