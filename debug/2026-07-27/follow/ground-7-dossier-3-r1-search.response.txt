Dharmendra Pradhan resigned as Union Education Minister on Saturday, July 25, 2026, amid widespread student protests and a hunger strike over the NEET-UG paper leak. In a public letter, he stated that his resignation was not a matter of personal prestige, but was driven by the need to protect the future of students and to prevent "anti-national forces" from exploiting the protests at Jantar Mantar and elsewhere in the country. He also claimed that his exit was to free students from legal uncertainty so they could focus on their studies and careers.

Pradhan defended the government's handling of the NEET-UG crisis and his own role, stating that he took full responsibility from day one and was determined not to let the potential of meritorious students be ruined by an "exam mafia" or allow injustice to befall any student. He expressed deep pain that some "individuals in responsible positions" continued to mislead students and create obstacles even after the examination was completed. He ended his letter on an emotional note, stating that serving the nation would always be his highest priority and that he would continue working for the aspirations of Mother India, the people of Odisha, and the youth of the country.
