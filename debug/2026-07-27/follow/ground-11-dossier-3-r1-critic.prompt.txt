SUBJECT: India's Widespread Exam Paper Leak Scandal and Student Protests

LEDGER SO FAR:
- 2024-06-22: The High-Level Committee of Experts, chaired by Dr K. Radhakrishnan, was constituted by the Union Ministry of Education after the NEET (UG) paper leaks.
- 2024-08: The Supreme Court widened the mandate of the Radhakrishnan committee.
- 2024-10-21: The Radhakrishnan committee submitted its report with 101 recommendations.
- 2025-04: The Union Ministry of Education made the Radhakrishnan committee report public.
- 2025-12-08: The 371st Report of the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Education, chaired by Digvijaya Singh, was tabled in the Rajya Sabha.
- 2026-05-03: The NEET UG 2026 examination was conducted for over 2.27 million aspirants seeking admission to undergraduate medical and dental courses.
- 2026-05-03: A teacher from a coaching center in Sikar, Rajasthan, discovered a handwritten 'guess paper' that was identical to the actual NEET UG 2026 exam paper.
- 2026-05-03: A handwritten copy of the NEET UG 2026 question paper was scanned and converted into PDF files in Rajasthan by the father of an aspirant and then circulated to students in coaching centers.
- 2026-05-04: The teacher who discovered the paper leak initially approached the Udyog Nagar police station to report the issue but did not file a written complaint.
- 2026-05-06: Initial reports and social media virality regarding alleged paper leaks from specific examination centers in various states began.
- 2026-05-07: The teacher officially lodged a complaint with the National Testing Agency (NTA), providing a 60-page handwritten PDF containing chemistry and biology questions that matched the actual exam.
- 2026-05-09: The National Testing Agency (NTA) initially denied a widespread leak of the NEET UG 2026 paper.
- 2026-05-12: The NEET UG 2026 examination was officially cancelled by the NTA following investigations that revealed significant overlaps between a pre-circulated guess paper and the actual question paper.
- 2026-05-12: The Director General of the NTA, Abhishek Singh, stated that while the 'entire paper was never available on Telegram or any other channel,' some questions did match.
- 2026-05-12: The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) officially took over the investigation into the NEET UG 2026 paper leak.
- 2026-05-15: The National Testing Agency (NTA) announced that the re-examination for NEET UG 2026 would take place on June 21, 2026, and students were promised refunds of their application fees.
- 2026-05-16: The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) established its demands, including compensation for student suicides, legislative oversight, gender equity, independent media, and electoral reforms.
- 2026-05-22: Manisha Sanjay Havaldar, a school headmistress and NTA subject expert, was arrested for allegedly leaking the physics question paper.
- 2026-06: Protests began to demand accountability for mismanagement of medical entrance examinations.
- 2026-06: The founder of the Cockroach Janta Party began organizing protests.
- 2026-06-19: Abhijeet Dipke made a social media post asking, 'What if all cockroaches came together?', which sparked the creation of the Cockroach Janta Party.
- 2026-06-21: The NEET UG 2026 re-examination was conducted.
- 2026-07-16: The result of the NEET UG 2026 re-examination was declared.
- 2026-07-18: Activist Sonam Wangchuk was forcibly removed from Jantar Mantar and admitted to a public hospital after 26 days of a hunger strike.
- 2026-07-20: Police and security forces used tear gas, batons, and allegedly pellet guns against student protesters marching towards Parliament in Delhi, resulting in numerous injuries.
- 2026-07-20: Sahil Lochab, a 19-year-old student, sustained pellet-like injuries to his right eye, face, neck, chest, and shoulder during student demonstrations in Delhi.
- 2026-07-20: An RAF jawan was photographed holding what appeared to be a pellet gun during the protests in Delhi.
- 2026-07-20: One RAF personnel fired seven rounds from his pellet gun during the student protests in Delhi.
- 2026-07-20: Authorities suspended metro services and mobile internet in Delhi during the protests.
- 2026-07-21: Sahil Lochab underwent surgery at the AIIMS Jai Prakash Narayan Apex Trauma Centre to remove pellets and repair his injured eye.
- 2026-07-21: Doctors informed Sahil Lochab's family that there was only a '1% chance' of vision returning to his right eye due to pupil damage.
- 2026-07-21: Political opposition leaders criticized the police crackdown on student protests, calling the government 'insensitive' and 'authoritarian'.
- 2026-07-21: Delhi police temporarily detained several top opposition leaders after a nearly three-hour protest in front of Prime Minister Modi’s residence.
- 2026-07-21: A 23-year-old NEET aspirant died by suicide in Dehradun due to 'dissatisfaction over excelling in her studies'.
- 2026-07-22: Sahil Lochab's first operation to remove particles from his pellet wounds in his shoulder, chest, and face was performed.
- 2026-07-22: An Outlook reporter sustained multiple puncture wounds on his arm from a weapon matching the description of a pellet gun during the protests.
- 2026-07-22: A 17-year-old student died after allegedly falling from the sixth floor of a residential building in Ahmedabad while preparing for the NEET re-test.
- 2026-07-23: Doctors reportedly offered some reassurance to Sahil Lochab's family that the chances of his vision improving might be increasing, though his prognosis remains 'guarded'.
- 2026-07-23: Human Rights Watch reported that Indian security forces used tear gas and batons to suppress largely peaceful student and youth protests in New Delhi on July 20.
- 2026-07-23: A special fast-track court was constituted at Delhi's Rouse Avenue Courts to exclusively handle offenses under the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024.
- 2026-07-25: Dharmendra Pradhan resigned as Union Education Minister following student protests over the NEET UG 2026 paper leak.
- 2026-07-25: Dharmendra Pradhan stated in a public letter that his resignation was to protect the future of students and prevent 'anti-national forces' from exploiting the protests.
- 2026-07-25: Youth activist group Cockroach Janta Party called off its protests after the government accepted its demands, including Pradhan's resignation and dropping legal measures against student demonstrators.
- 2026-07-25: Health Minister JP Nadda stated that the central government would soon fix the compensation amount for the families of children who died by suicide after question leaks.
- 2026-07-26: Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced the formation of a high-powered task force led by Nandan Nilekani to make India's examination system leak-proof and recommend structural and technological reforms.
- 2026-07-26: Union Minister Pralhad Joshi officially took charge of the Ministry of Education at Kartavya Bhavan.
- 2026-07-27: Prime Minister Modi stated that new legislation tightening laws and penalties for exam paper leaks will be introduced in Parliament.
- 2026-07-27: The Union government introduced the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026, in the Lok Sabha.
- 2026-07-27: Rahul Gandhi sent a letter to Home Minister Amit Shah demanding accountability for police actions against student protesters in Delhi on July 20.
- 2026-07-27: Following campaigns over paper leaks, the newly formed E20 Janta Party demanded the government provide consumers an option to buy 100% petrol and called for the resignation of minister Nitin Gadkari.
- 2026-07-27: Telangana Chief Minister A Revanth Reddy announced the state assembly will pass a resolution in August to recommend lowering the minimum Lok Sabha candidacy age to 21 from 25.
- 2026-07-27: The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) warned of resuming protests, stating there was a 'breach of agreement' regarding no police action against protesters.
- 2026-07-27: The fast-track court at Delhi's Rouse Avenue Courts adjourned the bail pleas of Dinesh Biwal and Vikas Biwal to August 3, as no CBI lawyer appeared.
- 2026-07-27: The fast-track court directed the director of prosecution to appoint a public prosecutor for the CBI.
- 2026-07-27: The Supreme Court of India is hearing petitions concerning alleged police excesses during protests related to the NEET UG 2026 paper leak.
- 2026-07-27: The Bihar government indicated plans to withdraw cases against those booked during NEET paper leak protests.
