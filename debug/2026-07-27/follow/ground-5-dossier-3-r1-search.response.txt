On July 20, 2026, in Delhi, police and security forces used tear gas, batons, and allegedly pellet guns against student protesters marching towards Parliament. Human Rights Watch reported that Indian security forces used tear gas and batons to suppress largely peaceful student and youth protests. Amnesty International India also stated that the police reportedly used unnecessary and excessive force, including batons, tear gas, and stone-throwing, and installed barricades to prevent protesters from reaching parliament. The Delhi Police denied using pellet guns, stating they neither possess nor use them. However, multiple reports and witness accounts allege that Rapid Action Force (RAF) personnel, a wing of the Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), used pellet guns. An RAF jawan was photographed holding what appeared to be a pellet gun during the protests. A report indicated that one RAF personnel fired seven rounds from his pellet gun.

These actions led to numerous injuries, with at least four people suffering pellet injuries. Sahil Lochab, a 19-year-old Delhi University student, was among those injured, sustaining pellet-like injuries to his right eye, face, neck, chest, and shoulder. Doctors informed his family that there is only a "1% chance" of vision returning to his right eye due to pupil damage. Lochab's family and a hospital source confirmed that his injuries were caused by pellets.
