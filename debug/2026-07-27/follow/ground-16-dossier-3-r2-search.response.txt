=== BLOCK q0 ===
Beyond the Delhi demonstrations on July 20, student groups across India have undertaken various protest actions regarding the exam leaks. In Mumbai, over 150 individuals were detained during protests. Clashes erupted in Bihar, and thousands marched in Tamil Nadu, demanding the abolition of NEET. Protesters also rallied in Guwahati, Haryana, and Kerala, calling for the education minister's resignation. In Kota, a major coaching hub, students held rallies and massive sit-ins and demonstrations at public spaces like the Dussehra Ground, seeking justice for students who died by suicide due to exam-related distress. In Uttarakhand, local student groups and aspirants held sit-ins and thousands marched toward the Uttarakhand Secretariat, leading to clashes with police. Lawyers in Haryana also observed a work suspension in solidarity with the student protests.

=== BLOCK q1 ===
Student protesters have primarily utilized a range of tactics, including nationwide demonstrations, sit-ins, hunger strikes, rallies, and marches. The "Cockroach Janta Party" (CJP), a youth-led movement, spearheaded many of these protests. The movement began as an online response and was fueled by memes and Instagram reels. Protesters marched to the Parliament of India on July 20, demanding the resignation of Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan. Activist Sonam Wangchuk also undertook an indefinite hunger strike at the protest site in Jantar Mantar, Delhi, starting June 28. The protests also called for broader reforms to India's education system, compensation for families of students who died by suicide following exam paper leaks, and a commitment that no legal action would be taken against those who participated in the protests.
