Sahil Lochab, a 19-year-old student, sustained severe injuries, including to his right eye, from pellet guns during protests on July 20, 2026. He underwent surgery on July 21, 2026, at the AIIMS Jai Prakash Narayan Apex Trauma Centre to remove pellets and repair the injured eye. Doctors initially informed his family that there was only a "1% chance" of vision returning to his right eye due to pupil damage. His uncle, Ajay Duggal, stated that Sahil cannot see from his right eye at all, describing it as "pitch black" with no blur or haze, and he is experiencing a lot of pain.

Lochab is awaiting another surgery, which his childhood friend Parvesh hopes will determine if his vision will return to normal. On Thursday, July 23, 2026, doctors reportedly offered some reassurance to the family, stating that the chances of his vision improving might be increasing. However, his visual prognosis is still described as "guarded," meaning the chances of recovering vision remain uncertain. A senior AIIMS official confirmed pellet injuries on the right side of his body and stated he is stable and on painkillers, but it is premature to conclude the extent of his vision loss. Besides the eye injury, Sahil also suffered multiple pellet injuries to his face, neck, chest, right shoulder, and arm, with one pellet found lodged in his right lung and in the pericardial fat (tissue surrounding the heart).
