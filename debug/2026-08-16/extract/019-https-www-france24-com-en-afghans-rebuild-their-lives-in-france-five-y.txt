# Afghans rebuild their lives in France five years after Taliban takeover

 Afghanistan 

Issued on: 15/08/2026 - 11:13

Share

Five years after the Taliban seized power, thousands of Afghans who fled to France are rebuilding their lives while facing challenges including language, housing and employment. For women like refugee doctor Fardina Samadi, France offers freedoms denied under Taliban rule, but rebuilding a career and integrating into French society remain difficult.
