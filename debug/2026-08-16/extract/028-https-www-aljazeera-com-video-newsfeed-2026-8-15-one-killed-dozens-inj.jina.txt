# One killed, dozens injured after wildfire hits Croatian coast

At least one person was killed and around 42 others were injured after a violent wildfire swept through a coastal town in Croatia. Nearly 200 people were rescued, prompting Prime Minister Andrej Plenković to call it “one of the worst fires in recent years.”
