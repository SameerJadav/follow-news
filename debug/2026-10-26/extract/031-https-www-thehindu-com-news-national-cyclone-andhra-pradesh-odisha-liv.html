Monsoon tracker LIVE: Depression over Bay of Bengal set to become ‘very severe cyclonic storm’, cross A.P. coast by October 28

IMD says the cyclone is likely to cross the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts on Wednesday morning, with winds of 115 kmph to 125 kmph, gusting to 140 kmph; set to have worse impact than last year’s Montha cyclone

Updated - October 26, 2026 01:04 am IST

The Hindu Bureau
SEE ALL Remove

Clouds build over Visakhapatnam as coastal Andhra Pradesh braces for a cyclonic system forming in the Bay of Bengal. | Photo Credit: K.R. Deepak

Under the influence of the impending Very Severe Cyclonic Storm over the Westcentral Bay of Bengal, extremely heavy rainfall is likely over Coastal Andhra Pradesh on October 27 and 28, Odisha and Telangana on October 28 and Chhattisgarh on October 28 and 29, 2026.

The Depression over Eastcentral and adjoining Southeast Bay of Bengal moved southwestwards with speed of 5 kmph during past 6 hours, intensified into a deep Depression and lay centered at 11:30 a.m. today, over the same region, near Latitude 15.1°N and Longitude 91.0°E, about 420 km north-northwest of Sri Vijaya Puram (Andaman Islands), 870 km east-southeast of Vishakhapatnam (Andhra Pradesh), 960 km east-southeast of Kakinada (Andhra Pradesh), 1060 km east-Southeast of Machilipatnam (Andhra Pradesh) and 1130 km east of Bapatla (Andhra Pradesh).

It is very likely to move nearly southwestwards, and intensify further into a cyclonic storm over eastcentral & adjoining southeast Bay of Bengal by tomorrow early morning. Then it is very likely to move west-northwestwards over the Eastcentral & adjoining Westcentral Bay of Bengal and intensify into a severe cyclonic storm over westcentral Bay of Bengal around Tuesday (October 27, 2026) morning.

Thereafter, it is very likely to move northwestwards, intensify further into a very severe cyclonic storm and reach Westcentral Bay of Bengal near Andhra Pradesh coast around morning of October 28, 2026. It is very likely to cross Andhra Pradesh & Yanam coasts between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam as a very severe cyclonic storm with the maximum sustained wind speed of 115-125 kmph gusting to 140 kmph by forenoon of 28th October, 2026.

Meanwhile, the Regional Meteorological Centre (RMC) has forecast heavy to very heavy rain at isolated places in Tamil Nadu’s Chennai, northern, delta and western districts.
