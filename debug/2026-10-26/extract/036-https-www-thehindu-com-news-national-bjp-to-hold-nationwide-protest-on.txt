BJP slams Kharge over ‘indecent’ remarks on PM Modi storyline 1 Mr. Kharge’s remarks at a Patna event on Sunday (October 25, 2026), in which he dragged Mr. Modi’s late father, triggered a sharp reaction from senior Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) leaders, who accused the principal Opposition, under Rahul Gandhi’s leadership, of increasingly resorting to personal attacks against Mr. Modi and his family. Criticising the “Modi ka baap bhi jayega [even Modi’s father will go]” comment, Union Home Minister Amit Shah said Mr. Kharge has “torn apart all norms of decorum”.
