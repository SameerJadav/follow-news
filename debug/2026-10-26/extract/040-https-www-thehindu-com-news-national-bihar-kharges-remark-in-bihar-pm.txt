NEW DELHI The BJP on Sunday (October 25, 2002) hit out at Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge for his remarks against Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The party said it would hold nationwide protests on Monday (October 26, 2026) against the “inappropriate and offensive remarks”. Kharge sharply retorted, ‘Modi ka baap bhi jayega’ (Even Modi’s father will go), sparking a major ruckus at the event. The incident occurred during the 139th birth anniversary celebration of Bihar’s first Chief Minister, Dr. Shri Krishna Singh.
