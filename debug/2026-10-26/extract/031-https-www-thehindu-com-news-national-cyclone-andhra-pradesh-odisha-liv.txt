Then it is very likely to move west-northwestwards over the Eastcentral & adjoining Westcentral Bay of Bengal and intensify into a severe cyclonic storm over westcentral Bay of Bengal around Tuesday (October 27, 2026) morning.

Thereafter, it is very likely to move northwestwards, intensify further into a very severe cyclonic storm and reach Westcentral Bay of Bengal near Andhra Pradesh coast around morning of October 28, 2026.
