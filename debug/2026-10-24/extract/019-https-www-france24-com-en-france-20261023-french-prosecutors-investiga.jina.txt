A gunman firing from his car killed one person and wounded five – including a 12-year-old girl – in the Paris suburb of [Trappes](https://www.google.com/search?q=trappes%20map&rlz=1C1GCEA_enFR1230FR1230&oq=trappes%20map&gs_lcrp=EgZjaHJvbWUqBggAEEUYOzIGCAAQRRg7MgYIARBFGDwyBggCEEUYPNIBCDE1ODNqMGo3qAIAsAIA&sourceid=chrome&source=chrome.ob&ie=UTF-8) on Friday morning.

Speaking from the city in the evening, [Macron](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/emmanuel-macron/) told reporters the victims had been targeted solely due to their ‌religion and the color ​of their skin.

“What happened today marks a turning ​point for ​the Republic and our ​country. What happened today ​was a terrorist and racist attack,” he said.

Trappes mayor Ali Rabeh said the man was “firing at random, wanting to cause a bloodbath” as he drove through Trappes and neighbouring La Verriere.

Most of the victims were from foreign backgrounds and prosecutors are investigating whether the acts targeted the “real or supposed race, ethnicity, nationality or religion” of the victims.

The 12-year-old girl shot eight times was “seriously wounded” but in a stable condition, the mayor of La Verriere, Nicolas Dainville, told AFP.

Police arrested the alleged shooter – a 39-year-old man, according to a source familiar with the case – in nearby Coignieres.

Officers found him in possession of a submachine gun and tear-gas grenades, according to a police source.

He had also booby-trapped his flat with three gas canisters and electric wires “connected to a timer”, according to anti-terrorism prosecutors.

Prosecutors said they had opened an investigation for “murder in connection with a terrorist enterprise”.

## Bursts of gunfire

Rabeh said the man was firing sporadically from his car and that the person killed was “of African origin”.

One of the wounded, “in critical condition, is of north African origin”, he added.

It was “objectively a racist, probably Islamophobic attack,” the mayor said, calling for national unity as the toll rose from an earlier count of four wounded.

Dainville said that two of the wounded in his town – cleaners attending to a school Friday morning when the shooter got out of his car to open fire – were of African origin.

[Laurent Nunez](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/laurent-nunez/), France’s interior minister, travelled to the two affected towns. He called the shooting “clearly a terrorist crime”.

A police source told AFP that the man had fired in bursts from his vehicle around 10:30am (0830 GMT).

Adel, a resident of Trappes, about 20 kilometres west of Paris, said his “heart was broken” as he learned the victims might have been targeted for their race.

“I’m north African and it’s frightening for everyone who has the same background,” the 50-year-old told AFP.

Samy, another resident, told AFP he had seen a body on the ground from his window.

His mother, he said, saw a grey Renault Clio car speeding by and heard “loud bangs”.

## ‘Terrible ordeal’

Writing on X, Prime Minister [Sébastien Lecornu](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/s%C3%A9bastien-lecornu/) said the case was “very serious” and that “all resources of the security forces and the judiciary are fully mobilised”.

Macron earlier in the day said “the nation is standing with” the victims of this “terrible ordeal”.

The shooting came with France on edge following [last weekend’s riots](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/french-student-protests/) on the sidelines of mass protests over the cost of living, some of the worst street violence in recent years.
