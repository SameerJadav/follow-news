**New Delhi:**
The Delhi High Court on Friday rejected a petition by Neha Bora, president of the All India Students' Association, in which she'd challenged a notice asking her to sign a peace bond before a special executive magistrate over her participation in the recent wave of protests, particularly a Mandi House gathering on October 16, over alleged electoral fraud.

Justice Subramonium Prasad, who had reserved the verdict after hearing arguments on Thursday, pronounced the order after 6 pm. The notice, issued by the SEM court in New Delhi district on October 17, the day Bora was released from detention, directed her to appear before the magistrate and execute a personal bond of ₹10,000 with one surety.
