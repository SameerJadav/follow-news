US President Donald Trump on Sunday (local time) stepped up his criticism of Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, accusing him of not wanting to end the ongoing war with Russia.

In a lengthy Truth Social post, Trump wrote, “Zelenskyy doesn’t care, and frankly, with all of the money that Europe is famously giving him, and that Crooked Joe Biden gave him in the past, he doesn’t want to end this war, and probably never did. He could have made a better deal two years ago than he can now. His country is in shambles, a large portion of his land is lost, his oceans and seas are gone, and his male population is low, wounded, and/or dead. I WANT THIS WAR END!”

He also slammed Zelenskyy after Kyiv launched one of its biggest drone attacks this year on Russia overnight on Saturday, striking a key oil terminal in defiance of Trump's demand that it stop attacking Moscow's energy infrastructure.

Trump's remarks came two days after he waived some key Russia-related sanctions under the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act, citing national interest as the reason for the waiver. Defending his decision to waive sanctions against Russia, Trump wrote in the post, "My decision on Russia is a very simple one. I am in the process of negotiating Peace in the war between Russia and Ukraine, and don’t want to do anything that will drive, foolishly, Russia away from the bargaining table."

He added, "Great progress is being made, and that includes current hostage negotiations! Signing that document at this time will do that, and I can sign it later, or any time that I want! Perhaps more importantly, Russia is doing very little, if anything, to help the Islamic Republic of Iran, compared to what they could be doing, and I want to keep it that way, and will, unless stupid people get in the way."

The sanctions waiver came a week after Trump's phone call with Russian President Vladimir Putin. On 9 October, Trump announced that Moscow would supply 300,000 tonnes of diesel immediately, followed by an additional 500,000 tonnes in November and one million tonnes "immediately thereafter."

Amid renewed tensions with Trump, who has accused him of prolonging the war, Zelenskyy met with top US envoys, including Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, on Sunday (local time) to ease tensions.

Announcing the meeting on X, Zelenskyy wrote, "I held a conversation with representatives of the President of America, Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner. Peace comes only through strength and cooperation built on trust. We are doing everything on our side to achieve this and to ensure that America's position is strong and helps bring about the peace that everyone except Russia has been awaiting for over four and a half years."
