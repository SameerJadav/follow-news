Kenya convicts cult leader over the murder of 190 children

The leader of a Kenyan cult who instructed followers to starve themselves and their children to death in order to reach heaven has been found guilty of murder. Paul Mckenzie and 28 of his cult leaders are convicted of killing 190 children and one adult.
