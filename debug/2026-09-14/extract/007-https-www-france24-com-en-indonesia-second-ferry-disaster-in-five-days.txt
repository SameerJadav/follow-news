# Indonesia: Second ferry disaster in five days

 Asia / Pacific 

Issued on: 13/09/2026 - 15:17

Share

At least one person has died and more than 100 people remain missing after a ferry carrying 243 people sank in rough weather between the Indonesian islands of Java and Borneo. The disaster is the second major ferry accident in the region in five days, renewing concerns over safety standards, overcrowding and maintenance.
