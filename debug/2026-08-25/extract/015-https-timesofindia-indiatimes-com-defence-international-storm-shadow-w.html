What is Storm Shadow?

Storm Shadow/Scalp is an air-launched, long-range, conventionally armed deep-strike missile designed for pre-planned attacks against high-value fixed or stationary targets, including hardened bunkers and key infrastructure.The missile weighs about 1,300kg and is 5.10 metres long. It is designed for long-range deep-strike missions and can be operated day and night in all weather conditions.Its navigation system combines inertial navigation, GPS and terrain referencing. After launch, the missile descends to a terrain-hugging altitude intended to reduce the chances of detection.As it approaches its target, an onboard infrared seeker uses the stored target image to guide the final phase of the strike.The system is designed for high survivability through long stand-off range and low observability, while its mission-planning and navigation systems are intended to help it avoid enemy ground-based air defences.The missile's terminal guidance uses an imaging infrared seeker and automatic target recognition for precision. Its warhead incorporates a precursor charge and a penetrator, with detonation options including airburst, impact and penetration modes.Storm Shadow/Scalp can be integrated with aircraft including the Eurofighter Typhoon, Rafale, Mirage 2000 and Tornado. It is in service with the Royal Air Force, French Air Force, Italian Air Force and several export customers, and has seen operational use in Iraq, Libya and Syria.

The immediate purpose of the British decision is to allow the release of information on British-made components incorporated into Scalp missiles. The initiative would form part of wider efforts to increase Ukraine's domestic defence production and reduce its reliance on supplies manufactured outside the country.
