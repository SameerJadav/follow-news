It is designed for long-range deep-strike missions and can be operated day and night in all weather conditions.Its navigation system combines inertial navigation, GPS and terrain referencing. After launch, the missile descends to a terrain-hugging altitude intended to reduce the chances of detection.As it approaches its target, an onboard infrared seeker uses the stored target image to guide the final phase of the strike.The system is designed for high survivability through long stand-off range and low observability, while its mission-planning and navigation systems are intended to help it avoid enemy ground-based air defences.The missile's terminal guidance uses an imaging infrared seeker and automatic target recognition for precision. Its warhead incorporates a precursor charge and a penetrator, with detonation options including airburst, impact and penetration modes.Storm Shadow/Scalp can be integrated with aircraft including the Eurofighter Typhoon, Rafale, Mirage 2000 and Tornado.
