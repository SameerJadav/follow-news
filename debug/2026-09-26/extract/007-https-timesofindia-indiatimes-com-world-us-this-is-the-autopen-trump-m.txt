US President Donald Trump took a swipe at former President Joe Biden during Chinese President Xi Jinping's visit to the White House, joking about Biden's use of an autopen while showing Xi a display of presidential portraits.Trump and Xi were walking through the White House colonnade on Thursday when Trump stopped at the Presidential Walk of Fame, a row of portraits of former US presidents.Pointing to portraits representing his two terms in office, Trump told Xi, "Trump, and then Trump," before gesturing towards the space representing Biden."And, this is the autopen," Trump said, prompting Xi to laugh."It's Biden," Trump added, tapping Xi on the arm.Biden represented by autopenBiden is the only former president whose portrait is not displayed alongside the other commanders-in-chief in the row. An autopen instead represents him, a reference to Trump's repeated criticism of Biden's use of the machine to sign official documents.Biden has rejected Trump's claims surrounding his use of the autopen.The exchange was captured on video and later posted by the White House on social media with a laughing emoji. The video quickly drew reactions from Trump's supporters and critics.Former Democratic Representative John Delaney condemned the episode."Let's be very clear on this one point," Delaney wrote on X.
