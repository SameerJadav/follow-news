Iran to reopen Strait of Hormuz within a week if US agrees to plan

Iran will reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days, but the choice now rests with the US, says Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araqchi who spoke from the United Nations. The plan is based on the previously-agreed-upon Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) brokered in June.
