# Macron says ‘military resources’ to be sent to Saudi Arabia

 Middle East 

Issued on: 25/09/2026 - 16:04

Share

Speaking to broadcasters TF1 and France 2, French President Emmanuel Macron on Thursday announced that “military resources” will be sent to protect the Yanbu oil site in Saudi Arabia, integral to maintaining the kingdom’s fuel exports and increasingly threatened by Yemen’s Iran-backed Houthi rebels.
