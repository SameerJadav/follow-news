# Embattled new White House Press Secretary Zacharia 'let go' from her role

 Americas 

A White House Press official said Friday that Secretary ​Katie Zacharia was "let go" from her role after US President Donald Trump signaled she was on her way out, a week after he picked her for the post.Zacharia's credentials as an attorney had come under scrutiny and she admitted to not having passed the bar in an interview with Fox News on Thursday.

Issued on: 16/10/2026 - 17:57 Modified: 16/10/2026 - 20:38

[White House](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/white-house/) Press Secretary ​Katie Zacharia was "let go" from the role, a White House official said on Friday, after US President [Donald Trump](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/donald-trump/) said he had ​told ‌her to reconsider the job following public ⁠scrutiny.

"I told her: 'I think you're better off being happy, and I'm not sure ‌you can ever be happy in this stuff.' ⁠There's nothing easy about this stuff," Trump told reporters while departing the White House.

He added: "I said to ​her honestly, 'If I were you, I'd reconsider ...think ‌about it. But I would reconsider.'" [Fox News](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/fox-news/), citing a White House official, later reported that

Zacharia was "transitioning" out of the ‌role of press secretary. Zacharia started the job on Monday and held ​her debut White House briefing on Tuesday.

She had said she received a call about the role of press secretary last Wednesday ​and got offered the job to [succeed Karoline Leavitt](https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20260812-white-house-press-secretary-and-trump-adviser-karoline-leavitt-to-leave-role) ​last Friday.

Read more[Trump's White House spokesperson Karoline Leavitt to leave role](https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20260812-white-house-press-secretary-and-trump-adviser-karoline-leavitt-to-leave-role)

Trump later announced her appointment. ​In his announcement, he described her as an attorney.

Over the last week, the issue ​of whether Zacharia, a graduate of [California](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/california/)'s Pepperdine University law school, had actually passed the bar – making her an attorney – came under fire.

During a Fox News interview on ⁠Thursday, Zacharia said she did not pass the bar.
