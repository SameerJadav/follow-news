Opposition leader Rahul Gandhi on Friday asked the Union government to explain why United States Secretary of State Marco Rubio had “summoned his employee” to Mumbai.

Rubio will be visiting Mumbai and Jamnagar between October 19 and October 22.

US Ambassador to India Sergio Gor said on Thursday that Washington was “expecting” Indian External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar and other officials to travel to Mumbai to meet Rubio.

Gor also said that Rubio will meet “some of the folks at Reliance” at Jamnagar.

“Reliance is one of the biggest oil refinery companies in the world...” Gor said at the Bloomberg New Economy Forum on Thursday. “They are opening the first refinery in the United States in 50 years...”

As of Friday afternoon, New Delhi had not announced the details of Rubio’s visit.

On Wednesday, the Ministry of External Affairs said that it would “make the announcement at the appropriate time as to what will be the issues to be taken up”.

During a press briefing on Friday, Ministry of External Affairs spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said that Jaishankar will hold talks with Rubio on October 20 “and attend the celebration of the 250th anniversary of the independence of the United States as the guest of honour”.

Jaiswal said that it had been a practice “in the last decade or so that we have hosted foreign secretaries outside Delhi”.

He noted that Prime Minister Narendra Modi had hosted his counterpart from Spain in Gujarat’s Vadodara. “The prime minister of Mauritius was hosted by him in Varanasi, and then the external affairs minister hosted the foreign ministers’ meeting in Goa,” added Jaiswal.

On Thursday, the Congress said that it was “not surprising” that the US ambassador had given more details about Rubio’s visit than New Delhi had.

Gandhi added: “Modi ji, your officials in the external affairs ministry are confused about what’s going on…You might want to explain. Unless, of course, you want me to.”

Jamnagar is home to the world’s largest single-location refining complex, operated by Reliance Industries. The facility is known to have refined Russian crude.

New Delhi’s purchase of Russian oil has been a source of friction with Washington. In August 2025, the US imposed an additional 25% tariff on Indian imports to discourage New Delhi from buying Russian crude. The tariff was removed in February after the two countries reached an interim trade deal. Rubio’s visit comes as India and the US seek to conclude negotiations on the agreement.

The US secretary of state is expected to meet senior officials of the Indian government to strengthen relations, “exchange views on regional and global dynamics, and discuss prospects for enhanced energy cooperation”, Washington said on Tuesday.

Rubio will also meet business leaders to “promote opportunities for commercial investment and economic cooperation”, the US state department added. It did not specify who Rubio will meet.

On Thursday, Congress leader Jairam Ramesh said that it was “quite extraordinary” for Gor to say “we expect the minister Dr Jaishankar to be joining us there” along with others.

“Since when has the announcement of India’s Minister of External Affairs meetings within India been outsourced to somebody other than official spokesperson of the MEA?” he asked. “India gets to know from the US ambassador just as it first got to know from Mr Rubio himself on the evening of May 10, 2025, that Operation Sindoor had been halted.”

Ramesh added: “Why is the MEA silent on the external affairs minister’s tryst with Mr Rubio in Mumbai?”

The ceasefire that ended the four-day conflict between India and Pakistan in May 2025 had been announced by Washington minutes before New Delhi or Islamabad.

The relations between India and the US have been frosty since Donald Trump became the president for the second term in January 2025.

The Trump administration imposed tariffs on import of India goods and a punitive levy for New Delhi’s purchase of Russia oil. Washington has repeatedly alleged that India’s purchases were helping fund Russia’s war on Ukraine.
