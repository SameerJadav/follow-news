Ukrainian President [Volodymyr Zelensky](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/volodymyr-zelensky/) urged EU leaders Friday to keep financing [Kyiv](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/kyiv/)'s fight [with nearly $80 billion more next year](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20261016-live-trump-calls-renewed-ukraine-attacks-on-russian-oil-refineries-a-disgrace), as he insisted he was standing up for his country amid a fresh row with US President [Donald Trump](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/donald-trump/).

"I defend the interests of [Ukraine](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/ukraine/) and the Ukrainian people exclusively," Zelensky told journalists after talks with EU leaders in [Brussels](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/brussels/).

Asked if he was being made a scapegoat for Trump's domestic woes ahead of the US midterms, Zelensky just smiled.

"This is my answer," he said, after an extended pregnant pause.

The latest breakdown between Trump and Zelensky has fuelled fear that the US leader is siding with [Moscow](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/moscow/).

Those concerns have been heightened by [disruptions to a critical US-provided satellite system](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20261014-ukraine-says-starlink-restricted-operations-overnight-disrupting-military-missions) that has [hampered Ukraine's ability to counter](https://www.france24.com/en/video/20261015-ukraine-struggles-with-starlink-and-us-satellite-data-as-missiles-hit-kyiv)[Russia's intense bombardments](https://www.france24.com/en/germany-s-merz-visits-kyiv-as-russia-steps-up-attacks-on-ukrainian-cities).

It also uses SpaceX's parallel Starlink civilian satellite system, but not for offensive operations.

[Tech](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/tech-1/) billionaire Musk – though a virulent Zelensky critic – said his company was not behind the disruption and a US official denied any intervention by Trump.
