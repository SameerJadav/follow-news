# Greenland, Denmark expect to sign deal with US on sidelines of UN General Assembly

 Europe 

Denmark PM says deal with US on Greenland will be signed by three governments next week during UN General Assembly.The Greenland security deal reached by the United States and Denmark guarantees that China and Russia cannot set up bases there, the State Department said Friday.

Issued on: 19/09/2026 - 00:18

 1 min Reading time

Share

 By: 

[FRANCE 24](https://www.france24.com/en/author/france-24/ "FRANCE 24")

![Image 2: The small town Ilulissat, Greenland,  Aug 16, 2007.](https://s.france24.com/media/display/79892e7c-949d-11f1-acc6-005056bf30b7/w:1024/p:16x9/AP070816032596.jpg)

 The small town Ilulissat, Greenland, Aug 16, 2007 © Michael Kappeler, AP 

Greenland, Denmark and the United States expect to sign a deal next week to strengthen security in the Arctic and the North Atlantic region, Danish Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen said Friday.

"I am pleased that there is a prospect of a good agreement for Greenland, the Kingdom of Denmark and the United States," she said in a statement, adding that the agreement would be signed on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly.
