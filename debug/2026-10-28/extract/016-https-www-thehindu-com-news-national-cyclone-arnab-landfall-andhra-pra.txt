The landfall process of “very severe” cyclonic storm Arnab was continuing along the Andhra Pradesh coast on Wednesday (October 28, 2026), the India Meteorological Department (IMD) said. The northern spiral band of the cyclone has further entered the land, and the landfall process is expected to continue for the next three hours, it said.

The landfall process began near Narsapur town in West Godavari district of Andhra Pradesh, at around 1.30 a.m. on Wednesday. Experts from the India Meteorological Department (IMD), however, said it is too early to indicate the exact location of the landfall.
