[Israel](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/israel/)'s wartime election appeared deadlocked on Tuesday, ​exit polls showed, a potential setback for veteran Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/benjamin-netanyahu/) if the projections hold, with neither side having a clear path to power but rival Gadi Eisenkot's larger bloc potentially giving him the first chance to form a [government](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/government/).

The stalemate could yet work in Netanyahu's favour, allowing him to remain in power in a caretaker capacity while parties try to forge a governing [coalition](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/coalition/).

Turnout was 77.7%,Israel's Central Elections Committee said, the highest in more ​than two decades and ‌around 7 percentage points higher than in the previous election in 2022.

Israel's three major networks updated their exit polls, giving Eisenkot's bloc a two-seat edge over Netanyahu's Likud-led camp, at ⁠52-50.

The remaining 18 seats were allocated to unaligned Arab parties in the exit polls. But Eisenkot, leader of the Yashar party, has all but ruled out forming a government with them, complicating his path to a majority despite his bloc's lead.

Some Israeli [media](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/media/) outlets, including pro-Netanyahu Channel 14, projected a far stronger showing ‌for Netanyahu and his parties, saying they would collectively win 60 seats.

Final results could take days, and Israel's fragmented political system leaves smaller parties potential kingmakers. No single ⁠party has ever won 61 seats outright in the 120-seat [Knesset](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/knesset/), making coalitions the norm.

It was the first election since the Hamas-led attacks of October 7, 2023, and the ensuing wars in Gaza, [Lebanon](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/lebanon/) and [Iran](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/iran/).

Yohanan Plesner, president of the Israel Democracy Institute, called the exit polls a major defeat for Netanyahu's coalition, which they showed falling from 68 seats in the outgoing Knesset ​to about 50. But he said Eisenkot had not emerged as a clear winner.

Plesner cautioned that much could change as votes were counted. If the final result mirrored ‌the exit polls, he said, Eisenkot could try to form a minority government that would remove Netanyahu from power, even if it lasted only until another election.

## ANTI-NETANYAHU BOOST

While there was no clear outcome, the exit polls reflected the strength of the anti-Netanyahu opposition.

Opposition leader [Yair Lapid](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/yair-lapid-1/) said in a statement: “Don’t let anyone confuse you: the Netanyahu bloc has suffered a crushing defeat. This is a total collapse and the public has told them to go home.”

Eisenkot, 66, a hawkish former [military](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/military/) ‌chief who lost a son and two nephews in the Gaza war, emerged as Netanyahu's strongest challenger, stressing his security credentials while positioning himself as a centrist domestically.

He entered politics only four years ago and joined Netanyahu's war cabinet after the 2023 [Hamas](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/hamas/)[attack](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/attacks/), but quit eight months later, criticising the prime minister's leadership.

Eisenkot ​offers a break with Netanyahu's leadership and far-right allies, but not with the hardline approach to security that has become more entrenched since the 2023 Hamas attack: He opposes Palestinian statehood and says Hamas' military wing must be destroyed or dismantled.

Eisenkot says Israel's response — a war that has killed more than 74,000 Palestinians, according to Gaza health authorities —was a failure because Hamas remains in charge.

"Dear Democrats, a decision was made," ​Yair Golan, leader of the Democrats party and a Netanyahu rival, said at a party rally after polls closed. "The nation has given us a sacred duty: to translate the trust they have placed in us into ​action, to fortify [democracy](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/democracy/), to save Israel and to provide true defense for the state."
