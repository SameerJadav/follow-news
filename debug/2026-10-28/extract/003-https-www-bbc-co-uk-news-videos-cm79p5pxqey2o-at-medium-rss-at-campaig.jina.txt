# Watch: What happens now after close vote in Israeli election?

Polls have closed in the Israeli election and votes are being counted.

Exit polls suggest Gadi Eisenkot's bloc have won the largest number of seats, but has not wont a majority.

The polls also suggest Netanyahu's Likud party is set to be the biggest single party in the parliament.

The BBC's Hugo Bachega looks at what might happen next.
