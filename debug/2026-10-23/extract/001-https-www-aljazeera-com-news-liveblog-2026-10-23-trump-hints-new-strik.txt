(00:15 GMT) ## Tehran criticises ‘Iran conflict’ framing of US-Israel war

Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei says naming the US-Israel war the “Iran conflict” obscures responsibility as it “erases the aggressors from the frame and places the targeted country in the title”.

In a post on X, Baghaei says war names can “shape narratives that serve the aggressors”, citing an American University analysis on how conflicts are framed.

(00:07 GMT) ## WATCH: Iran threatens US bases as Trump claims Hormuz oil shipments exceed pre-war levels

Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) has threatened to attack US military bases across the Middle East if US warships continue escorting vessels through the Strait of Hormuz.

The IRGC says it attacked four ships in the northern Gulf over alleged illegal oil transfers, while three others reportedly struck mines in the strait. Neither claim has been independently verified.

The UK Maritime Trade Operations agency says at least 14 vessels were attacked between October 8 and 17, with shipping traffic remaining 70 percent below pre-war levels.
