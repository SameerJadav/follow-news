An earthquake of magnitude 7 struck Tibet region of China at around 10.33 pm local time on Thursday, Reuters reported citing the China Earthquake Networks Center (CENC).

The epicenter was in northern Tibet's Nagqu at a depth of 10 km. The area has an average elevation of around 4,864 metres. The epicentre was about 400 km from the Tibetan capital, Lhasa.

State-run Xinhua News Agency said the Ministry of Emergency Management activated a Level IV national emergency response and sent a working group to the affected region. Xinhua also reported that there were casualties due to the earthquake but didn't give any number.
