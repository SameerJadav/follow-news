# Dozens killed in two mass shootings in South Africa

At least 27 people have been killed in two mass shootings across South Africa, after gunmen attacked a tavern near Johannesburg and a shop outside Cape Town. Police are probing a possible business rivalry, days after 11 people died in a separate shooting near Durban.
