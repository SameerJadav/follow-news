# Deadly strike hits market in Yemen’s Taiz

A strike on a market in the Yemeni city of Taiz has killed at least seven people and wounded 40, according to Houthi authorities. Government sources claim Houthi fighters had set up a military camp nearby, but conflicting accounts and limited ground access make the strike and its intended target difficult to verify independently.
