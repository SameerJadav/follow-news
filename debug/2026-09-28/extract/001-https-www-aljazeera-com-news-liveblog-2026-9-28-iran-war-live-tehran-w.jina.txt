## A recap of recent developments

 

    *   Iran’s foreign minister, Abbas Araghchi, says his country is “fully prepared” if the war with the US resumes, after Washington [rejected](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/27/better-deal-whats-behind-trumps-rejection-of-irans-truce-offer) its seven-day roadmap to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.
    *   US President Donald Trump has said he expects more talks with Iran this week and claims Washington is going to win the war very soon.
    *   The US military rejects Iran’s claim that its military has seized an advanced underwater US drone in the [Strait of Hormuz](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/9/27/strait-of-hormuz-tensions-linger-as-iran-and-us-move-further-from-a-deal).
    *   Five men have been arrested near a United Kingdom airbase used by the US to attack Iran on suspicion of explosive and “terrorism” offences.
    *   Israel has revoked the diplomatic status of Dutch diplomats in the [occupied West Bank](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/27/israeli-minister-bezalel-smotrich-calls-for-war-in-occupied-west-bank) city of Ramallah after the Netherlands imposed a ban on imports from illegal Israeli settlements.
    *   Israeli attacks across Gaza have killed at least four Palestinians and wounded 12 on Sunday, medical sources said.
