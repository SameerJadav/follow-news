# Five arrested near UK RAF base over suspected explosives ‘terror’ plot

Five men have been arrested near RAF Fairford in the UK on suspicion of explosives and terrorism offences, forcing the evacuation of nearby homes. US and UK leaders say the men were under investigation and planning to cause ‘big damage’.
