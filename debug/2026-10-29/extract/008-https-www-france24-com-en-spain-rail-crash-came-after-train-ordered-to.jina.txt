# Spain rail crash came after train ordered to go in error: minister

Issued on: 28/10/2026 - 23:30 Modified: 28/10/2026 - 23:31

Share

A rail collision in Spain that claimed at least 68 lives on Wednesday came after a train was ordered to go in "error" despite a technical failure that halted all services, the transport minister said. The first findings about the accident in the central region of Castilla-La Mancha are likely to revive questions about rail safety in Spain after another high-speed collision in January killed 46 people. FRANCE 24's James Vasina reports.
