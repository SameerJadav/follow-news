# Israelis rally for Jewish-Arab government after election gains

Israelis rallied in Tel Aviv for a Jewish-Arab gov’t after parties representing Israel’s Arab citizens made major gains in Tuesday’s election. The two main Arab parties are projected to win around 19 of the Knesset’s 120 seats, potentially giving them a say in who forms gov’t.
