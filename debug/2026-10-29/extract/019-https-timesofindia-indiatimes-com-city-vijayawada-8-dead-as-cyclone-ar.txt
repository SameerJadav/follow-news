VISAKHAPATNAM/KAKINADA/AMALAPURAM: Eight people have died in parts of Andhra Pradesh after severe cyclonic storm Arnab crossed the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts early on Wednesday.The storm triggered heavy rainfall, uprooted hundreds of trees and electric poles, damaged houses and roads, disrupted power supply and communication, and damaged standing crops across coastal districts. Naidu spoke to Union Home Minister Amit Shah over the phone and briefed him on the cyclone and its impact on the state.Two people were killed in Bhimavaram in West Godavari district. In the first incident, Ch Kishore (45) died after a tree fell on him, while Rambabu died after an electric pole collapsed on him.In Konaseema district, K Venkayamma died after a thatched house collapsed on her in Sakenetipalle mandal. Similarly, another woman died in Prakashpuram in Sakenetipalle mandal.In Visakhapatnam city, two women were killed after electric poles collapsed on them in the Goshala and Balajinagar localities. One of the victims was identified as K Madhavi.Further, a 55-year-old woman died after accidentally stepping on an exposed live wire outside her home in Yellamanchili town in Anakapalli district on Wednesday morning.It may be recalled that one person, A Pinni Devudu (65), died of electrocution in the Mangalapalem locality of Vizag city after coming into contact with a live wire that had snapped.Cyclone Arnab crossed the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts to the southwest of Yanam, about 20 km east-northeast of Narsapur, 40 km southwest of Yanam and 60 km southwest of Kakinada, between 2 am and 8 am on Wednesday.It crossed the coast as a very severe cyclonic storm, with maximum sustained wind speeds of 125-135 kmph and gusts reaching 150 kmph. The system weakened from a very severe cyclonic storm to a severe cyclonic storm after crossing the coast.The system is likely to move north-northwestwards over the next 48 hours and weaken gradually. It is expected to weaken into a cyclonic storm during the next six hours and further into a deep depression over the subsequent six hours.The IMD has forecast isolated heavy rainfall in Srikakulam, Vizianagaram and Parvathipuram Manyam districts on Thursday (October 29).Konaseema is among the worst-affected districts in Andhra Pradesh, with winds reaching up to 130 kmph. However, no major damage has been reported, and rainfall subsided after 2 am on Wednesday.
