# Riot police deployed in Madrid as protests erupt over deadly train crash

A deadly train crash fuelled anger in Spain as far-right demonstrators clashed with police during an anti-government protest in Madrid. Hundreds demanded the transport minister’s resignation amid questions over the authorities’ handling of the disaster, which killed 68 people.
