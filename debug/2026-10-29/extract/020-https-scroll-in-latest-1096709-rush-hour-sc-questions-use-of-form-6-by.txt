The Supreme Court verbally asked the Election Commission why it was telling those enrolled in voter lists before the special intensive revision to use Form 6 after they were deleted from the draft rolls for not filling the enumeration forms during the exercise. Form 6, which is used to register first-time voters, requires applicants to declare that they have not been electors before, the court observed.

The bench headed by Chief Justice Surya Kant said that there would be several voters in Delhi who could not have been served enumeration forms as they may have changed their residential addresses or been travelling at the time. The court asked why voters cannot now be allowed to fill the enumeration form or Form 8, which is for changing addresses, instead of Form 6.

The counsel representing the Election Commission said that the authorities will examine the suggestions made by the court. Read on.

Congress leader Rahul Gandhi accused the Union government of “criminally destroying” the Tata Group, the largest Indian conglomerate. While Gandhi did not specify what prompted his remarks, they came amid a dispute within the leadership of Tata Sons, the holding company of the conglomerate’s businesses.

The dispute revolves around the reappointment of N Chandrasekaran as the executive chairman of Tata Sons and the proposed listing of the holding company on the stock market.

Gandhi said that Indian businesses were not Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Union Home Minister Amit Shah’s “plaything”. Read on.

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu-led alliance was ahead of the Opposition bloc in the country’s general elections as the votes were being counted. As of 4 pm Indian time, the Netanyahu-led coalition was leading in 54 of the 120 seats in Parliament. A party or an alliance needs 61 seats to form the government.

The Opposition bloc was ahead in 49 seats. The Arab parties, which are supported largely by Israel’s Palestinian minority, were leading in 17 seats. More than 77% of the votes had been counted.

This was the first parliamentary election since the beginning of the conflict in Gaza three years ago and the Israel-United States’ war on Iran that started in February. Read on.
