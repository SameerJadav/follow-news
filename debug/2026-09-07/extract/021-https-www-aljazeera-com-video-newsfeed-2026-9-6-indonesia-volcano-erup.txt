# Indonesia volcano eruption disrupts hundreds of flights

Two new eruptions were recorded as Indonesia’s Anak Krakatau continued to spew volcanic ash, disrupting hundreds of flights at six airports. Travellers describe sudden cancellations, while residents deal with ash blanketing roads and public spaces.
