Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar: Maratha quota activist Manoj Jarange announced a five-day march to Mumbai starting Sept 15 on the 15th day of his indefinite hunger strike on Saturday despite appeals from several ministers to defer the programme during Ganeshotsav. According to the schedule, the march will halt at Patoda (Beed) on Sept 16, Shrigonda in Ahilyanagar on Sept 17, Hadapsar in Pune on Sept 18 and Khopoli in Raigad on Sept 19 before reaching Azad Maidan (Mumbai) on Sept 20. Although Koparkhairane is being considered as an alternative halt, Jarange told his supporters to treat Khopoli as the planned fourth stop.The route from Antarwali Sarati will pass through Shahagad, Gevrai, Beed, Manjarsumba, Limba Ganesh and Patoda before heading towards Mumbai. Jarange said the marchers would cover about 100km a day before reaching each halt.The march is expected to coincide with the Ganesh festival and the run-up to immersion processions, posing a major logistical and security challenge for police and civic authorities.
