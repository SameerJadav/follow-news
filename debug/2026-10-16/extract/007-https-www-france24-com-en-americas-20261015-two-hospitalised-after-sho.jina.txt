# Teen shooter kills three children and teacher at Oregon elementary school

 Americas 

A 16-year-old gunman killed three children aged 5, 8 and 11, and a teacher at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, on Thursday before being killed by a community member who pursued him inside, police said. Two other people were wounded in the shooting.

![Image 1: Police tape is placed outside Silver Rail Elementary school, where a shooting took place on in Bend, Oregon.](https://s.france24.com/media/display/0bdabf8a-c8eb-11f1-84b0-2d1c143cdef3/w:1024/p:16x9/AP26288796036302.jpg)

 Police tape is placed outside Silver Rail Elementary school, where a shooting took place on Oct. 15, 2026 in Bend, Oregon. © Joe Kline, AP 

A 16-year-old [opened fire](https://www.france24.com/en/france/20261015-france-charges-teen-shooter-citing-revenge-for-bullying-in-school)at an [elementary school](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/education/) in Bend, Oregon, on Thursday, killing three [children](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/children/) and a teacher before he was shot by a person at the scene who pursued him into the school, authorities said.

Authorities found the suspect dead inside the school minutes after the shooting was first reported, Bend Police Chief Mike Krantz said.

The three children killed were aged 5, 8 and 11. Another child, 7, and a teacher, 54, were injured and taken to a hospital in critical condition, Krantz said.

The attacker, identified as a Bend resident, forced his way into the school by shooting at locked doors, police said. He was shot and went down inside the school shortly after officers arrived, police spokesperson Sheila Miller said.

The first 911 call came just before 1 p.m. from a person in a vehicle outside Silver Rail Elementary who reported being shot at by someone with a long gun, Krantz said.

Further 911 calls came from inside the school, and officers arrived on scene within minutes, Miller said.

[Police](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/police/) said they did not know if the attacker knew anyone at the school or whether he had attended.

The attacker used a pump-action [shotgun](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/guns/) that was purchased by his mother, according to a law enforcement official briefed on the shooting. The official spoke to The Associated Press on the condition of anonymity because they were not authorised to publicly discuss the investigation.

The school in Bend, Oregon, which is more than 100 miles (160 kilometres) southeast of Portland, had 459 enrolled students as of last year, according to the Oregon Department of Education. The school serves students in kindergarten through fifth grade.

At a news conference Thursday night, Oregon Gov. Tina Kotek said her heart was “absolutely broken” and thanked the civilian who pursued the shooter.

“To the community member who heroically intervened to stop further loss of life: Your actions carry a heavy emotional burden, and I just want you to know that my thoughts are with you as well,” she said.

Reunification of children with their parents was ongoing early Thursday evening.

_(FRANCE 24 with AP)_
