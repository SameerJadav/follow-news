The 43-year-old actor, an Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) cardholder, is likely to file a petition in HC by Friday (October 16, 2026) or Monday (October 19) through his advocate Zulfiquar Memon. In the draft petition, the actor has termed the "leave India" directive "drastic" and argued that no provision under the current law or statutory rules prohibits an OCI cardholder from participating in a peaceful protest.
