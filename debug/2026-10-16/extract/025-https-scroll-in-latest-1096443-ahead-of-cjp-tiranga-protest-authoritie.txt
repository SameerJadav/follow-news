The Delhi Police was imposing widespread restrictions in Connaught Place and central areas of the national capital ahead of a protest by the Cockroach Janta Party planned for Friday.

The political campaign has announced that it will hold a “Lehra Do Tiranga” demonstration at Connaught Place at 5 pm on Friday, to wave the national flag.

The demonstration comes as a response to the arrest of Cockroach Janta Party leaders Ashutosh Ranka, Saurav Das and Ajinkya Shinde on Wednesday, a day after they trespassed at Jantar Mantar and waved the national flag.

While Ranka and Das remain in judicial custody, Shinde was released on a personal bond.

They were detained on Tuesday with other members of the Cockroach Janta Party after they “sneaked into” Jantar Mantar to protest the authorities’ decision to denotify the area as a designated protest site. The leaders have also been accused of violating a bond and obstructing a public servant from discharging their duties.

The arrests were widely condemned by the Opposition, with several leaders including Congress’ Rahul Gandhi saying that “waving the tricolour” at Jantar Mantar during a peaceful protest “is not a crime” and jailing young persons for it is an “act of cowardice”.

Besides the Cockroach Janta Party, the Aam Aadmi Party has urged its workers and supporters to join the protest.

The Delhi Police was deploying more than 6,000 personnel in and around Connaught Place, the Hindustan Times reported.

This is in addition to the 26,000 personnel from the central armed police forces already deployed in central Delhi in the past week amid protests by the Cockroach Janta Party, the Opposition parties, Left groups and the civil society demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar.

Protests against Kumar have been held in Delhi and other parts of the country since October 2 over concerns about the functioning of the Election Commission and its handling of the special intensive revision of electoral rolls.

The deployment of central police forces is among the largest in the national capital since the 2020 riots in northeastern Delhi, the Hindustan Times quoted an unidentified police officer as saying.

Barricades had been installed along roads near Connaught Place, Patel Chowk, ITO and Sansad Marg on Thursday, the newspaper reported.

Additional surveillance cameras were being installed at Central Park and the Rajiv Chowk metro station in Connaught Place on Thursday, The Indian Express reported.

The police reiterated on Thursday that an order prohibiting gatherings of four or more persons was in effect in central Delhi.

The police said that protesters should not join gatherings in the area and legal action would be taken against those violating the law.

The municipal council said that the Central Park at Connaught Place will be closed on Friday and Saturday “for administrative reasons”.

The civic body has asked the police for “proper safety and security” of the park and “to prevent any unauthorised access of any one, keeping in view the importance and sensitivity of this iconic public park”, the police said.

The police added that it “can facilitate a group of selected persons for carrying the national flag and signing of the national anthem at a suitable place”.

The Delhi Traffic Police said that the movement of vehicles on certain roads in and around Connaught Place is likely to be affected starting from 3 pm on Friday.

On Thursday, the Delhi High Court refused to stop the protest. It noted the police’s submission that all possible steps would be taken to regulate and control the gathering.

At a press conference at midnight on Friday, Cockroach Janta Party founder Dipke claimed that the Delhi Police were following him and could detain him. “If one has to go to jail for waving the tricolour in this country, then we are ready for it...” PTI quoted him as saying.
