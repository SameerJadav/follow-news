It will eventually achieve a nominal perigee of 170km, apogee of 28,934km and inclination of 19.28 degrees. The satellite will be moved to the final orbit through a series of manoeuvres using onboard thrusters.Isro chairman V Narayanan said the rocket injected the satellite in the intended orbit precisely. This is the 107th launch from Sriharikota and 19th GSLV flight.
