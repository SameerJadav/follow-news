Nepal flood victims to be identified through DNA

At Nepal’s border with China, flash floods devastated the town of Timure, leaving bodies scattered across the country’s worst-hit area. With many victims dismembered, decomposed and stripped of clothing, forensic teams are relying on DNA testing to identify the dead.
