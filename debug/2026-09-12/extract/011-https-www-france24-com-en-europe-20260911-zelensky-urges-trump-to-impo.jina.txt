# Zelensky urges Trump to impose new Russia sanctions 'now', after deadly Ukraine strikes

 Europe 

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky on Friday urged US President Donald Trump to impose new sanctions on Russia over deadly strikes on Ukraine, as Kyiv and Moscow step up attacks and US-led efforts to end the war remain deadlocked.

Issued on: 11/09/2026 - 21:13

 1 min Reading time

Share

 By: 

[FRANCE 24](https://www.france24.com/en/author/france-24/ "FRANCE 24")

![Image 1: Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky attends the funeral of Norway's King Harald on September 9, 2026.](https://s.france24.com/media/display/8c23efbe-ae0f-11f1-929d-005056a90284/w:1024/p:16x9/2026-09-09T104246Z-849861914-RC2MFNAOGGWF-RTRMADP-3-NORWAY-ROYALS-KING-HARALD-FUNERAL.jpg)

 Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky attends the funeral of Norway's King Harald on September 9, 2026. © Leonhard Foeger, Reuters 

[US](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/usa/) President [Donald Trump](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/donald-trump/)'s administration should immediately impose new [sanctions](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/sanctions/) on [Russia](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/russia/)[over recent deadly strikes on Ukraine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X4wuYa-QjpY), Ukrainian President [Volodymyr Zelensky](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/volodymyr-zelensky/) said Friday.

The comments came after Zelensky announced he planned to meet Trump in late September, amid US-led efforts to broker an end to the four-and-a-half year Ukraine war.

"Sanctions need to be imposed now," Zelensky told the Yalta European Strategy forum.

"Honestly, I sometimes do not understand why decisions cannot be made quickly," he added.

Read more[Trump tells Putin he wants swift end to Ukraine war, Kremlin says](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260908-trump-tells-putin-he-wants-swift-end-to-ukraine-war-kremlin-says)

Zelensky separately called for a truce with Russia covering strikes on energy targets, saying Ukraine was ready to hit back if [Moscow](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/moscow/) attacked his country's energy grid over the winter.

"Russia knows that if they strike... they will receive the same in return," he said.

Both Moscow and [Kyiv](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/kyiv/) have escalated long-range strikes on each other in recent months, as [US-led talks on ending Europe's deadliest conflict](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260908-trump-tells-putin-he-wants-swift-end-to-ukraine-war-kremlin-says) since [World War II](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/world-war-ii/) remain deadlocked.

Read more[After meeting with US envoys in Kyiv, Zelensky says war likely to ‘continue in winter’](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260906-live-trump-s-envoys-head-to-ukraine-after-substantive-talks-with-putin)

An early morning Russian drone strike on a petrol station in Kyiv killed two people and hospitalised four, the city's mayor said.

_(FRANCE 24 with AFP)_
