# Russian officials say 'situation under control' after plague reports

 Health 

Issued on: 05/10/2026 - 21:19 Modified: 05/10/2026 - 21:19

Share

Moscow on Monday downplayed reports that a laboratory technician had died of plague, stressing that post-mortem tests had not detected the disease, while Washington said it was closely monitoring the situation. Reports on Friday that plague had killed a 28-year-old woman named Darya Shipilova, who had been working for the anti-plague institute in Siberia's Irkutsk region, sparked alarm. FRANCE 24's August Hakansson reports.
