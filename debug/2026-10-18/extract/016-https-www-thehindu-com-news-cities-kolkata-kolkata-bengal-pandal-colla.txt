At least 19 people died, and 25 others were injured in a stampede at the Chorbagan Durga Puja pandal in north Kolkata on Saturday (October 18, 2026).

West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari, who rushed to the Kolkata Medical College and Hospital, where the victims were taken, also announced that his government will provide ₹25 lakh and a Group D govt job for the family of each deceased. He said police detained Chorbagan Durga Puja organisers, private security guards, and on-duty staff for questioning. “As of now, 19 people have died, and 25 are undergoing treatment,” Mr. Adhikari said.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi described the incident as “extremely distressing” and announced compensation of ₹2 lakh for the dead and ₹50,000 for the injured. He said the local administration is providing all possible assistance to those affected.

The majority of those feared dead and injured are women and children. The injured have been shifted to Medical College, Kolkata. Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari and State Health Minister Sharadwat Mukherjee have reached the hospital.

After the stampede, firefighters have reached the accident spot for rescue operations. Senior police officials and a disaster management team have reached the spot. Additional Commissioner of Police ( Crime) Kunal Agarwal visited the spot and is likely to visit the injured at the hospital within some time.

The Prime Minister has announced that an ex gratia of ₹2 lakh from PMNRF would be given to the next of kin of each of those who lost their lives. The injured would be given ₹50,000.

“Why did you come to see the Durga Puja? Why were there no police? How did so many people die?” asked a family member of the deceased, all of whom are from Bardhaman.

“I lost both my daughters. There is no one left to call me mother,” said a grieving mother, whose daughters were in their twenties and one of whom leaves behind a child.

Kolkata Police are interrogating 12 detained people, including organisers, volunteers and others, in connection with the case.

West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendhu Adhikari said, “I appeal to all the Durga Pujas in the State to maintain vigilance and precaution for the remaining part of the festival. Durga Pujas will be held, but for the next few days we will stand with the families of the deceased and injured.”

West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendhu Adhikari on Sunday (October 18) said that the next of kin of the deceased will receive compensation of ₹25 lakh.

He added, “One member of the family will be provided a government job. The State government will pay compensation of ₹5 lakh to the injured.”

Talking to the Hindu intern at Medical College, Kolkata, alleged anonymously, “They are forcing us to refer patients to private hospitals. There are no basic emergency facilities available here.”

West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari said, “We are looking at the CCTV when the accident occurred at the Durga Puja Pandal. Those who failed to control the crowd will be taken action against them.”

He also said that we will write to the Chief Justice of the Calcutta High Court to appoint a sitting judge under whom we can constitute a Special Investigation Team to probe the incident,” Mr. Adhikari said.

Offering his heartfelt condolences to those who lost their lives in the Kolkata pandal stampede, Maharashtra CM Devendra Fadnavis said the tragic Chorbagan Durga Puja pandal in Kolkata is extremely heartbreaking.

CM Fadnavis said, “I offer my heartfelt condolences to those who lost their lives in this unfortunate incident. My deepest sympathies are with the grieving families.”

“Wrong announcements were made that the Durga Puja pandal had collapsed, so the crowd started rushing, which led to the tragedy. There were hardly any officials or security personnel who came to the rescue,” a local eyewitness told The Hindu, raising allegations.

Former West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee said, “Deeply shocked and saddened to learn about the stampede at the Chore Bagan Puja Pandal.”

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh said that relief and rescue efforts are underway to ensure timely assistance to all those affected. “My heartfelt condolences to the bereaved families,” he added.
