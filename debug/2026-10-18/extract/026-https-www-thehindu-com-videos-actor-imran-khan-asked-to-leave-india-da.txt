Bollywood actor and US citizen Imran Khan has been issued a notice by Mumbai Police directing him to leave India by October 28 for allegedly violating immigration laws. The action follows his detention after participating in an October 10 protest at August Kranti Maidan organized by the Cockroach Janata Party to demand the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar.
