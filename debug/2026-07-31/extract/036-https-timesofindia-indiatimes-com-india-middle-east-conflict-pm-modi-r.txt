NEW DELHI: Amid the deepening Middle East crisis, PM Narendra Modi Thursday chaired what was the fourth special meeting of the cabinet committee on security (CCS) and directed establishment of a mechanism to provide timely information, emergency assistance and counselling to seafarers, serving on both domestic and foreign-flagged vessels, and their families."The PM said all efforts must be made to safeguard citizens as well as the Indian diaspora from the impact of this conflict," an official statement said. It added that Modi also addressed issues related to fertilisers, energy and safety of Indian diaspora and seafarers, while calling for a whole-of-government approach to tackle the challenges.The release said the meeting also assessed the fertiliser requirement for the ensuing rabi season and discussed alternative sources of fertilisers.
