# Russian detained over plot to 'execute' Ukrainian-American in Warsaw, Tusk says

 Europe 

Poland has arrested a Russian national who was allegedly hired to plan an"execution" targeting a Ukrainian-American dual citizen in Warsaw, Prime Minister Donald Tusk said Thursday. Tusk said it was the first time that “someone on the order of Russia” had sought to attack an American citizen on NATO territory.

Issued on: 13/08/2026 - 16:54 Modified: 13/08/2026 - 19:00

 1 min Reading time

![Image 1: Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk addresses the press during a press conference held in the gardens of the Prime Minister's Office in Warsaw on July 3, 2026](https://s.france24.com/media/display/7ece479e-9724-11f1-9d26-005056a90284/w:1024/p:16x9/000-B97V4G3.jpg)

 Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk addresses the press during a press conference held in the gardens of the Prime Minister's Office in Warsaw on July 3, 2026. © Wojtek Radwanski, AFP 

[Poland](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/poland/)'s prime minister said Thursday that authorities arrested a Russian citizen who was allegedly recruited by [Russia](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/russia/)'s secret services, thwarting his plan to kill a Ukrainian American citizen in the Polish capital,[Warsaw](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/warsaw/).

The Russian was detained ​on ‌August 7 and had been ⁠recruited to kill a man who was "inconvenient to the Putin regime", [Donald Tusk](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/donald-tusk/) told ‌journalists.

Read more[Poland detains suspect in fatal shooting of Russian artist critical of Putin, Tusk says](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260618-poland-detains-suspect-in-fatal-shooting-of-russian-artist-critical-of-putin-tusk-says)

The Russian had been hired to kill a man ​who had both Ukrainian and US passports, Tusk said, without giving any more information on his identity.

"At the last ​minute, thanks to the action of the ​Internal Security Agency and the police, ​we managed to prevent this execution, this attack," the prime minister said.

"We have to accept the assumption that the Putin regime will try to eliminate people who are inconvenient for various reasons," Tusk said.

Warsaw, he added, "will likely come under similar pressure again".

Poland, a NATO and EU member state along the alliance's Eastern flank, has accused Russia of "sabotage" on several occasions since Moscow's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022.

Tusk added that it was the first time that “someone on the order of Russia” sought to attack an American citizen on the territory of another NATO country.

In June, a ​Russian artist critical of President [Vladimir Putin](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/vladimir-putin/) was shot and killed in the eastern Polish town of Biala Podlaska, prosecutors said. Poland says its role as a hub ⁠for military and other supplies to neighbouring Ukraine has made it ⁠a target ​for Russian [espionage](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/espionage/) and attacks.

The Russian embassy in Warsaw did not ⁠immediately respond to an emailed request for comment.

Several opponents of the Russian authorities have been victims of attacks abroad, including in the United Kingdom, Germany, and Lithuania.

Moscow has regularly dismissed accusations by European powers ​of attacks, saying the West is stoking anti-Russian ‌paranoia.

_(FRANCE 24 with AP, AFP and Reuters)_
