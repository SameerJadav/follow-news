**Uttarakhand Chamoli Helicopter Crash Highlights:**A helicopter carrying six people, including the pilot, has crashed amid snowfall in Uttarakhand's Joshimath. The Dehradun-bound helicopter, owned by a private company, Trans Bharat Aviation Pvt, took off from Badrinath around 1:14 PM.

The helicopter, which bore call sign VT-HCX, lost communication when it was flying over Joshimath valley, officials said.

"At the same time, the tracking system could no longer track the helicopter, and its current location has not been ascertained yet," the Uttarakhand Civil Aviation Development Authority said in a statement.

Uttarakhand Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami said the chopper crashed near the ChaieN Village.

"A joint rescue operation is being conducted by the district administration, disaster management department, and SDRF," he wrote on X, adding that he is continuously monitoring the entire incident.

### Major Helicopter Accidents Involving Tourists Across Char Dham Circuit Since 2017

The following are some major accidents involving tourists along Char Dham routes since 2017:

June 10, 2017: An Agusta AW119 MK II, VT-NRK helicopter operated by Kestrel Aviation Pvt Ltd crashed onto sloping terrain near Badrinath helipad shortly after take-off while operating on the Badrinath-Haridwar sector of the Char Dham circuit. One aircraft maintenance engineer died; two pilots sustained minor injuries. Five pilgrims onboard escaped without injury.

October 18, 2022: Aircraft: Bell 407, VT-RPN helicopter operated by Aryan Aviation Pvt Ltd crashed into a mountain ridge at Garud Chatti shortly after taking off from Kedarnath for Guptkashi as part of passenger shuttle service. Deteriorating weather and descending clouds were reported. All seven occupants, comprising one pilot and six passengers, died.

May 8, 2025: Bell 407, VT-OXF helicopter operated by Aero Trans Services Pvt Ltd crashed into a gorge near Gangnani in Uttarkashi district while operating a Char Dham passenger service. The main rotor struck an overhead fibre-optic cable as the pilot attempted to descend towards the highway. Six people, including the pilot, died; one passenger survived with serious injuries.

June 7, 2025: A Leonardo AW119 MK II, VT-RNK helicopter operated by Kestrel Aviation Pvt Ltd lost height shortly after taking off from the elevated Sersi helipad in Rudraprayag for Kedarnath with five passengers. Its tail rotor struck the edge of the helipad and the aircraft crash-landed on the road below, damaging nearby property. No passenger fatalities; the pilot sustained injuries.

June 15, 2025: Bell 407, VT-BKA helicopter operated by Aryan Aviation Pvt Ltd crashed near Gaurikund in Rudraprayag while operating a Kedarnath-Guptkashi passenger shuttle service. It encountered extensive cloud cover and poor visibility near the valley exit. All seven occupants, including the pilot, five passengers and an infant, died.
