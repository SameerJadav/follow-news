# Stranded locals use rare truce to leave Moscow-occupied town in Ukraine

Issued on: 30/10/2026 - 17:44 Modified: 30/10/2026 - 17:44

Share

Some residents of Oleshky, a Russian-occupied town in southern Ukraine ravaged by food and medicine shortages, left on Friday during a rare ceasefire brokered by the United States – due to end on Sunday evening. Ukrainian and Russian officials visited the town together on Friday, delivering aid and marshalling those who wanted to leave.
