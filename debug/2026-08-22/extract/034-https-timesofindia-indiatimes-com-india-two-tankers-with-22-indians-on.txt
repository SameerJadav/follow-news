Two commercial vessels carrying 22 Indian crew members have been hijacked by armed pirates in separate incidents off Yemen and Somalia this week, with the sailors reported safe so far, according to a senior shipping ministry official. The Eritrea-flagged MT Sibu 1, carrying 16 Indians among 20 crew members, was seized in the Gulf of Aden on August 20, around 30 nautical miles off Yemen, while the Cameroon-flagged M/V LUTUF, with six Indians among its 10-member crew, was hijacked off Somalia's Puntland coast on August 17.The MT Sibu 1 was taken over by six armed pirates after an unauthorised vessel approached the tanker, while eight armed pirates operating from two skiffs boarded the M/V LUTUF off Puntland. Both vessels remain under pirate control, while no injuries have been reported among the crew.Tanker with 16 Indians hijacked off YemenThe Eritrea-flagged oil products tanker MT Sibu 1 was hijacked in the Gulf of Aden on August 20, about 30 nautical miles off the Yemeni coast.
