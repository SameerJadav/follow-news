[Turkey](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/turkey/) said Friday it would seek an [Interpol](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/interpol/) red notice for the arrest of Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/benjamin-netanyahu/) as part of a domestic criminal case linked to ​an attack ‌on [activists seeking to deliver humanitarian aid](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260529-france-to-investigate-treatment-of-nationals-on-gaza-flotilla-after-reports-of-abuse) to [Gaza](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/gaza-strip/).

[NATO](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/nato/) member Turkey has been ⁠one of the fiercest critics of [Israel](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/israel/)'s devastating assault on Gaza and has previously issued domestic arrest warrants for Netanyahu over the war there. It has repeatedly called ‌for international measures against Israel over what Ankara calls its violations of [human rights](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/human-rights/) and international ⁠law. Israel denies those charges.

In May, Israeli forces opened fire on at least two vessels from the Global Sumud Flotilla sailing towards Gaza as it was making a renewed attempt to ​deliver aid to the enclave after earlier missions were intercepted by Israel in international ‌waters.

About 50 vessels were in the Global Sumud Flotilla that left Turkey in May heading for the besieged Palestinian territory. The [activists](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/activists/) were detained in the Mediterranean by the Israeli military and then taken to an Israeli prison before being expelled.A number of activists [accused Israeli security forces](https://www.france24.com/en/france/20260522-french-gaza-flotilla-activists-return-to-paris-denounce-violent-detention-in-israel) of physical or sexual violence during their detention.

Israel's far-right National Security Minister [Itamar Ben-Gvir](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/itamar-ben-gvir/) also sparked outrage by releasing a video showing himself [taunting flotilla activists](https://www.france24.com/en/middle-east/20260521-france-condemns-israel-37-french-nationals-deported-over-gaza-flotilla) who were on their knees with their hands tied behind their backs.

In a post on X, Turkish Justice Minister Akin Gurlek said arrest warrants had been issued for Netanyahu and another Israeli, named as Afek Moskovitch, on charges that included [genocide](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/genocide/), [crimes against humanity](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/crimes-against-humanity/) and [torture](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/torture/) as part of a case against 35 people ‌over the Israeli interception in international waters.

"In line with the arrest warrants issued against Benjamin Netanyahu ... our justice ministry has requested that the interior ministry issue (Interpol) red ​notices for them so that they may be sought internationally," Gurlek said.

According to Turkey's IHA news agency, Moskovitch is an Israeli soldier who is also accused over actions in Gaza.

IHA, citing the indictment, said Moskovitch had posted footage on [Instagram](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/instagram/) showing tanks and a bulldozer destroying buildings at the Islamic University of Gaza, including a hospital and medical faculty

Turkey will use all available legal mechanisms to ensure Israel is held accountable for its crimes in Gaza, he added.

## Red notice

A red notice is a ​request to police forces worldwide to locate and provisionally arrest a person pending extradition.

Interpol reviews requests submitted ​by member states before deciding whether to issue red notices.

Asked about ​Gurlek's statement, Netanyahu's office said: "(Turkish President [Tayyip) Erdogan](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/recep-tayyip-erdogan/)'s pathetic attempt to intimidate the leaders and soldiers of Israel, the only true democracy in the Middle East, ​will go nowhere."

The prime minister's office also accused Erdogan of being an "[anti-Semitic](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/anti-semitism/) dictator".

A statement from the Turkish president's office in turn denounced what it described as Netanyahu's "petty political tactics", slamming the Israeli government's attempt "to present Turkey as a new threat".

Turkey and Israel also traded barbs this week over [Syria](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/syria/), where Israel carried out [air strikes](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/air-strikes/) on [an air base it said Turkish troops could be deployed to](https://www.france24.com/en/middle-east/20260818-middle-east-live-iran-declares-60-day-negotiation-deadline-null-and-void), drawing condemnation from Ankara and criticism from the United States. Syria has said there were no plans to deploy Turkish troops to the base.

Turkey's influence in Syria ⁠as an ally of Syrian President [Ahmed al-Sharaa](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/abu-mohammed-al-jolani/)has stirred Israeli alarm since Sharaa toppled veteran leader [Bashar al-Assad](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/bashar-al-assad/) in 2024.

Ankara has ⁠said Israeli rhetoric against Turkey ​and its actions in Syria are fuelled by the approach of a parliamentary election in Israel in October.
