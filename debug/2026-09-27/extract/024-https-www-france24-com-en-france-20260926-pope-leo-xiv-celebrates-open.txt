[Pope Leo XIV](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/pope-leo-iv/) on Saturday rode along the[Champs-Élysées](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/champs-%C3%A9lys%C3%A9es/) in the "popemobile" and [celebrated an open-air Mass](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260926-live-more-than-half-a-million-expected-at-pope-s-open-air-mass-in-paris) in front of hundreds of thousands in [Paris](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/paris/), before flying to the holy pilgrimage site of Lourdes in southern France.

The leader of the world's 1.4 billion Catholics is on a four-day trip to [France](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/france/), an officially secular country with deep Catholic roots, on the first official papal visit there in nearly two decades.

In the highlight of the visit's second day, the 71-year-old pope led believers in a Mass at Place de la Concorde, where a giant white canopy had been erected to serve as a makeshift altar.

"It is truly good and pleasant when brothers and sisters come together and glorify the greatness of God," said Pope Leo, dressed in white liturgical vestments.

Over more than two kilometres, some 800,000 people had gathered all the way beyond the [Arc de Triomphe](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/arc-de-triomphe/), the [Vatican](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/vatican/) said. The city centre was sealed off for the service, with helicopters circling overhead and snipers positioned on rooftops.

"It's one time in history to see the pope," said Aline Riziki, 41, who was born in the Democratic Republic of Congo and who has lived in France for 11 years.

"I dropped everything to come here and see him, to pray for peace in the east" of her homeland, wracked by conflict for decades, she said.

## 'We're sharing this with her'

Jeanne de Montagnac, 29, came with her husband and two-year-old girl, who was perched on his shoulders.

"We're happy that, even if she won't remember it, we're sharing this with her," she said, a small golden cross dangling over her jumper.

"It's an important moment for us to be surrounded by other people who also have faith."

The first US pontiff was visiting France seven months before voters choose a successor to centre-right President [Emmanuel Macron](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/emmanuel-macron/), with the anti-immigration [far right](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/far-right/) eyeing its best opportunity yet to capture the presidency.

A number of presidential hopefuls were present, including far-right leader [Marine Le Pen](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/marine-le-pen/) and centrist Edouard Philippe.

The president himself did not attend the Mass but was represented by his wife Brigitte.

On Sunday, Leo will speak with seven victims of clerical [sex abuse](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/sex-abuse/). A 2021 inquiry found that clergy or lay members of the Church had abused about 330,000 people as minors in France, over seven decades.

## 'Rejecting the mentality of war'

Earlier in the day, the pope [met migrants at Maison Bakhita](https://www.france24.com/en/pope-leo-xiv-calls-to-protect-migrants-during-his-official-visit-to-france), an association supporting refugees. Leo said it was "possible to encounter one another and to live together in dignity and peace".

He also stressed the "fundamental role of women in building a civilisation of love".

The association said the pope's visit sent a "message" ahead of the April-May election.
