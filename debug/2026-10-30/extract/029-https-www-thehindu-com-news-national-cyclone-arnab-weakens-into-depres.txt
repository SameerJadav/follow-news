A low-pressure area over the south Andaman Sea is likely to strengthen into a depression around October 31 and then move west-northwest towards the Andhra Pradesh coast over the following two days, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) said on Thursday (October 29, 2026). Arnab itself has weakened into a depression over central Chhattisgarh.

About 300 villages are cut off in Odisha’s Ganjam district. In north Andhra, the Bahuda flooded Ichchapuram and a submerged railway bridge nearby halted trains.

Rain has now spread to West Bengal, Jharkhand and Madhya Pradesh. Union Home Minister Amit Shah spoke to the Chief Ministers of Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, Chhattisgarh and Puducherry and assured them of all help.

Congress leader Rahul Gandhi expressed deep concern over the devastation caused by Cyclone Arnab in Andhra Pradesh and the heavy rainfall and flooding in Odisha, and urged the governments to ensure swift relief and rehabilitation for every affected family.

The Odisha government deployed more than 300 teams from different disaster response agencies for the rescue operations in flood-hit Ganjam and eight other districts, as Cyclone Arnab left a trail of destruction in several parts of the State.

Andhra Pradesh Energy Minister Gottipati Ravi Kumar said in West Godavari district that the very severe cyclonic storm Arnab has inflicted a loss of at least ₹500 crore to the State’s electricity infrastructure.

The Energy Minister observed that West Godavari district was one of the most impacted regions, where 3,700 power poles were uprooted.

“The cyclone has caused preliminary losses estimated at approximately Rs 500 crore to the state’s electricity infrastructure. A comprehensive assessment would provide a clearer picture of the extent of the damage,” Mr. Kumar said in a press release.

As many as 367 babies were born after 3,100 pregnant women were shifted to hospitals as a precautionary measure ahead of very severe cyclonic storm Arnab in Andhra Pradesh.

The health and medical department identified 4,228 pregnant women, including those expected to deliver within two weeks and those with high-risk pregnancies and shifted 3,100 of them to medical facilities ahead of the cyclone.

“Of the 3,100 pregnant women shifted to hospitals as a precautionary measure during very severe cyclonic storm Arnab, 367 deliveries were recorded,” the department said in a press release issued on Thursday (October 29, 2026).

Andhra Pradesh State Disaster Response and Fire Services Department has cleared more than 10,000 fallen trees following Cyclone Arnab, deploying 281 personnel in 120 wood-cutting teams and 19 boat-rescue teams across affected areas, officials said on Thursday (October 29, 2026).

The Department activated preparedness and emergency-response measures in 14 districts from October 27 to 29, an official press release said.

The Department recorded 3,072 calls, including 2,463 emergency calls and 609 calls through the Swarna Grama Swarna Ward portal. Of these, 3,041 were related to tree-cutting and 31 to rescue operations.

The Department deployed 23 inflatable rubber boats, nine rescue tenders and 21 quick response vehicles. Its equipment included 1,328 life jackets, 853 life buoys, 76 Advanced Search and Rescue Apparatus (ASKA) lights and 120 power saws.

Fire services personnel also undertook water-bailout operations and cleared electrical hazards while teams were deployed to remove fallen trees and road obstructions, assist rescue and evacuation operations and respond to fire emergencies, the release said.

Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N Chandrababu Naidu on Thursday (October 29, 2026) said the very severe cyclonic storm Arnab has inflicted a loss of at least Rs 4,600 crore on the state.

Addressing a press conference at Gachakayala Pora village in Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Konaseema district, the Chief Minister underscored that the estimation of loss will further inflate once the final pictures of the devastation emerge.

The CM noted that he apprised Union Home Minister Amit Shah of the impact of Arnab and explained the situation and efforts undertaken.

According to Mr. Naidu, Mr. Shah promised to dispatch a Central team to the State on Friday. He also said Andhra Pradesh has submitted the preliminary report on the cyclonic destruction.
