The demolition of a heritage mosque near Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s official residence in Delhi has been put on hold for four weeks. The government informed the court that the Land and Development Office will decide on a representation by the Delhi Waqf Board during this period.

However, the government opposed allowing access to the mosque, citing safety concerns because of the demolitions in the area.

The mosque, which the government claims is “unauthorised”, is listed as a heritage property on the municipal council’s website. It had received an eviction notice on October 19, amid a wider demolition drive in the Race Course Road area, including the removal of slum dwellers. Read on.
