# Niger military says it is regaining control after attempted mutiny

Niger’s military says it is regaining control after a mutiny in Niamey, the third major instance of unrest at the capital’s airport complex this year. General Abdourahamane Tiani seized power in 2023, promising security, before breaking with Western partners and strengthening ties with Russia.
