# Warehouse hit by Russian attack stored munitions, Zelensky says

 Europe 

Issued on: 29/08/2026 - 14:35

Share

A depot that exploded in a Russian strike overnight was owned by the Ukrainian military and stored shells, drones, and other munitions, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Saturday. FRANCE 24's Emmanuelle Chaze reports.
