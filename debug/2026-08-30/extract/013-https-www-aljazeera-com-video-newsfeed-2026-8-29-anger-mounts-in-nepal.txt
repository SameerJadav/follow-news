# Anger mounts in Nepal over the government’s response to flash flood

Anger is growing in the flood-hit Nuwakot district as survivors accuse the government of delays in delivering aid and finding missing loved ones after a flash flood devastated the area near the Nepal-Tibet border on Wednesday, Al Jazeera’s Barnaby Lo reports.
