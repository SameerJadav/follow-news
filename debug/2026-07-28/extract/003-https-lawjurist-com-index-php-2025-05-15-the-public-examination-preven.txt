<!-- /wp:paragraph --><p><b><i>Abstract </i></b></p>
<p>Public Examinations plays an important role in selection of eligible candidates for important positions and ranks both in the state and central levels . Hence prevention of any kind of unfair means such as cheating , fraud or misrepresentation becomes crucially important in eliminating any sort of malice involved in making a fair and rational selection , as well as to ensure parity and transparency in such selection . In recent times we have noticed that in many important public examinations unfair means have been used which has disrupted the picture and position of the public examinations in the eyes of the common people , and hence the Indian government in the enactment and implementation of the Public Examination ( Prevention Of Unfair Means ) Bill , 2024 . Through this legislative commentary my main focus is to concentrate on the purpose , features , scope , and impact of this aforesaid bill on the mentioned issue .</p>
<p><b><i>Introduction </i></b></p>
<p><b><i>What are Public Examinations ?</i></b></p>
<p>Ans :-  Section 2 (k) of the Public Examination ( Prevention Of Unfair Means ) Act 2024 explains as any examination conducted by a public examination authority listed in the Act’s Schedule or by any other authority as notified by the Union Government .</p>
<p>The Union Government can add new authorities in the schedule through a notification as and when required .  </p>
<p>The Schedule list includes the following :- Any  public examination conducted by the :- </p>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">Union Public Service Commission </li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">Staff Selection Commission</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">Railway Recruitment Boards </li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">Institute Of Banking Personnel Selection</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">Ministries or Departments of the Union Government and their attached subordinate offices for staff recruitment</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">National Testing Agency </li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">All Ministries or Departments of the Union Government and their attached and subordinate offices for staff recruitment</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">Any other authority as may be notified by the Union Government </li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li aria-level="1"><b><i>Background Of The Above Stated Act :- </i></b></li>
</ul>
<p>The Central Government on 21st June 2024 officially made an announcement for the implementation of the Public Examination ( Prevention Of Unfair Means ) Bill , 2024 . This act was passed by the both the houses of the Union Parliament on 9th February 2024 and it received the assent of the President of India on 12th February 2024 . Despite of the enactment of the said legislation the inordinate delay involved in its implementation gave rise to many questions and objections . This act came into force during the times of frequent leaks of public examinations on a regular basis which created a widespread tensions across the country . The latest Neet – UG Examination question paper leak case of 2024 created a chaotic situation among the candidates who appeared for the said exam in the year of 2024 . As a consequence the said exam was cancelled by the NTA . <b><i></i></b></p>
<ul>
<li aria-level="1"><b><i>Aims and Objectives Of The Act :- </i></b></li>
</ul>
<p>Some of the aims and objectives of the above mentioned act are listed below :- </p>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">It ensures transparency and parity in conducting of public examinations </li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">It makes sures that the candidates are not involved in any kind of malice or wrongful activity which can effect their candidature in that particular public examination</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">It seeks to maintain a clear picture of the dignity and reputation of public examinations in the eyes of the common people</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">It attempts to recruit suitable candidates by a strict implementation of rules and regulations and close monitoring of illegal means during the examination period</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1">It makes sures the strict penalties are imposed on the offenders using or involved in illegal means to resist the possible repetition of such offences in the near future </li>
</ol>
