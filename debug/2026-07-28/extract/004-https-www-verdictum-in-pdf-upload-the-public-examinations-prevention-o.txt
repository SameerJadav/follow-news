New Delhi, the 12 th February, 2024/ Magha 23 , 1945 ( Saka )

The following Act of Parliament received the assent of the President on the 

12th February, 2024 and is hereby published for general information:— 

THE PUBLIC EXAMINATIONS (PREVENTION OF 

UNFAIR MEANS) ACT , 2024 

NO. 1 OF 2024 

[12 th February , 2024.] 

An Act to prevent unfair means in the public examinations and to provide for 

matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. 

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventy-fifth Year of the Republic of India as 

follows:— 

CHAPTER I 

PRELIMINARY 

1. (1) This Act may be called the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) 

Act, 2024. 

(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by 

notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.
