Anything and everything is being called a “revolution”, shows the so-called Gen Z are history challenged. With due respect, it would do well to read about the concept of Revolutions before calling an Instagram page filled with bot accounts comparable to the world’s largest political party.

As a millineal who has studied the history of revolutions across the world, let me tell you one thing: Revolutions create leaders worse than the ones they replace.

For the uninitated, I will take the most violent revolution in history - The French Revolution as a case study in itself. Napoleon was much worse in concentrating the power in his own hands eventually declaring himself as the Emperor. His era was marked by censorship of the press, opposition and other voices, precisely against the ideals of Liberty, Equality and Fraternity which the Revolution stood for in the first place

If CJP just to be another Instagram page which can circulate memes against the BJP, then it's okay. If they really want to effect political change on the ground it needs something more concrete than an Instagram page.

Without any face, organizations and structure, CJP does not stand even the slightest chance to make a real change.
