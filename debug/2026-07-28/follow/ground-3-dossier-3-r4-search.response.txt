He referred to certain activists and unemployed youth as "cockroaches" and "parasites of society." Specifically, he stated that "there are youngsters like cockroaches, who don't get any employment or have any place in profession" and that some of them become journalists, social media activists, or right-to-information campaigners and "start attacking everyone."

The Cockroach Janta Party was founded on May 16, 2026, by Abhijeet Dipke, a political communications strategist and activist, in response to these remarks. Kant later clarified that his remarks were directed at individuals who obtained fraudulent degrees and did not target India's youth in general.
