The Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024, was passed by Parliament on February 9, 2024. It received Presidential assent on February 12, 2024. However, another source states that President Droupadi Murmu gave her assent on February 25, 2024. One source also mentions February 13 as the date of presidential assent. The Act came into force on June 21, 2024.

=== BLOCK q1 ===
When the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024, came into force on June 21, 2024, its key provisions aimed to curb cheating and promote fair conduct in public examinations. The Act broadly defines "unfair means" to include various malpractices such as leaking question papers or answer keys, assisting candidates during exams through unauthorized communication or providing solutions, tampering with computer networks or resources, impersonating candidates, conducting fake examinations, issuing fake documents, and tampering with documents for merit lists or ranks.

For individuals, the Act prescribes imprisonment ranging from 3 to 10 years, depending on the severity of the offense, with fines up to Rs. 1 crore for organized crimes. For service providers involved in malpractices, fines can be up to Rs. 1 crore, and they can be barred from conducting public examinations for four years, with personal liability for involved directors or management. Organized crimes carry harsher penalties, including imprisonment between 5 and 10 years and a minimum fine of Rs. 1 crore, and the involved institution can face property attachment and forfeiture. The Act also empowers authorities to conduct surprise checks, seize electronic devices, blacklist guilty service providers, and facilitate information sharing and coordination among agencies to tackle organized cheating. The Act applies to public examinations conducted by authorities listed in its Schedule or notified by the central government, including the Union Public Service Commission, Staff Selection Commission, Railway Recruitment Board, National Testing Agency, and Institute of Banking Personnel Selection.
