# US signs ‘tremendous’ security deal with Denmark and Greenland

US President Donald Trump and the prime ministers of Greenland and Denmark have signed an agreement allowing an expanded US military presence in Greenland while keeping the island under Danish sovereignty. The deal follows months of Trump pushing to annex the territory.
