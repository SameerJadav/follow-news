## A recap of recent developments

 

    *   President Donald Trump has told reporters ⁠at the UN that US officials ‌had a “very good” three-hour meeting on ⁠Tuesday with the ⁠Iranian delegation at the UN General Assembly.
    *   Qatar’s Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani tells the UNGA that the Gulf is experiencing one of its most dangerous phases due to the US-Israel war on Iran, while urging diplomacy as a lasting solution.
    *   Iranian media has reported the sound of an explosion near Iran’s Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, though the cause remains unclear.
    *   Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan has condemned Israeli actions in Gaza and the occupied West Bank in a blistering speech at the UNGA.
    *   Fierce fighting is ongoing between Saudi-backed government forces and the Houthis in the cities of Taiz, Marib and al-Jawf, including exchanges of artillery fire.
    *   Yemeni government forces said they targeted Houthis in Mocha and the province of al-Bayda.
