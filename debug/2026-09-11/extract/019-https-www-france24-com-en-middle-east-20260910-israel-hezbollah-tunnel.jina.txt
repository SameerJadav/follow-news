# Israel destroys Hezbollah tunnel network in south Lebanon, triggering 4.1-magnitude tremor

 Middle East 

Israel said on Thursday it destroyed key tunnels built by Iran-backed Hezbollah beneath the strategic Ali al-Taher ridge in southern Lebanon, completing what it called operational control of the area. Lebanon reported a massive explosion, while the US Geological Survey recorded a magnitude 4.1 tremor nearby.

Issued on: 10/09/2026 - 23:50

 2 min Reading time

Share

 By: 

[FRANCE 24](https://www.france24.com/en/author/france-24/ "FRANCE 24")

![Image 2: Smoke rises from fires ignited by Israeli shelling that targeted the plain of Marjayoun in southern Lebanon](https://s.france24.com/media/display/b984e69c-a131-11f1-8ff4-005056a97e36/w:1024/p:16x9/d308f39dfd509f2937b01dea496a5c78a39e58ff.jpg)

 Smoke rises from fires ignited by Israeli shelling that targeted the plain of Marjayoun in southern Lebanon © AFP 

[Israel](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/israel/) said Thursday it demolished key tunnels built by the Iran-backed [Hezbollah](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/hezbollah/) group beneath the strategic Ali al-Taher ridge in southern[Lebanon](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/lebanon/), with a 4.1 magnitude tremor recorded in the area.

"This is strategic terrorist infrastructure that was built over two decades, with Iranian funding and planning," said a joint statement by Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/benjamin-netanyahu/) and Defence Minister Israel Katz.

The destruction "completes the achievement of operational control over the Ali al-Taher ridge -- above and below ground", the statement added.

The United States Geological Survey meanwhile reported a 4.1 magnitude tremor in the area, after Lebanon's state-run National News Agency (NNA) reported that Israel carried out a "massive explosion" on the Ali al-Taher ridge in the Nabatieh region, "causing tremors and a powerful rumble".

AFP journalists in south Lebanon also said they heard "huge explosions" on Thursday evening.

"We were sitting there and everything shook," Joelle Fares, who recently moved to Majayoun, told AFP.

Read more[Israel intensifies south Lebanon strikes, killing 13 after weeks-long lull](https://www.france24.com/en/middle-east/20260907-israeli-strikes-south-lebanon-violence)

The Israeli army said the Hezbollah infrastructure extended for more than two kilometres (1.2 miles) and contained "dozens of Iranian-made rockets, missiles, and UAVs (unmanned aerial vehicles), as well as machine [guns](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/guns/), dozens of anti-tank missiles, and hundreds of mines and explosive devices".

The underground compound also included a command centre of Hezbollah's "Badr" unit, the army added.

A [military](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/military/) official told AFP that some of the militants that had operated in the area of the ridge had abandoned it, while others were "eliminated by aerial strikes and artillery [fire](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/fire/)".

Israel announced last week that it had captured the Ali al-Taher ridge.

In March, Hezbollah dragged Lebanon into the [Middle East](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/middle-east/) war in support of its backer Iran.

There had been a lull in violence after the understanding reached between [Iran](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/iran/) and the United States in June, followed by an agreement between Lebanon and Israel.

But strikes have increased in intensity in recent days.

During negotiations, the United States, which sponsored the talks, proposed that the Lebanese army take control of the Ali al-Taher ridge, but Hezbollah refused, a source close to the parties told AFP.

[Beirut](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/beirut/) authorities say Israeli strikes have killed more than 4,300 people in total since March, and Israeli forces continue to occupy a so-called "security zone" in the south.

_(FRANCE 24 with AFP)_
