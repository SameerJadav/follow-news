Reading time 1 min

One of the tragic consequences of global warming. Scientists will tell us whether receding glaciers are to blame for last Wednesday’s ice shelf collapse that triggered a catastrophic flood, cascading down more than 3,000 meters from the Chinese border into Nepal.

We’ll get the latest and draw consequences for Nepal and its Himalayan superpower neighbors, China and India, and for the rest of humanity, as we gauge rescue and recovery in an area that not only boasts the world’s tallest mountains but also serves as Asia’s water tower. How do we prepare for the next big emergency and the drastic long-term changes to our natural habitats?

_Produced by François Picard, Rebecca Gnignati, Daniel Whittington, Juliette Brown, Charles Wente._
