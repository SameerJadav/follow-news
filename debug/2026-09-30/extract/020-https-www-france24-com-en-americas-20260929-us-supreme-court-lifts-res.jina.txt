# US Supreme Court lifts restrictions on third-country deportations

 Americas 

The US Supreme Court on Tuesday cleared the Trump administration to resume deporting migrants to countries other than their own while it considers the legality of the practice. The court’s conservative majority temporarily blocked safeguards requiring deportees to receive notice of where they would be sent and time to raise persecution concerns.

Issued on: 30/09/2026 - 01:17

 2 min Reading time

Share

 By: 

[FRANCE 24](https://www.france24.com/en/author/france-24/ "FRANCE 24")

![Image 2: A general view of the U.S. Supreme Court, where justices are expected to issue opinions on pending cases, in Washington, D.C., U.S.](https://s.france24.com/media/display/b3dfdf88-8270-11f1-9005-005056bfb2b6/w:1024/p:16x9/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B4%D9%86%D8%B7%D9%86.jpg)

 FILE PHOTO: A general view of the U.S. Supreme Court, where justices are expected to issue opinions on pending cases, in Washington, D.C., U.S., June 18, 2026. © Elizabeth Frantz, Reuters 

The [US Supreme Court](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/us-supreme-court/) on Tuesday lifted restrictions on the [deportations of immigrants](https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20260928-us-trump-deportations-triggering-cascade-of-rights-abuses-un-experts-warn-forbidden-stories) to [countries that are not their own](https://www.france24.com/en/africa/20260923-never-heard-eswatini-deported-by-us-country-that-rejects-them-forbidden-stories), while agreeing to hear the issue on its merits in December.

The [conservative](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/republican-party/)-dominated high court granted an emergency request by the Trump administration to suspend a lower court ruling that restricted, but did not outright ban, so-called "third-country removals."

The lower court had said that [people targeted with deportation](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/immigration/) must be given effective notice ahead of time about where they are being sent to allow them to raise concerns about potential persecution.

The three [liberal](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/democratic-party/) justices on the nine-member panel said they would not have granted the Trump administration's request.

US President [Donald Trump](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/donald-trump/) campaigned on promises to expel millions of undocumented migrants from the United States and has taken a number of actions aimed at speeding up deportations since returning to the [White House](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/white-house/) in 2025.

Read more[‘I had never heard of Eswatini’: Deported by the US to a country that rejects them](https://www.france24.com/en/africa/20260923-never-heard-eswatini-deported-by-us-country-that-rejects-them-forbidden-stories)

The Trump administration has defended third-country [deportations](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/deportations/) as necessary since the home nations of some of those who are targeted for removal sometimes refuse to accept them.

In a brief to the [Supreme Court](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/supreme-court/), opponents of lifting the restrictions argued that the lower court ruling did not fully block all third-country deportations, "it simply requires them to do so lawfully."

Trump's solicitor general, John Sauer, meanwhile told the court that the rapid deportation to third countries was "an essential tool to remove certain aliens, including some of the worst criminal aliens."

Read more[US deportations are triggering a 'cascade' of rights abuses, UN experts warn](https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20260928-us-trump-deportations-triggering-cascade-of-rights-abuses-un-experts-warn-forbidden-stories)

The lower court ruling "created substantial logistical problems" with removals, he said.

An AFP investigation found that the US has offered multimillion-dollar deals and threatened [visa restrictions](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/travel-visas/) in a bid to get "third countries," especially in [Africa](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/africa/), to take such deportees.

Once in these countries – and outside [US](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/usa/) jurisdiction – deportees have been subject to abuse by local guards, indefinite detention, or quickly re-deported to their home countries.

[Congress](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/us-politics/) has made it US policy not to deport people to countries where their lives would be in danger or they could be subject to [torture](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/torture/).

_(FRANCE 24 with AFP)_
