# 'Contrary to law': US judge strikes down Trump's 75-country visa ban

 Americas 

A US federal judge has overturned a Trump administration policy suspending immigrant visa processing for nationals of 75 countries, ruling that the measure was "contrary to law" and exceeded the State Department’s legal authority. The decision on Friday marks a setback to Trump's immigration crackdown, though the government can appeal.

Issued on: 22/08/2026 - 14:44 Modified: 22/08/2026 - 14:45

 1 min Reading time

Share

 By: 

[FRANCE 24](https://www.france24.com/en/author/france-24/ "FRANCE 24")

![Image 1: This photo illustration shows a visa stamp on a foreign passport in Los Angeles on June 6, 2020](https://s.france24.com/media/display/ed85732c-9e24-11f1-a4be-005056a97e36/w:1024/p:16x9/000-1UQ6ZV.jpg)

 This photo illustration shows a visa stamp on a foreign passport in Los Angeles on June 6, 2020. © Chris Delmas, AFP file photo 

A [US](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/usa/)federal judge has struck down a government [ban on visas](https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20260114-us-suspends-immigrant-visa-processing-for-75-countries)to nationals from 75 countries who wish to settle in the country indefinitely, dealing a blow to President [Donald Trump](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/donald-trump/)'s [immigration](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/immigration/) crackdown.

In a ruling issued on Friday in the Southern District of New York, Judge Jeannette Vargas called the policy "contrary to law" and said Secretary of State [Marco Rubio](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/marco-rubio/) had exceeded his "statutory authority" in issuing it.

Read more[Supreme Court allows Trump administration to lift deportation protections for Haitians, Syrians](https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20260625-supreme-court-allows-trump-administration-to-lift-deportation-protections-for-haitians-syrians)

The policy, which came into force in January, suspended the processing of immigrant visas to nationals of 75 countries including [Afghanistan](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/afghanistan/), [Brazil](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/brazil/), [Egypt](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/egypt/), [Iran](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/iran/), [Iraq](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/iraq/), [Nigeria](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/nigeria/), [Somalia](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/somalia/), [Thailand](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/thailand/) and [Yemen](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/yemen/).

At that time, the State Department had said the decision was made "to ensure that immigrants from high-risk countries do not unlawfully utilize [welfare](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/welfare/) in the United States or become a public charge".

But Vargas ruled that consular officers were wrongly ordered to refuse immigrant visas solely based on an applicant's country of origin, even if they had determined the applicant was otherwise.

Friday's ruling vacates any visa denial based solely on the policy.

The government can appeal the ruling.

Trump campaigned for the White House on a promise to expel millions of undocumented [migrants](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/migrants/) and has taken a number of actions since returning to the [White House](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/white-house/) aimed at speeding up [deportations](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/deportations/) and reducing border crossings.

_(FRANCE 24 with AFP)_
