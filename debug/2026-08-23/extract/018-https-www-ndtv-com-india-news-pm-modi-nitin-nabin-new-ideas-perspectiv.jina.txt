This was the first time that the new team under BJP president Nitin Nabin had a meeting with the party chief and PM Modi after their appointment on August 17.

BJP vice-presidents Ram Madhav and former Rajasthan chief minister Vasundhra Raje, general secretaries Smriti Irani, a former Union minister, former Tripura chief minister Biplab Deb, general secretary (organisation) B L Santhosh and treasurer and Union minister Piyush Goyal were among the attendees of the meeting.

Some office-bearers of the outgoing national executive also attended the meeting, according to a report by news agency PTI.

Earlier today, the BJP announced a one-month-long nationwide campaign called 'Seva Sankalp Abhiyan' to mark the completion of 25 years of PM Modi in public office as head of a government.

"Every year, BJP organises 'Seva Pakhwada' in the country on the prime minister's birthday. This year, 7 October marks 25 years since Narendra Modi took oath as the Chief Minister of Gujarat. BJP has decided to launch a one-month-long 'Seva Sankalp Abhiyan'," said BJP National General Secretary Vinod Tawde.
