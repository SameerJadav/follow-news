# France to deliver interceptor missiles to Ukraine after fresh wave of Russian strikes

 Europe 

Issued on: 22/08/2026 - 15:26

Share

This Saturday saw a continued exchange of fire between Kyiv and Moscow. French President Emmanuel Macron announced Paris would deliver new interceptor missiles to Ukraine, whose air defences are running short after the latest round of deadly Russian attacks. Details from the ground by FRANCE 24 correspondent in Ukraine, Emmanuelle Chaze.
