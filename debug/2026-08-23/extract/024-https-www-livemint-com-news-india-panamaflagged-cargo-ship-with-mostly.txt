A Panama-flagged cargo ship carrying 24 crew members sank in the Bay of Bengal on Saturday, around 240 nautical miles from the Paradip coast in Odisha, PTI reported.

The vessel was travelling towards Singapore with a cargo of iron ore from Odisha when it sank.

The Indian Coast Guard and Indian Navy have launched a rescue operation to locate and evacuate the crew members.

"We are not sure how many of the crew members have been rescued," a senior IPS officer told PTI, adding that most of those on board were Chinese.

Other vessels operating in the vicinity have also been alerted and asked to assist in the rescue operation, the officer said.

The ship had left the Odisha coast on Friday, according to the officer. The circumstances leading to the vessel's sinking were not immediately known.
