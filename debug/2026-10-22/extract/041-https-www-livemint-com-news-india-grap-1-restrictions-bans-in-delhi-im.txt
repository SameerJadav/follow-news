The Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) imposed stage 1 measures under the Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) for the Delhi-NCR region. The move came after air quality slipped into the 'poor' category on Wednesday, recording a hike from 179 (moderate) on October 20 to 244 (poor) at 2 pm on October 21.

"The Air Quality lndex (AQI) of Delhi which was observed 179 (Moderate category)on October 20, has exhibited a sharp rise with AQI of 244 at 2 pm on October 21 (Poor category)," said the CAQM in a statement.

Under Stage-I of GRAP, authorities have been directed to strictly enforce Pollution Under Control (PUC) norms and take action against visibly polluting vehicles, including impounding them or charging penalties. Existing restrictions on overage diesel and petrol vehicles must also be enforced. Non-destined truck traffic passing through Delhi is to be diverted via the Eastern and Western Peripheral Expressways. Inter-city and interstate buses operating in Delhi-NCR must comply with the prescribed vehicle standards: only electric, CNG or BS-VI diesel buses are permitted as per directions.

GRAP Stage-I is the first level of pollution-control measures and applies when the AQI reaches the ‘poor’ range of 201–300. CAQM invoked it across Delhi-NCR after Delhi’s AQI rose to 244.
