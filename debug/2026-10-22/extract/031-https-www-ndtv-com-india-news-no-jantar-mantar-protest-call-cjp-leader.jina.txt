Das and Ranka had been in judicial custody since October 14. The prosecution said the act breached a peace bond the two had signed after being detained on October 10, the day they had originally called for a protest over alleged electoral fraud.

"Truth has triumphed," Das said as the two were led away from court. The Delhi Police had sought three more days of judicial custody for the two.

Das and Ranka were initially sent to judicial custody for five days when produced before the SEM on October 14, and the custody was extended by two days on Monday.

The CJP, founded by Abhijeet Dipke, alleged that proceedings were being deliberately delayed to keep the two in custody, and moved the Delhi High Court against the extension.
