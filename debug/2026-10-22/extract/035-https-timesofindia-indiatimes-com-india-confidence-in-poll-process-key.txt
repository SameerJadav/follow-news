NEW DELHI: Supreme Court on Wednesday said instilling confidence in the election process was vital for democracy and the rule of law as it directed Andhra Pradesh State Election Commission (SEC) not to declare the final candidate list for 19 of the 100 local bodies where opposition YSRCP has alleged that its candidates were prevented from filing nominations.
