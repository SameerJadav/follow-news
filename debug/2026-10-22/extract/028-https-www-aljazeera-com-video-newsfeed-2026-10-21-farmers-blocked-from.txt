# Farmers blocked from olive harvest in occ. West Bank, volunteers detained

Palestinian farmers are still being blocked from accessing their olive groves by Israeli settlers and the military during this year’s harvest in the occupied West Bank. Volunteers supporting farmers have also been detained, including US congressional candidate Brad Lander and activist Andrey X.
