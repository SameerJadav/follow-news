# Exit polls show 'disaster' for Merz' CDU party in two state elections

 Europe 

Issued on: 20/09/2026 - 18:54

Share

Germans headed to the polls today in two state elections - in Berlin and Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania. Exit polls suggest a dramatic loss for the Chancellor Freidrich Mertz's Christian Democratic Union party. Details by FRANCE 24 correspondent in Berlin, Jörg Taszman.
