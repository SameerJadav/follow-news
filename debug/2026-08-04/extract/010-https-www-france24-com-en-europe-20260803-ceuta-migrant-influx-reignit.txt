The [unprecedented influx of migrants](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260803-morocco-blames-ceuta-migrant-rush-on-misleading-information-on-social-media) who made their way from [Morocco](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/morocco/)into the [Spanish enclave of Ceuta](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260802-spain-ceuta-navigates-aftermath-border-surge-after-most-migrants-return-morocco) in North Africa last Thursday and Friday has created tensions between [Spain](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/spain/)and the European partners most critical of its migration policy.[Italy](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/italy/), followed by [Denmark](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/denmark/), immediately called for Spain to be suspended from the Schengen Area.

The demand has angered [Madrid](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/madrid/), who requested a videoconference [meeting between EU interior ministers](https://www.france24.com/en/europe-to-hold-meeting-tuesday-over-ceuta-after-migration-flux) to take place on Tuesday.

In recent months, [Brussels](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/brussels/) has already [significantly tightened its immigration legislation](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260326-return-hubs-eu-parliament-clears-the-way-for-offshore-migrant-detention-centres) through the [New Pact on Migration and Asylum](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260612-eu-adopts-major-landmark-asylum-reform-hardening-border-procedures) which, among other measures, allows member states to send rejected asylum seekers to centres outside of the EU’s borders in so-called “[return hubs](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20251208-eu-migration-return-hubs)”.
