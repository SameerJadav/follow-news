# Video: Trump signs order to re-name Lake Ontario as Lake America

US President Donald Trump has signed an executive order to rename Lake Ontario as Lake America. One of the five Great Lakes, Ontario spans the border between Canada and the United States. Trump’s move comes amid an escalating trade war between the two countries.
