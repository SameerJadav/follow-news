# Patients treated in tents after earthquake damages Indonesia hospital

An Indonesian healthcare worker filmed patients being treated in makeshift tents outside Ruteng General Hospital after a 7.7-magnitude earthquake damaged the facility. More than 50 people were killed and thousands displaced in the quake.
