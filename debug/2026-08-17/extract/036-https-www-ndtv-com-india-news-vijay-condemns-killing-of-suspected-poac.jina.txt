**Chennai:**
Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Vijay has condemned the shooting of three Tamil men in Karnataka's Chamarajanagar district. Calling the explanation provided by the Karnataka forest department "unacceptable", he demanded a thorough probe into the deaths.

Early on Saturday morning, suspected poachers had allegedly entered the forest to hunt wildlife. Acting on a tip-off, forest officials had rushed to the area.

Sources said the poachers had allegedly opened fire at forest personnel, who retaliated, leading to a gun battle. Three men, suspected of being poachers, were killed in the exchange of fire. The men were identified as Antony Swamy, Peter and Kumar.

Some documents and other materials, including guns, have been seized. A postmortem examination is being conducted.

"Anthony Sami, John Rose Peter, and Kumar, who were Tamils residing in villages in the Hanur taluk of Chamarajanagar district in Karnataka, were shot dead by Karnataka Forest Department officials in the early hours of August 15, 2026, in the Cauvery Wildlife Sanctuary area. I strongly condemn this," Vijay said in a post on social media platform X.

"The Karnataka government must provide all necessary assistance to the families of the victims," he added.

Karnataka Chief Minister DK Shivakumar saod he received the information about the encounter around 5 am.

"The forest department officials came and gave me first-hand information," he said. The guns of the poachers have been seized along with two bags of meat of wild animals, he said.

"One person had escaped... They had shot our forest personnel who were involved there," the Chief Minister said.

Shivakumar said he has already ordered an inquiry by a magistrate. "I have also said that there should be a proper response and appropriate action," he said. Compensation will be paid to the families, he added, pointing out that the details were yet to be finalised.

Leader of the Opposition Ashok said, "The bullets hit the heart, though it should hit only the leg".

Accusing the commissioner of giving a wrong report, he said the BJP demands a judicial inquiry in the matter.
