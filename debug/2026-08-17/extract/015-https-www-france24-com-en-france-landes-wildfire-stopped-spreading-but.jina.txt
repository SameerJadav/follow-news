# France: Landes wildfire stopped spreading but remains out of control

 France 

Issued on: 16/08/2026 - 12:43

Share

Firefighters in southwestern France are continuing to battle a wildfire that has burned 1,700 hectares of forest in the Landes region. The blaze has stopped spreading but remains out of control, with 550 firefighters, military personnel and six water-bombing aircraft working to contain it and allow evacuated residents to return home.
