At least 19 persons, including women and children, were killed and 25 injured in a stampede at a Durga Puja pandal in north Kolkata on Saturday evening, Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari said.

The stampede occurred at around 7.40 pm as crowds gathered at the pandal during rainfall, ANI quoted the Kolkata Police as saying.

The rain prompted people waiting outside the pandal to rush inside for shelter. An announcement warning that the pandal might collapse if the crowd was not brought under control reportedly added to the confusion and triggered a sudden rush, according to The Indian Express.

The injured were taken to Kolkata Medical College and Hospital and other hospitals for treatment.

Seven persons, including the secretary and vice-president of the organising committee, have been arrested in connection with the incident, PTI quoted the Kolkata Police as saying on Sunday. Two persons in-charge of security and a person responsible for construction work were among those arrested.

Police have registered a suo motu case of culpable homicide not amounting to murder at the Girish Park police station. Fourteen persons were detained for questioning on Saturday night, of whom seven have been arrested. The police said more arrests could follow.

Adhikari said surveillance camera footage would be examined and those found responsible for failures in crowd control would face action, ANI reported.

He also said the government would seek the appointment of a sitting judge of the Calcutta High Court to oversee a special investigation team probe.

The pandal has since been closed to visitors, and the site was handed over to investigators and forensic teams, the chief minister said.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced Rs 2 lakh in compensation from the Prime Minister’s National Relief Fund for the family of each person killed in the stampede, and Rs 50,000 for each injured person.

Adhikari announced Rs 25 lakh for the family of each person killed, along with a government job for one family member in a Group D post, which typically involves entry-level support work such as office assistance.

The state government will also provide Rs 5 lakh to each injured person and cover their medical treatment costs, the chief minister said.

President Droupadi Murmu, Union Home Minister Amit Shah, Leader of Opposition in the Lok Sabha Rahul Gandhi and former West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee were among those who expressed condolences.

The Kolkata Police on Saturday asked several major Durga Puja organisers to temporarily close their pandals following the stampede, including at Tala Pratyay, Alipore Sarbojanin, Chetla Agrani and Ballygunge Sarbojanin, The New Indian Express repororted.

The police said the closures were temporary measures to control crowds, with a review meeting scheduled for Sunday.

Prohibitory orders under Section 163 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita were imposed in parts of Bowbazar on Sunday until Tuesday to “prevent a breach of peace”, ANI reported.

The restrictions prohibit gatherings of five or more people, carrying lathis or dangerous weapons, and activities that could disrupt public harmony.

The restricted area includes the vicinity of Kolkata Medical College and Hospital in central parts of the city.
