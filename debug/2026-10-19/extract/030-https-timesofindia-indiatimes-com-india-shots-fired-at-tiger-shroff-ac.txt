NEW DELHI: Unknown gunmen opened fire at actor Tiger Shroff on Sunday, injuring his driver and another person, an autorickshaw driver. The incident took place near Mumbai's Pushpa Park, within the limits of Juhu police station in the western suburb.According to police, the actor escaped unhurt, while his driver, identified as Firdaus, sustained an injury to his left hand."The chain of events leading to the incident is being pieced together," a police official said."Shroff is safe and has not sustained any injuries in the firing incident. His driver, identified as Firdaus, and another driver of the autorickshaw, identified only as Chauhan, were injured in the shooting and were rushed for medical treatment," the official added.Shroff and his driver were taken to Nanavati Hospital in Vile Parle.
