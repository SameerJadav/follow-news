# Trump defends Russia sanctions relief, accuses Zelensky of rejecting peace

 Europe 

US President Donald Trump on Sunday accused Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky of prolonging the war with Russia,claiming Kyiv had little interest in peace.Trump said negotiations to end the four-year conflict were making“great progress”but alleged Zelensky was reluctant to reach a deal because of European financial support.

Issued on: 18/10/2026 - 23:59

 2 min Reading time

Share

 By: 

[FRANCE 24](https://www.france24.com/en/author/france-24/ "FRANCE 24")

![Image 2: Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky (L) and US President Donald Trump pose for a photo during their meeting in Washington, DC](https://s.france24.com/media/display/c4a57392-8d19-11f1-9ad5-005056bfb2b6/w:1024/p:16x9/000-C3EB39E-1.jpg)

 Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky (L) and US President Donald Trump pose for a photo during their meeting in Washington, DC. © Ukrainian Presidential Press Service, AFP 

US President [Donald Trump](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/donald-trump/) on Sunday defended his decision to waive some [sanctions](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/sanctions/) on Russia, while once again accusing Ukrainian leader [Volodymyr Zelensky](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/volodymyr-zelensky/) of not wanting to end the war.

"My decision on Russia is a very simple one. I am in the process of negotiating Peace in the war between [Russia](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/russia/) and [Ukraine](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/ukraine/), and don't want to do anything that will drive, foolishly, Russia away from the bargaining table," Trump wrote on his Truth Social platform.

The remarks come after Trump signed an executive order late Friday waiving broad new sanctions mandated by legislation named for the late US senator Lindsey Graham, a staunch supporter of Ukraine's defense.

Read more[Who capped Starlink in Ukraine? Musk, Trump and Rubio give three different answers](https://www.france24.com/en/tv-shows/tech-24/20261018-who-capped-starlink-in-ukraine-musk-trump-and-rubio-give-three-different-answers)

Trump has also temporarily waived sanctions on Russian diesel, allowing large quantities to flow into global markets in a bid to lower fuel prices ahead of US [elections](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/elections/) early next month.

The diesel deal was met with shock by [Kyiv](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/kyiv/) and its allies and sparked a feud between Trump and Zelensky that has seen their roller-coaster relationship plummet again.

"Great progress is being made" in talks on the war, "and that includes current hostage negotiations!" Trump said.

"Signing that document at this time will do that, and I can sign it later, or any time that I want!" he said, though it was unclear to what document he was referring.

Asked for more clarity on the statement, the [White House](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/white-house/) did not immediately respond to AFP.

"Perhaps more importantly, Russia is doing very little, if anything, to help the Islamic Republic of [Iran](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/iran/), compared to what they could be doing, and I want to keep it that way, and will, unless stupid people get in the way," Trump added.

Read more[Rubio says Trump to 'analyze' global impact of Russia sanctions during pause](https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20261018-rubio-says-trump-russia-sanctions-waiver-designed-to-analyze-global-impact)

He then reiterated his accusation that Ukraine was purposefully targeting Russian refineries to attempt to hurt the Republican Party in the November 3 election.

Polling shows Trump's party heading for a likely drubbing in the election, with many voters deeply upset by fuel costs, which have soared since the [Iran war](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/iran-conflict/) began in late February.

"Zelensky is using Diesel Refineries in Russia as a target, knowing that these attacks are seriously hurting the world diesel markets, including those in the United States," Trump said.

"Zelensky doesn't care, and frankly, with all of the money that [Europe](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/europe/) is famously giving him, and that Crooked [Joe Biden](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/joe-biden/) gave him in the past, he doesn't want to end this war, and probably never did," he added.

_(FRANCE 24 with AFP)_
