# Israeli settlers attack European diplomats, beat French mayor

Issued on: 18/10/2026 - 18:51

Share

Israeli settlers attacked a convoy of European diplomats in the West Bank and beat up a visiting French mayor on Saturday, witnesses said, while US Secretary of State Marco Rubio warned against a brewing "crisis" in the territory a day after an American photojournalist was injured in a brutal attack.
