CEC protests: Several detained during ‘Tiranga March’ in Mumbai

The Mumbai Police on Friday detained several persons who had gathered for a “Tiranga March” at Carter Road in Bandra, the Cockroach Janta Party has alleged.

The CJP had called the march in solidarity with its leaders Ashutosh Ranka, Saurav Das and Ajinkya Shinde, who were arrested by the Delhi Police amid protests in the country against Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar.

The police had not granted permission for the march, but protesters reached Carter Road to participate in the demonstration. The CJP said its supporters were detained by the Vakola and Bandra police.

CJP volunteer Purva Khetan also alleged that some students were not allowed to leave their place of stay in Vakola, in Santacruz East, ahead of the march. She told Scroll that the police had not informed the students why they were being prevented from leaving.

Vakola Senior Police Inspector Navnath Ghuge told Scroll that none of those persons had been taken to the police station. He said the police had not allowed a few persons who had “seemed suspicious and did not give their names and residential details” to leave their place of stay.

A similar Tiranga March had been organised in Delhi on Friday, ahead of which CJP founder Abhijeet Dipke was detained and later placed under preventive arrest. He was released on Saturday.

This comes against the backdrop of protests in the national capital by the the political campaign, the Opposition parties and student groups to demand the resignation of Kumar over concerns about the functioning of the Election Commission and its handling of the special intensive revision of electoral rolls.

Protests against Kumar have been held in the national capital and other parts of the country since October 2.

The trigger for the protests was a September 23 investigation by The Indian Express, which revealed that Election Commissioners Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi had formally objected 14 times in 10 months to decisions by the poll panel related to the special intensive revision, saying some had been taken without their knowledge.
