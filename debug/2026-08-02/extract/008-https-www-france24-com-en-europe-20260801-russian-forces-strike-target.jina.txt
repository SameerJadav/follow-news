# Russian forces strike targets in Ukraine's Odesa, Mykolaiv ports

 Europe 

Russia said on Saturday its forces had struck fuel storage facilities in the Ukrainian port of Odesa and a tugboat used by the Ukrainian military in Mykolaiv. Russia and Ukraine have intensified attacks in recent weeks on Black Sea shipping routes, rattling global grain markets.

Issued on: 01/08/2026 - 17:53

 1 min Reading time

 File photo: Smoke rises from civilian cargo ship Golden Leo following an alleged Russian missile strike on the Black Sea near Odesa on July 19, 2026. © Stringer, AFP 

[Russian](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/russia/)⁠forces ​carried out ​strikes on fuel storage tanks in ​the ‌[Ukrainian](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/ukraine/) ⁠port ‌of [Odesa](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/odesa/) and hit ⁠a marine tugboat repurposed for ​use ‌by the Ukrainian army ‌in the ​port of Mykolaiv, Russia's Interfax news agency ​reported ​Saturday, ​citing the Defence Ministry.

Read more[Has Russia given up on Kinburn Spit, its westernmost foothold in Ukraine?](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260610-has-russia-given-up-on-kinburn-spit-its-westernmost-foothold-in-ukraine)

The battlefield reports could not be independently verified.

Russia and Ukraine have both stepped up attacks in recent weeks on shipping in ⁠the [Black Sea](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/black-sea/) and Sea of ⁠Azov, ​driving [wheat](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/wheat/) prices higher.
