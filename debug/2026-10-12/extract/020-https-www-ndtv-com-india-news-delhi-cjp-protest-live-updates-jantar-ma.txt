### Left-leaning Student Outfits Hold Rally In Kolkata, Demand Gyanesh Kumar's Resignation

Several Left-leaning student organisations on Sunday held a rally in the metropolis, demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over alleged irregularities in the SIR exercise.

The outfits also condemned the police action on protesters in New Delhi.

More than 3,000 students participated in the rally organised by the Students' Federation of India, All India Students' Federation (AISF), All India Students' Association (AISA) and the All India Democratic Students' Organisation (AIDSO), among others, SFI central executive committee member Subhajit Sarkar said.

The procession passed through Esplanade, where vehicular traffic was disrupted for around an hour, before culminating at Rani Rashmoni Avenue.

The protesters also raised slogans demanding Kumar's removal.
