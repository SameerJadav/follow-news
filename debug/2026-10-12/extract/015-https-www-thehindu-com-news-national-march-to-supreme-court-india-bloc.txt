Amid intensifying anti-CEC protests and police crackdown on protesters, Leader of the Opposition (LoP) Rahul Gandhi has announced a march to the Supreme Court on Monday (October 12, 2026). The Congress leader has called on the “youth, all opposition parties, civil society groups, all those who believe in democracy and stand by the Constitution of India” to join the march. The Opposition alliance seeks to submit a memorandum to the Chief Justice of India, Surya Kant, over alleged ‘vote chori’ (vote theft), but the CJI has declined to meet the Congress leader.

The national capital, which is witnessing a string of street protests against Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) Gyanesh Kumar and Special Intensive Revision (SIR), is bracing for another march on October 12 by the opposition alliance INDIA bloc.

Mr. Gandhi said that opposition parties, civil society groups and the youth would march to the Supreme Court to submit a memorandum to the CJI on behalf of the country. The Congress has termed the October 12 protest as “Chalo Delhi - March for Democracy”, for which the leaders are scheduled to converge at the Connaught Place-Barakhamba Road junction at 1.30 pm.

In a post on X, Mr. Gandhi said the march to the Supreme Court would be taken out by Indians united beyond parties, gender, caste, community and State. He appealed to every single person who believes in democracy to come with us. “Doesn’t matter what your affiliation is, as long as you are affiliated to the Constitution of India and you want to protect it,” he said.

Referring to October 10 protests, called by several youth organisations, including the Cockroach Janata Party (CJP), Mr Gandhi said the nation’s youth had come out in Delhi to protest peacefully but were “beaten”, both by “BJP goons and the police”.

Mr. Gandhi and Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge have sought a meeting with CJI between 4 and 5 pm on Monday to hand over a memorandum demanding an end to “vote chori”, CEC Gyanesh Kumar’s resignation, halting of SIR of electoral rolls, and switching back to the paper ballot format.

October 12 will be the third day of the anti-CEC protests, which began on October 10. CJP and opposition leaders were among 7,000 protesters detained at multiple locations on October 10 as authorities thwarted their attempt to reach Jantar Mantar for a peaceful protest against CEC Kumar even as the national capital came under an unprecedented security clampdown.
