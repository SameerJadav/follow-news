The Delhi Police detained at least nine journalists covering protests against Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar for the second consecutive day on Sunday.

Three Indian Express Hindi journalists, two Peek TV journalists and at least three other journalists working with Malayalam news channels, including Reporter TV, 24 News and Asianet News, were also detained.

The Indian Express reported that its journalists Shashwat, Siya and Vijay were detained while reporting from Connaught Place. They were taken to Chhatrasal Stadium and later released, the newspaper reported.

Independent journalist M Rajshekhar was held while taking photographs of students being detained in Connaught Place.

On Saturday, Scroll journalists Ayush Tiwari and Shoaib Daniyal were detained in Delhi, and Divya Aslesha in Pune on Saturday while covering protests demanding the resignation of Kumar.

Journalists from Newslaundry, The Quint, the Navbharat Times, Reuters, Alt News, Boom Live, Molitics and Reporter TV were also detained. They were released later that day.

On Sunday, Reporters Sans Frontières raised concerns over the “brutal detention” of journalists in India while they were covering the protests.

At least 16 journalists, including Scroll’s Daniyal and Aslesha, had been detained, said the organisation, which is known as Reporters Without Borders in English..

“Covering events of public interest is not a crime,” the media watchdog said. “It is their job. The police’s actions against the press are unacceptable.”

The protests have been called by the Cockroach Janta Party political campaign and student groups affiliated with Left parties. Protests have been held in several parts of the country in recent weeks, with demonstrators demanding Kumar’s resignation over concerns about the functioning of the Election Commission and its handling of the special intensive revision of electoral rolls.
