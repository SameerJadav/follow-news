# German ex-spy chief arrested for suspected espionage, treason

 Europe 

Issued on: 06/10/2026 - 21:42 Modified: 06/10/2026 - 21:47

Share

A former German spy chief was arrested Tuesday on suspicion of espionage and treason, an embarrassing case at a time when the country is seeking to broaden the powers of its intelligence agencies. August Hanning, who ran Germany's BND foreign intelligence agency from 1998 to 2005, is accused of espionage, treason and working for foreign intelligence services, federal prosecutors said. FRANCE 24's Nick Holdsworth reports from Berlin.
