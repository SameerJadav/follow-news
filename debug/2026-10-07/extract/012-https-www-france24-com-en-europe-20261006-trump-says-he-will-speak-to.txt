US President [Donald Trump](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/donald-trump/) said Tuesday he would speak very soon with Russian President [Vladimir Putin](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/vladimir-putin/) over the [reported plague death](https://www.france24.com/en/russian-lab-worker-s-death-sparks-plague-fears-as-unverified-claims-spread-online-1) of a laboratory technician, as Washington pressured Moscow for transparency.

Russian authorities denied that they were imposing [anti-plague measures](https://www.france24.com/en/tv-shows/press-review/20261006-should-we-be-worried-about-a-plague-outbreak-in-russia) and played down the [reports of an incident](https://www.france24.com/en/russian-officials-say-situation-under-control-after-plague-reports) at an anti-plague institute in Siberia's Irkutsk region last week.

But [Washington](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/washington-d-c/) is showing growing concern, with Secretary of State [Marco Rubio](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/marco-rubio/) calling on Russia to be open about the situation.

"We encourage [Russia](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/russia/) to be transparent and share more information with the world so that all of us could react accordingly and appropriately," Rubio said during a trip to Iceland.

"Depending on what we learn about it, we would learn how to respond."

Trump later said he would be speaking to Putin about what happened.

"I have a call scheduled, yes," Trump told reporters at the [White House](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/white-house/) when asked if he had spoken to Putin on the issue yet, adding that it would happen "very soon."

The Wall Street Journal reported that the US State Department had sent Russia a confidential diplomatic communication known as a demarche warning Moscow that failure to disclose details runs counter to its international health obligations.

## Cause of death 'undetermined'

Russian news outlet People of Baikal reported Friday that the dead woman – named as 28-year-old Darya Shipilova – may have contracted pneumonic plague from a broken test tube.

Other media reported a possible link between her death and a lab accident at the facility.

The People of Baikal outlet also said up to 200 people with whom she may have had contact were under quarantine.

But Russian authorities say the cause of death is not confirmed and [health](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/health/) watchdog Rospotrebnadzor has denied claims circulating in a document online that special measures had been put in place.

"We wish to clarify that the document and information in question do not originate from official sources and are untrue," it said on Telegram.

Russian officials said postmortem tests had not detected the [disease](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/diseases/).

Irkutsk Regional Governor Igor Kobzev said Monday that a Rospotrebnadzor inspection had found the woman died of pneumonia and the cause was "undetermined".

"No microorganisms linked to the young woman's professional work were found in her biological samples," he said, adding that no other staff at the institute had sought medical attention since her death.

The [World Health Organization](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/who/) said that an update received Tuesday from Russia stressed that "no case of this dangerous disease has been recorded in the city of Irkutsk."

"Timely, complete and transparent information sharing under the International Health Regulations is essential to clarify conflicting reports and enable an accurate assessment of potential [public health](https://www.france24.com/en/tag/public-health/) risks," WHO chief Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus wrote on X.

The update said Russia had launched an epidemiological investigation into a "case of community-acquired pneumonia" in an employee at the Irkutsk institute, and had identified contacts and made arrangements for medical monitoring and laboratory testing.

"Test results showed no presence of dangerous infectious disease pathogens in any of the contacts," the update said, according to WHO.
