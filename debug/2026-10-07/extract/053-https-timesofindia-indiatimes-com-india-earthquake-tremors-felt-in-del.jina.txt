![Image 3: 4.9-magnitude earthquake hits Uttarakhand's Chamoli, tremors felt in Delhi-NCR](https://static.toiimg.com/thumb/msid-134746314,imgsize-37110,width-400,height-225,resizemode-4/delhi-ncr-earthquake.jpg)

Photo for representation.

Earthquake tremors were felt across Delhi-NCR on Tuesday.According to the National Centre for Seismology, a magnitude 4.9 earthquake struck Uttarakhand's Chamoli district at 10.23 pm.The [quake](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/topic/quake) occurred at a depth of 13 km, with its epicentre located at 30.103°N latitude and 79.561°E longitude.

> — NCS_Earthquake (@NCS_Earthquake) [](https://twitter.com/NCS_Earthquake/status/2107518338733842685)

Tremors were felt in parts of Delhi-NCR. There were no immediate reports of casualties or major damage.More details awaited.
