Total solar eclipse brings darkness across parts of Europe and Iceland

A rare total solar eclipse plunged parts of Europe into near-total darkness on August 12, 2026, creating a historic spectacle for millions of viewers1. The astronomical event began at 15:34 GMT, with the period of totality lasting about two minutes1. The path of totality passed over northern Russia, Greenland, Iceland, Spain, and part of Portugal, reaching approximately 15 million people1. On the same day, people across parts of Africa were able to see a partial solar eclipse2.

Thousands of people gathered in Spain, Iceland, and other European countries to observe the sky3. This event marked the first total solar eclipse visible in mainland Europe since 20064. In Spain, it was the first total solar eclipse in more than a century3. Spanish authorities expected at least 500,000 extra visitors to travel to viewing areas3. The Spanish government estimated that 450,000 extra tourists would bring a 347-million-euro ($400 million) economic boost to the country4.

To maintain safety and manage traffic during the surge of visitors, Spain deployed more than 33,500 law enforcement officers around eclipse viewing sites and access points3. Spanish Interior Minister Fernando Grande-Marlaska stated that nearly 25,000 police officers were mobilised for the event4.

Iceland also celebrated a major astronomical moment on August 12, 2026. The country experienced its first total solar eclipse since 19543. In the capital city of Reykjavík, the total eclipse was visible for the first time since 14333.

The event deeply moved experienced space travelers. European Space Agency reserve astronaut Sara García Alonso told RTVE that the eclipse was one of the most beautiful things she had ever seen and gave her goosebumps5. Astronaut Pablo Álvarez Fernández stated that the eclipse was much more beautiful than he had expected5.

People in the region will soon get another chance to see solar events. Another total solar eclipse is expected to cross southern Spain and northern Africa in August 20274. That will be followed by an annular eclipse over the Iberian Peninsula in 20284. However, after these events pass, the next total solar eclipse visible from the UK, France, and mainland Europe will not take place until September 20815.

White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt to leave role at end of August

White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt will step down from her position at the end of August 20261. President Donald Trump wrote in a social media post that Leavitt will become one of his top outside advisors and an influential voice within the Republican Party1. At age 28, Leavitt was the youngest person ever to serve as White House Press Secretary1.

During her time in the administration, Leavitt experienced several significant events. In February 2025, her office took control of the White House press pool from the White House Correspondents' Association1. She gave birth to her second child, Viviana, with her husband Nicholas Riccio in May 20261. She delayed her maternity leave that month following an alleged assassination attempt on Trump at the White House Correspondents' Dinner2.

Leavitt's departure follows the exit of four senior female Cabinet-level officials from Trump's second administration by mid-20263. These included Kristi Noem, Pam Bondi, Lori Chavez-DeRemer, and Tulsi Gabbard3. Before her White House role, Leavitt ran for a New Hampshire Congressional seat in 2022, winning the Republican primary but losing the general election to Democrat Chris Pappas1.

Major earthquake in Colombia leaves hundreds dead and triggers rescue effort

A powerful 7.4-magnitude earthquake struck western Colombia at 07:34 local time (12:34 GMT) on August 10, 20261. The epicenter was near San José del Palmar in Chocó province1. Colombian President Abelardo de la Espriella declared a state of emergency following the disaster2.

The earthquake caused widespread destruction across western Colombia. In Pereira, at least 92 buildings collapsed1. According to the association Asocapitales, 79 people died in Pereira and 95 died in Cali1. President de la Espriella stated that at least 265 people died and 496 remain missing1. France 24 reported that more than 200 people were confirmed dead and over 2,000 were reported missing 48 hours after the disaster2.

Search and rescue operations continued as teams searched for missing people in the ruins3. Emergency workers scored a dramatic success when 32-year-old Daniela Largo was rescued alive from the ruins of a collapsed hotel in Pereira4. She had spent 36 hours trapped under the debris following the earthquake1.

Tata Sons Chairman N. Chandrasekaran to step down in February 2027

Natarajan Chandrasekaran, the chairman of Tata Sons, has announced that he will step down from his leadership position at the end of his current tenure in February 20271. The announcement marks the upcoming end of a decade-long leadership run at the top of one of India's largest business conglomerates.

Chandrasekaran stated that he decided to step down because Tata Sons board member Noel Tata did not support extending his term for a third five-year period1. Noel Tata took on a major role in the group's leadership in October 2024, when he became chairman of Tata Trusts following the death of Ratan Tata1. Former Tata Sons Chairman Ratan Tata died on October 9, 2024, at the age of 862.

The relationship between Tata Trusts and Tata Sons is central to the group's governance structure. Tata Trusts controls a 66% stake in Tata Sons1. Meanwhile, the Shapoorji Pallonji Group holds approximately an 18% stake in Tata Sons1. Under internal company rules, the leadership of these two entities is kept separate. Tata Sons amended its articles of association in 2022 to bar the chairman of Tata Trusts from simultaneously serving as chairman of Tata Sons3.

Chandrasekaran has spent his entire career within the Tata Group. He worked at Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) for 30 years, starting in 19871. He served as the Chief Executive Officer of TCS from 2009 to 2017, before becoming the chairman of Tata Sons in January 20171.

His time leading Tata Sons was accompanied by high financial compensation. For the 2026 financial year, Chandrasekaran received a total remuneration of ₹158.66 crore from Tata Sons4. That payment included ₹140.69 crore in profit-linked commission4. Between the 2022 and 2026 financial years, Chandrasekaran earned a cumulative ₹671.4 crore in remuneration from Tata Sons4.

As Tata Sons prepares for leadership change, potential successors are being discussed. Tata Steel Managing Director TV Narendran is being considered among candidates to replace Chandrasekaran as Tata Sons chairman3. Meanwhile, the group faces legal and administrative matters elsewhere. The Sir Ratan Tata Trust (SRTT) is currently unable to convene trustee meetings due to a directive from the Maharashtra charity commissioner3.

China calls border situation stable following reports of new standoffs with India

Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun stated on August 12, 2026, that the border situation between China and India is generally stable at the moment1. The comment came after military standoffs and face-offs between Indian and Chinese troops emerged in the Taksing area of Upper Subansiri district in Arunachal Pradesh in early July 20261. China claims large parts of Arunachal Pradesh as South Tibet, referring to the region as Zangnan2.

The two nations held diplomatic talks shortly before the spokesperson's statement. India and China held the 36th meeting of the Working Mechanism for Consultation and Coordination on India-China Border Affairs in New Delhi on August 6, 20262. On August 7, 2026, the Indian Union Home Ministry officially issued standard Indian names for 27 locations and geographical features in Arunachal Pradesh3.

Tensions along the long frontier have flared in recent years. Clashes in the Galwan Valley in June 2020 killed 20 Indian soldiers and at least four Chinese soldiers1. In October 2024, India and China reached a disengagement agreement covering friction points at Depsang and Demchok3.
