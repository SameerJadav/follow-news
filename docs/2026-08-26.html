Iran and Oman discuss temporary shipping lane in Strait of Hormuz

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met Omani Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi in Tehran for talks about shipping1. The two officials discussed an interim framework for ships passing through the Strait of Hormuz1.

A joint statement carried by the Oman News Agency said Iran and Oman want to establish a temporary joint maritime corridor1. The two nations also plan a mine clearance project in the strait1.

War broke out on February 28 after the US and Israel attacked Iran2. Iran blockaded the Strait of Hormuz2. Washington then placed a naval counter-blockade on Iranian ports2. A 60-day peace deal between Iran and the US ended in mid-August1.

US President Donald Trump said on social media that the US Navy cleared mines from the strait1. Trump warned that any boat placing new mines will be destroyed immediately1. On August 25, an unidentified projectile struck an oil tanker at the mouth of the strait3. The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations Centre reported the attack3.

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced sanctions on 60 people, entities, and vessels trading with Iran3. Chinese buyers take about 90% of Iranian oil exports, according to reporting by the Financial Times4. China's foreign ministry spokesperson Lin Jian said China will firmly safeguard its rights3.

Canada places retaliatory tariffs up to 50% on US goods

Canada announced retaliatory tariffs of up to 50% on US goods starting September 81. The new measures target $20 billion worth of American imports1.

The trade dispute between the two nations has grown deeper. The new Canadian tariffs will impose duties across many goods1. One report said the duties cover 700 products1. Another report said the counter-tariffs apply to nearly 900 items2. These items equal nearly C$28 billion in value2. That amount equals $20 billion2.

The tariffs include a 50% duty on American steel and aluminum1. Canadian officials said that US steel imports to Canada have already fallen 30%3. That fall happened after Canada introduced an earlier 25% tariff3.

The political leaders of both countries have exchanged strong statements. Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney accused US President Donald Trump of wanting to destroy Canadian industries2. President Trump suggested on social media that he may change the name of Lake Ontario to Lake America2. Trump made the comment on Truth Social2.

Trump also said his administration will raise tariffs on Canadian goods in the future4. He said US tariffs on Canadian vehicles, automotive parts, and steel will rise to 50%4. That increase is set to start on January 1, 20274.

Trade between the two neighbours remains large. Canada maintains a trade surplus with the US of C$9.9 billion5. According to Statistics Canada, that trade surplus equals $7.1 billion5.

To protect the domestic economy, the Canadian government announced financial aid. Canadian Finance Minister François-Philippe Champagne announced an additional C$7.5 billion support package2. The funds will support businesses and workers impacted by US tariffs2.

Canadian Industry Minister Mélanie Joly outlined details of the C$7.5 billion support package3. The aid plan includes C$3 million in grants3. It also includes C$2 million in interest-free loans for small businesses3.

CIA Director John Ratcliffe makes unannounced trip to Moscow

CIA Director John Ratcliffe made an unannounced visit to Moscow on August 25 for talks with Russian officials1. CBS News first reported the trip1.

Tracking data from FlightRadar24 showed a US military C-17 plane left Riga at 05:50 GMT1. The aircraft landed in Moscow at 07:04 GMT1. It flew back to Riga shortly before 17:00 GMT on August 251. The US Air Force Boeing C-17A Globemaster III started its journey from Joint Base Andrews in Maryland2. It landed at Moscow's Vnukovo International Airport2.

A senior Ukrainian official told CBS News that the US notified Kyiv about the visit1. The US requested Ukraine to pause strikes until the delegation left1. The Financial Times reported the Trump administration asked Kyiv to pause long-range strikes on major Russian cities during the visit3. But Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said no talks with US envoys were planned for the week4.

This trip is the first known visit by a US spy chief to Moscow since former CIA Director William Burns visited in November 20215.

US sanctions four Indian companies over Iranian petroleum imports

The United States Department of State sanctioned four Indian companies for importing Iranian petroleum products1. The sanctions were issued under Operation Economic Outcast1. They used Executive Orders 13846 and 139492.

The US targeted Portease Partners, Sadashiva Overseas, PP Softtech, and Prakrutees Infra Impex1. Three individuals were also sanctioned1. They are Portease partners Indrismiya Ashrafmiya Sheikh and Harish Ramachandra Rangi, and PP Softtech director Prashant Garg1.

The US Department of State said Sadashiva Overseas imported about $69 million worth of Iranian petroleum products1. That trade took place between February 2024 and June 20253. PP Softtech and Prakrutees Infra each imported about $25 million worth1. PP Softtech imported products between January 2024 and June 20253. Prakrutees Infra imported products between May 2023 and February 20263.

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the goal is to sever every economic lifeline sustaining Iran1. Iranian Economy Minister Ali Madanizadeh told state television that Tehran was fully prepared for the new sanctions1.

Ajit Doval and Wang Yi hold border talks in Beijing

Indian National Security Advisor Ajit Doval met Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi in Beijing for border talks1. The meeting marked the 25th round of Special Representatives talks on the India-China boundary question1.

Both countries discussed their long-standing border issue. India and China agreed to continue seeking a political settlement of the boundary question1. They agreed to do this in the context of their overall and long-term interests1.

During the talks, Doval spoke about progress on local trade and travel. Doval said that the Kailash Manasarovar Yatra via Nathu La has successfully resumed1. He also said that traditional border trade via all three passes has successfully resumed1.

Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi spoke about the broader importance of ties between the two nations. Wang said that the China-India relationship has gone beyond the bilateral scope1. He said the relationship has taken on global and strategic significance1. Wang also called for gradually building a positive narrative on boundary affairs between India and China2.

Before holding the main border talks, Doval met another senior Chinese leader. Doval held a bilateral meeting with Chinese Vice President Han Zheng in Beijing on August 253. That meeting took place before the Special Representatives border talks3.

Chinese Ambassador to India Xu Feihong shared details about that meeting. Xu said that Vice President Han highlighted earlier high-level contacts between the two nations3. Han highlighted meetings between Chinese President Xi Jinping and Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi3. Those meetings took place in Kazan and Tianjin over the past two years3.

Student protesters clash with police in Bihar over job exams

Student protesters clashed with police in Patna on August 25 during a march toward the Chief Minister's residence1. The march was heading toward Lok Sevak Awas, the official home of the Chief Minister1.

Police used batons and water cannons after protesters broke through barricades at Dak Bungalow Chowk1. A Deputy Superintendent of Police was severely injured after being hit by thrown stones1. Police detained at least 25 protesters in Patna before releasing them later2.

The protests follow a government job announcement. The Bihar government issued a notification on August 18 for 32,388 teaching posts1. The posts are to be filled through the Teacher Recruitment Examination1. Protesting students demanded the exam be held in a single phase without negative marks1.

Bihar Public Service Commission Examination Controller Rajesh Kumar Singh expressed regret over comments he made1. He had quoted a Hindi proverb that students interpreted as calling them dogs1.

Student representatives met Bihar top bureaucrat Pratyaya Amrit2. Amrit assured them of a favourable response within a couple of days2.
