Houthis Seize Yemeni Lands as Tanker Attack Hits Strait of Hormuz

Houthi rebel forces seized the Yemeni port of Mokha1. The forces also took Mayyun Island near the Bab el-Mandab strait1. The Saudi-backed Yemeni government lost these key coastal oil shipping lands to the Iran-aligned Houthis2. Fighting across Yemen has displaced about 100,000 people3.

A projectile hit the MT El Gaia tanker over the weekend in the Strait of Hormuz4. The ship was sailing in dark mode with its identification system turned off4. The tanker carried 25 crew members4. Among them were 14 Indian nationals4. The ship also carried 11 Pakistani nationals4.

Pakistani Third Engineer Muhammad Hammad Obaida is missing after an engine room watch4. Indian Oiler Sumit Kumar Singh is also missing4. The Omani Coast Guard brought 23 remaining crew members ashore4.

Iran's IRGC Navy said the ship hit naval mines on an illegal route4. But the US said a missile and a drone hit the vessel4.

Only four ships crossed the Strait of Hormuz on Monday3. Before the war, about 130 ships crossed each day3. Saudi Aramco closed its East-West pipeline after a Houthi attack3. Oil loadings stopped at the Saudi port of Yanbu3. The price of Brent crude rose nearly 3% to $108 a barrel3.

Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman talked with Egyptian President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi in Cairo5. They called for free navigation in the Red Sea5. Egypt lost about $7 billion from the Suez Canal between 2023 and 20242. This was about 60 percent of canal revenues2.

NATO Intercepts Drone Over Lithuania as Baltic Tensions Rise

Lithuanian Defence Minister Robertas Kaunas commented on the downed drone1. Kaunas said authorities detected an explosive device on the drone1. Bomb-disposal experts were called to the site1. The experts neutralized the explosive device1.

Military actions also escalated elsewhere in the region3. Poland scrambled its own fighter jets as a preventive step3. Russian jet-powered unmanned aerial vehicles were carrying out strikes on Ukrainian territory3.

Polish security forces later found a drone in the Baltic Sea3. The aircraft was identified as a Russian drone3. Forces recovered it near the beach in the town of Rusinowo3.

Denmark reported another dangerous event in the Baltic Sea1. A Russian frigate fired flares at a Danish air force helicopter1. The helicopter was performing routine surveillance work1. The incident occurred off Gedser in the Baltic Sea1.

Danish Defence Minister Jeppe Bruus released details of the confrontation3. Bruus said the Russian ship fired two flares at the helicopter3. The warship gave no prior warning before firing3. One of the flares passed within metres of the aircraft3.

Denmark summoned the Russian ambassador to respond to the flare incident4. The summons concerned the threat to the Danish air force helicopter4.

Russian ambassador Vladimir Barbin addressed the Danish accusations4. Barbin said Russia would thoroughly investigate the incident4. But Barbin accused Danish helicopters of taking provocative actions4. He said these actions occurred near Russian warships4.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy made a statement on retaliatory strikes5. Zelenskyy said Ukrainian forces struck an oil refinery located in Syzran, Russia5. He said the strike was a direct response to Russian attacks5. Russia had targeted Ukraine's energy infrastructure5.

US War With Iran Costs Billions and Depletes Interceptor Stockpiles

The Congressional Budget Office reported that the war with Iran has cost the US military $38 billion1. This figure covers costs up to August 1, 20261. The report estimated the conflict adds $3 billion in military costs for every extra month1. Rebuilding the depleted US stockpile of interceptors will take at least five years1. These include Patriot, Thaad, and Navy interceptors1.

A Pentagon inspector general report showed the US spent $22.3 billion on munitions alone through June 301. Total spending on the conflict reached $33.4 billion by that date1. The CBO estimated the US has used between one-half and two-thirds of its missile defense interceptors since June 20252. CSIS estimated Patriot interceptors dropped from about 2,330 before the war to between 759 and 827 by July 273.

President Donald Trump denied reports of weapons shortages4. Trump claimed US munitions supplies are virtually unlimited4.

The US military lost four F-15E fighter jets, one F-35 fighter, and 30 MQ-9 Reaper drones through June 304. Three F-15E jets were destroyed by friendly fire over Kuwait on March 13. Another was lost over Iran in April3. At least 11 US service members died in action through June 303. Seven died in non-hostile events3. Another 417 were wounded3.

The White House requested $87.6 billion in extra funding in June5. The request set aside $67.1 billion for the Department of Defense5.

Guo also asked the US to stop preparing for war in outer space4. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said Russia counts on international efforts to completely demilitarise space4.

India Introduces Merchant Fees for UPI Payments Over 2,000 Rupees

The National Payments Corporation of India introduced a new fee framework for UPI merchant payments1. The Merchant Discount Rate framework takes effect on October 151. NPCI issued a circular about the rule on Tuesday, September 15, 20262.

Under the framework, customers will pay no charges for making UPI payments1. This zero-fee rule for consumers applies regardless of transaction value1. NPCI explicitly barred UPI app providers from charging platform fees to consumers3. App providers are also barred from adding any other charges to consumers for UPI payments3.

Merchants receiving UPI payments above Rs 2,000 in a single transaction will pay a 0.4% fee1. The fee is capped at a maximum of Rs 3001. Small merchants receiving up to Rs 1 lakh per month will pay no fees on incoming payments1.

The Union Ministry of Finance issued the official notification under Section 10A of the 2007 Payment and Settlement Systems Act4. Parliament passed the 2026 Taxation and Other Laws Amendment Bill in August 20264. That bill amended the 2007 law to allow charges on UPI and RuPay debit card payments4.

The Indian government said that data analysis shows only 4% of merchant UPI transactions will be impacted2. Standard credit card fees typically range from 1.5% to 2.5% per transaction, according to NPCI3.

The Finance Ministry said that collected fees will be shared among payment participants1. These participants include banks and app providers1. The money will support operations and system expansion1. A dedicated fund will receive 5% of total MDR collections2. This fund will promote UPI usage by small merchants2.

A flat fee of Rs 5 will apply to UPI transactions above Rs 2,000 in essential sectors1. These sectors include railways, telecommunications, insurance, fuel, and agricultural inputs1. Essential sectors account for nearly 17% of merchant UPI transaction volume5. They also account for around 46% of merchant transaction value5.

Capital market transactions will attract a fee of 0.02%1. This applies to mutual funds, securities, stockbrokers, and dealers1. The fee is capped at Rs 300 per transaction1.

Person-to-person UPI transfers will remain completely free for individuals1. These transfers account for 37% of total UPI transaction volume2. They also account for 70% of total UPI transaction value1.

Opposition parties in India criticized the new fee5. They called the charge the "Modi tax"5. Congress media head Pawan Khera spoke out against Prime Minister Narendra Modi5. Khera said Modi popularised UPI for a cashless economy and is now taxing people for using it5. Leader of Opposition Rahul Gandhi alleged that Modi surrendered to American pressure5. Gandhi said American payment companies had opposed India's zero-fee policy5.
