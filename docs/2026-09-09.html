They face charges of culpable homicide and negligence1. Police also apprehended labour contractor Sanoj in Uttar Pradesh1.

The Municipal Corporation of Delhi demolished 24 properties across Delhi after the collapse2. It also sealed seven illegal structures2. The MCD suspended South Zone deputy commissioner Rakesh Kumar and four engineers2. Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta instructed officials to demolish an adjacent dilapidated building3. Delhi Lieutenant Governor Taranjit Singh Sandhu ordered a structural audit of all student housing hubs within a week2.
