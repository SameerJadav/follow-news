Russian Drone Strike Hits Kryvyi Rih Mall in Double-Tap Attack

A Russian double-tap drone strike targeted the largest shopping center in Kryvyi Rih, leaving many dead and injured1. News outlets and regional officials gave varying casualty numbers following the attack1. The BBC reported that at least 15 people were killed in the strike1. In contrast, Al Jazeera reported that 14 people were killed2. Dnipropetrovsk regional head Oleksandr Ganzha also stated that the drone strikes on the Kryvyi Rih shopping centre killed 14 people3.

The number of reported injuries from the mall attack also varied across reports1. The BBC stated that 130 people were injured, including 23 children, when the drone attack struck Kryvyi Rih's largest mall1. Al Jazeera reported that the strike injured over 100 people, including 22 children2. Meanwhile, Oleksandr Ganzha stated that the drone strikes wounded 121 others3. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky stated that the second wave of the drone strike specifically targeted emergency workers who were responding to the scene1.

Russian strikes were reported in other areas of Ukraine as well1. Separate Russian attacks in Ukraine's Mykolaiv region killed four people, including three children1. In the Kharkiv region, a separate Russian drone strike killed two women, aged 73 and 66, and injured two other people4.

Ukrainian forces also conducted drone strikes across the border inside Russia5. A Ukrainian drone attack struck a car near Razumnoye in Russia's Belgorod region, killing one person and wounding four others5. President Volodymyr Zelensky stated that a Ukrainian drone attack hit an oil refinery in Perm as well as the Marinovka military airfield in Volgograd5. Russia's Defence Ministry stated that its air defences intercepted 325 Ukrainian drones over 15 Russian regions and Crimea4.

The drone strikes occur as the broader conflict continues1. Russian President Vladimir Putin launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 20221. Moscow currently controls about a fifth of Ukrainian territory1. Meanwhile, international political leaders are preparing for diplomatic discussions regarding Ukraine3. The Coalition of the Willing meeting, set to take place on Ukraine's independence day, will be co-chaired by French President Emmanuel Macron, German Chancellor Friedrich Merz, and UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham3.

Hong Kong Court Convicts Former Tiananmen Vigil Leaders Under Security Law

A panel of three judges in Hong Kong has convicted former Tiananmen vigil organizers Lee Cheuk-yan and Chow Hang-tung for incitement to subversion1. Lee Cheuk-yan, aged 69, and Chow Hang-tung, aged 41, face a maximum penalty of 10 years in prison under Hong Kong's national security law2. Former Hong Kong lawmaker Albert Ho, who was a co-defendant in the subversion trial, pleaded guilty in January2.

The subversion case against their organization, the Hong Kong Alliance, focused on its slogan demanding an end to one-party rule2. The Hong Kong Alliance was originally founded in May 1989 to support student pro-democracy rallies in Beijing3. Chow Hang-tung previously had her 2022 conviction overturned by Hong Kong's top court regarding a 2021 arrest for inciting the public to join the Tiananmen vigil3. While in detention, Chow Hang-tung wrote a letter from prison to UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham, urging Britain to adopt a more robust stance against China2.

Rahul Gandhi Stages Sit-In Protest Over Student Pellet Gun Injury in Delhi

Indian political leader Rahul Gandhi staged a seven-hour sit-in protest outside the office of New Delhi's Deputy Commissioner of Police to demand formal police action regarding an injured student1. Gandhi's protest demanded a First Information Report, known as an FIR, over severe pellet gun injuries suffered by 19-year-old student Sahil Lochab1. During a student march in Delhi on July 20, Sahil Lochab sustained over 200 pellets in his body and lost vision in his right eye1. Following Rahul Gandhi's sit-in protest, Delhi Police registered an FIR under Sections 118(1) and 125 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita1. Rahul Gandhi claimed that the final approval to register the FIR came directly from Home Secretary and Union Home Minister Amit Shah2.

The police actions taken during the July 20 student protest have drawn conflicting reports and claims from government figures and security forces1. A general diary entry recorded at the Sansad Marg police station stated that Rapid Action Force personnel fired two rounds from anti-riot guns1. However, an inquiry conducted by the Central Reserve Police Force found that Rapid Action Force personnel actually fired seven pellet rounds1. In contrast to reports of severe injuries, Union Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju claimed that no protester was seriously injured or suffered broken bones during the July 20 police action3.

Official investigations and political fallout followed the demonstration1. The Supreme Court of India constituted a five-member High-Powered Enquiry Committee, headed by a former Supreme Court judge, to investigate the police action during the July 20 protest1. The political impact was also felt in the national cabinet, where Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan resigned on July 25 following opposition protests over police action against student demonstrators4.

Government figures attacked Rahul Gandhi's motivations for staging the demonstration5. Union Minister JP Nadda criticized Rahul Gandhi's sit-in as camera-centric drama designed to distract from criticism regarding the national song and flag5.

Indian Government Rules Out Ethanol Diversion as Sugar Prices Increase

The Indian government estimated domestic sugar production for the current season at 306 lakh tonnes, which is 11 percent lower than the initial estimate of 343 lakh tonnes1. Lower supply contributed to rising costs, with retail sugar prices in India reaching Rs 58.2 per kg, representing a 20 percent increase over the previous month1. Regional differences emerged across the country2. Odisha registered the highest retail sugar price in India at Rs 64.72 per kg, which is up Rs 17.80 compared to the same date in 20252. International sugar prices also rose, jumping more than 16 percent to $552 per tonne on August 20 from $474 per tonne on June 303.

The government rejected claims that sugar shortages were caused by ethanol production1. Official data showed the share of Indian sugar diverted for ethanol production declined from around 12 percent in 2022-23 to around 9 percent in 2025-261. To address the shortage and stabilize domestic prices, the Indian government permitted the duty-free import of 10 lakh tonnes of raw sugar1. It also imposed a stock limit of 400 tonnes on sugar dealers from August 1 to November 302. Meanwhile, payment processing remained high, as 97 percent of sugarcane dues for the 2025-26 season had been paid to Indian farmers as of August 203.

Supreme Court Cuts Legal Practice Requirement for Entry-Level Indian Judges

The Supreme Court of India ruled in a 2-1 majority that advocates with one year of practice are eligible to apply for entry-level judicial officer posts, reducing the prior three-year requirement1. Chief Justice Surya Kant and Justice A G Masih formed the majority opinion, while Justice Vinod Chandran dissented1.

Under the new rules, candidates applying for Civil Judge recruitment between May 25, 2025, and March 31, 2027, will be deemed to meet the one-year legal practice requirement without needing to provide proof certificates2. However, starting April 1, 2027, candidates applying for Civil Judge recruitment must produce a certificate of proof of practice showing participation in effective judicial proceedings2.

Once selected, judicial candidates will undergo a mandatory two-year post-selection training period1. During their first year of training, candidates will receive fixed emoluments equal to half the salary of magistrates1. The comprehensive post-selection judicial training program includes one year at a State judicial academy, six months of clerkship under a Principal District Judge, and six months under a High Court judge2.
