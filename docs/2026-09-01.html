Draft Voter Lists in India Delete Millions of Registered Names

Electoral authorities removed 6.15 crore voter names from draft lists across 19 Indian states and territories1. The deletions happened during phase three of the Special Intensive Revision1. Out of 36.08 crore total voters in those areas, officials kept 29.93 crore names on the rolls1. Delhi recorded the highest percentage of deletions at 32.78 percent1. Delhi deleted 47.7 lakh names, dropping its electorate from 1.45 crore to 97.5 lakh2. Maharashtra saw the largest absolute reduction, losing 2.06 crore voter names1. That removed 21.1 percent of Maharashtra's previous voter base, leaving 7.7 crore voters3. Karnataka recorded 1.07 crore voter deletions, which was the second highest total1. Draft rolls also removed over 19 percent of voters in Karnataka, 16 percent in Haryana, and 10.8 percent in Andhra Pradesh4. Karnataka Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar and Telangana Chief Minister A. In March, the Supreme Court upheld the legality of the Special Intensive Revision process1.
