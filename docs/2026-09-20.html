United States and Denmark reach new security agreement for Greenland

The United States and Denmark announced a security agreement regarding Greenland to resolve a diplomatic dispute1.

US President Donald Trump wrote about the agreement on social media1. He said Denmark will grant the US permanent control over security in Greenland1. He said Denmark will grant control over all other needs1. Trump added that this control will come at no cost to the United States1.

Leaders plan to sign the security deal next week1. They expect to sign it at the UN General Assembly1. The agreement involves the US, Denmark, and Greenland1.

An official from the US State Department spoke about the agreement rules1. The official said non-NATO countries cannot build military bases in Greenland1. Non-NATO countries also cannot invest in sensitive sectors under the deal1.

Danish Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen spoke about the agreement1. Frederiksen said the deal strengthens common security in the Arctic1. She said it strengthens security in the North Atlantic1. She said it recognizes the sovereignty of Denmark1. She said it recognizes the territorial integrity of Denmark1. She said it recognizes the right of the Greenlandic people to self-determination1.

French President Emmanuel Macron commented on the deal1. Macron said the agreement reaffirms Danish sovereignty over Greenland1. He said the deal respects international law1.

Danish Foreign Minister Lars Lokke Rasmussen praised the deal2. Rasmussen called it a binding agreement2. He said it strengthens security while respecting Denmark's red lines2.

Greenland Prime Minister Jens-Frederik Nielsen also spoke2. Nielsen said the agreement recognizes Greenland's interests2. He said the deal benefits all parties2.

Greenland has a population of roughly 56,000 residents3. More than two-thirds of its territory lies within the Arctic Circle3.

The United States already operates a base in Greenland3. It runs Pituffik Space Base in northwestern Greenland3. The US operates the base under the Defense of Greenland Treaty3. That treaty was signed in 19513.

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio praised the new agreement3. Rubio called the agreement a historic deal3. He said it completely addresses US national security concerns3.

Donald Trump first suggested buying Greenland from Denmark in 20194. He suggested buying the island during his first term as US president4. In January 2026, Trump indicated the US could use force to seize Greenland4.

United States enacts law to place high tariffs on Russian oil buyers

US President Donald Trump signed a new trade bill into law on Friday, September 18, 20261. The White House confirmed that Trump signed H.R. 53341. The official name of the law is the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act, 20262.

The new law permits the United States to punish certain countries2. It permits the US to impose a 100% tariff on goods from target nations2. This tariff applies to countries that continue importing crude oil or natural gas from Russia2. The tax takes effect 30 days after enactment2.

The bill received strong support in the US Congress1. The US Senate passed the legislation in August with an 86 to 11 vote1. The House of Representatives passed the measure by a vote of 262 to 1591.

Republican Senator Lindsey Graham spearheaded the legislation3. Senator Graham died in July 20263.

The law was originally created to stop funding for Russia's war in Ukraine2. It targeted top Russian leadership and energy customers2. Lawmakers later amended the bill text2. The new text extended sanctions on Iran for another five years until 20312.

India relies heavily on Russian energy supplies2. Russia accounted for more than 51% of India's crude oil imports in July 20262. India's imports of Russian oil hit a 38-month low in December 20253. Imports then reached an 11-month high in April 2026 after the US and Israel war on Iran3.

India already faces other US trade tariffs2. India faces a 10% tariff under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 19742. It faces a 50% tariff under Section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 19622.

The Trump administration previously put a 25% punitive tariff on India in August 2025 over Russian oil3. Trump removed that tariff in February 2026 after an interim trade deal3.

Indian leaders criticized the new US law1. Union Minister of State Kirti Vardhan Singh said India considers the US tariffs very unfair1. Singh said India will buy energy from viable sources independently1. India's Ministry of External Affairs said New Delhi is committed to energy security through diversified sourcing3.
