Train collision in central Spain kills 69 people as safety system investigation begins

A high-speed train collided with a stationary train in central Spain on Wednesday morning1. The crash near Urda killed 69 people1. It also injured more than 100 people1. The moving Renfe AVE train struck the stationary Iryo train from behind at about 8:53am1.

Spain began three days of official mourning on Thursday after the crash3. Spanish train drivers went on strike to demand more investment in railway safety3. Foreign ministry officials reported that 42 foreign citizens from seven countries were involved in the accident3. One foreign citizen died3. At least seven foreign citizens suffered injuries3.

The Guardia Civil set up offices in Madrid, Toledo, Córdoba, and Seville to collect DNA samples3. Relatives reported 64 people missing by Thursday morning3.

Several prominent people died in the crash1. Julieta Gallego, a 24-year-old long jumper for the AD Sprint athletics club in Madrid, was killed1.
