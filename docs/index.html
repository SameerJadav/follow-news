United States Redeploys Aircraft Carrier to Middle East Amid Tensions With Iran

The United States is moving the aircraft carrier USS George Washington to the Middle East to relieve the USS Abraham Lincoln1. A US defense official confirmed that the USS George Washington is currently en route1. The deployment of the USS Abraham Lincoln was extended because the ongoing conflict with Iran demanded its continued presence in the region, according to Acting Secretary of the Navy Hung Cao1. The USS Abraham Lincoln has spent more than 240 consecutive days at sea during its mission in the Middle East2.

In New York, US President Donald Trump stated that Iran was being very badly defeated3. President Trump also suggested that he would soon declare the Strait of Hormuz a territory of the United States3. Iranian officials quickly rejected his statements3. Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi posted on X that the Strait of Hormuz cannot be seized with a tweet or with an aircraft carrier3. In another post on X, Gharibabadi stated that the waterway has been Iranian, is Iranian, and will remain Iranian4. Security in the crucial waterway remains fragile, as state-owned United Arab Emirates oil giant ADNOC reported that one of its vessels was attacked in the Strait of Hormuz on Friday night4.

Meanwhile, diplomatic disputes over military actions at the start of the conflict continue between Iran and Qatar5. Iran called on Qatar to release three Iranian pilots who were captured after two fighter jets were hit by air defence systems5. Iranian Brigadier General Mohammad Bagherzadeh sent a letter to the International Committee of the Red Cross stating that pilots Javad Salehi, Abdul Majid Dashtian, and Imran Behroshian have been held captive by Qatar for six months5. General Bagherzadeh also stated that the body of a fourth pilot, Majid Kazemi, was returned after he died during a combat mission on March 2, 20265.

Qatar has strongly denied the claims made by Iran regarding the detention of the pilots5. Qatari officials described Iran's statements as misleading5. Qatari Foreign Ministry spokesman Majed Al-Ansari confirmed that Qatari air defence forces engaged two Iranian warplanes in March after the aircraft entered Qatari airspace without responding to warnings3. Qatar's Defence Ministry previously reported in early March 2026 that its forces shot down two Iranian Sukhoi Su-24 bombers during an attack targeted at Al Udeid Air Base3.

Powerful 7.7-Magnitude Earthquake Kills Dozens in Eastern Indonesia

A powerful 7.7-magnitude earthquake struck off the island of Flores in eastern Indonesia shortly before 05:00 local time on Saturday1. The earthquake hit at a depth of 15 kilometres1. At least 47 people were reported killed across several provinces in the region1. Authorities initially issued a tsunami warning but lifted it about three hours later when no significant rise in sea level was detected1.

The main quake was followed by more than 230 aftershocks, including a large tremor measuring 6.12. Within the same 24-hour period, a separate 6.9-magnitude earthquake hit Sumatra at a depth of 172 kilometres2. The United States Geological Survey reported that the epicentre of the 7.7-magnitude quake was 68 kilometres northwest of Ende at a depth of 10 kilometres3.

In the port town of Maumere, rescue workers found 20 dead, six severely injured, and two people trapped under rubble4. Sixteen bodies were recovered from a landslide in Reok village in the Manggarai region3. Landslides in Ende regency severed the 435-mile Trans-Flores mountain highway4. The Indonesian government sent 50,000 aid packages totaling 275 tonnes5. European Union chief Ursula von der Leyen offered satellite support from the Copernicus Earth observation system5.

Israeli Airstrikes in Southern Lebanon Kill at Least 11 People

Israeli airstrikes in southern Lebanon on Saturday killed at least 11 people and injured 19 others1. An airstrike on a house in the village of Ansar killed seven people, including three children and two women1. The Israeli military stated that the strike in Ansar killed Ali Samir Al-Haj Hassan, a commander in Hezbollah's Radwan Force unit1. Lebanese Prime Minister Nawaf Salam posted on X that the civilians killed in Ansar were not military infrastructure1.

The Israeli Prime Minister's office stated that a Hezbollah attack on the Ali al-Taher Ridge had seriously injured three Israeli soldiers before the retaliatory strike2. In the border town of Bint Jbeil, Israeli forces detonated several homes, according to Lebanese official news agency NNA2.

The attacks occurred during diplomatic efforts. Lebanese President Joseph Aoun stated that the Israeli strikes sent a clear message regarding US-led framework negotiations2. Hezbollah released a statement demanding an end to humiliating peace negotiations with Israel3. However, Netanyahu spokesman Doron Spielman stated that the latest strikes should jumpstart negotiations and make them much more serious4.

Taliban Celebrates Five Years in Power Amid Criticism Over Women's Rights

Supporters of the Taliban gathered in central Kabul on Saturday, August 15, to mark five years of Taliban rule in Afghanistan1. During the anniversary events, Taliban members performed the traditional Pashtun attan dance in a public square in Kabul1. The Taliban regained control of Afghanistan on August 15, 2021, following the withdrawal of foreign NATO troops2.

Since taking power, the administration has placed strict limits on women. The Taliban imposed an indefinite ban on education for girls beyond the sixth grade and later suspended higher education for women2. U.N. Women stated that Afghanistan has systematically dismantled the rights of half its population over the past five years2. However, Taliban deputy broadcasting minister Molvi Hayatullah Mahajar Farahi stated that the government has delivered security, currency stability, and infrastructure improvements2.

Meanwhile, hundreds of Afghan Special Immigrant Visa holders living in the US received official notices stating that the approval basis for their visas was revoked or denied3. The US State Department has issued 160,000 Special Immigrant Visas to Afghans since the program began in 20093.

Moroccan Security Forces Intercept Over 100 Migrants Near Spanish Enclave

Moroccan security forces detained 111 migrants near the town of Fnideq while stopping a mass attempt to cross into the Spanish enclave of Ceuta1. Moroccan police used tear gas to disperse groups of migrants gathered on hills near the border fence1. Moroccan news portal Le360 reported that up to 294 people, including 46 Moroccans, were arrested during Saturday's operation2. False posts on social media claiming the border would open for the Assumption holiday had spurred migrants to gather3.

The border situation follows earlier unrest. More than 72,000 migrants crossed into Ceuta during a major influx on July 30, which resulted in at least 96 deaths4. Spanish Interior Minister Fernando Grande-Marlaska stated that around 5,000 migrants remained in Ceuta4. Spain deployed over 500 additional police officers to Ceuta, bringing its total security force in the enclave to more than 1,600 personnel4. Following the massive surge in late July, Italy suspended Schengen free-movement arrangements with Spain and introduced border checks1.

Severe Wildfires Burn Across Europe Amid High Summer Temperatures

Wildfires are causing widespread damage across several European nations. In eastern Belgium, a severe wildfire destroyed a third of the High Fens natural reserve, forcing two nearby villages to evacuate1. Wallonia regional spokesperson Nicolas Yernaux called the blaze the largest wildfire in Belgium's recent history2. Local authorities reported the fire expanded from 80 hectares on Friday to over 2,700 hectares by late Saturday, scorching at least 16 square kilometres2. Belgium activated the European Union's civil protection mechanism to request firefighting aircraft and helicopters1.

In Croatia, a violent coastal wildfire killed at least one person and injured approximately 42 people3. In south-western France, authorities in Landes evacuated 650 residents due to a reignited forest fire1. French authorities have detained 474 people, including 183 minors, on suspicion of starting wildfires since July 14.

In western Germany, more than 2,000 people evacuated their homes as a fire in the Hürtgen forest approached the village of Gey4. Firefighters were forced to keep their distance after unexploded Second World War ordnance detonated in the burning forest4.

Bar Council Chairman Apologises to Students Following Supreme Court Criticism

Bar Council of India chairman Manan Kumar Mishra issued a letter on August 15 apologizing to law students for any hurt caused by his recent directives or words1. The apology came after a controversy involving graduating students at NALSAR University of Law1.

Around 450 NALSAR students had submitted six representations to their administration asking to reconsider inviting Chief Justice of India Surya Kant to their convocation2. The students objected to CJI Surya Kant over his remarks regarding a petition on alleged police brutality in Delhi3. In response, the Bar Council of India ordered state bar councils to defer the advocate enrolment of NALSAR's 2026 graduating batch, though it later withdrew the directive1.

CJI Surya Kant criticized the intervention by the Bar Council of India, stating that peaceful student protests were a matter strictly between him and the students3. The Supreme Court restrained the Bar Council of India and state bar councils from pursuing any punitive or criminal action against students and faculty over the issue4. Graduating students from NLSIU Bengaluru condemned the action against NALSAR graduates and demanded an apology5. A Supreme Court petition has now requested structural reforms to the Bar Council of India, including fixed term limits for its chairman5.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi Delivers 80th Independence Day Speech

Prime Minister Narendra Modi delivered his 13th Independence Day address from the Red Fort, introducing a seven-point reform plan named Saptadhara1. During his 75-minute speech, Prime Minister Modi focused heavily on national development and mentioned the youth of India approximately 50 times2. For the first time in 80 years, the national song Vande Mataram preceded the national anthem during the Red Fort ceremony1.

Prime Minister Modi announced several major public initiatives aimed at education, technology, and energy. He announced free online coaching for competitive examinations as well as an artificial intelligence skilling program designed to train one crore youth over the coming year2. On energy goals, the Prime Minister stated that India has set a target to reach 100 gigawatts of nuclear power capacity by 2047 following the passage of the SHANTI Act2.

During his address, Prime Minister Modi made strong political statements regarding domestic security and ideology. He stated from the Red Fort that 'dimagi Naxals' are threatening Indian society and called for them to be found and isolated3. Opposition leaders quickly criticized the remark3. Congress general secretary Jairam Ramesh called the 'dimaagi naxals' comment a sign of desperation3. Aam Aadmi Party chief Arvind Kejriwal criticized the Prime Minister following a brief pause in his speech, describing him as an under-confident, nervous, and shaky leader4.

Key opposition figures were absent from the official government celebration. Congress leaders Rahul Gandhi and Mallikarjun Kharge skipped the Red Fort Independence Day event for the second consecutive year5. Their absence was due to ongoing disagreements regarding seating protocols5.
