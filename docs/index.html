US Federal Reserve raises interest rates to 4% despite demands from Donald Trump

The US Federal Reserve raised its main interest rate on September 16, 20261. The central bank voted unanimously to approve the change1. The vote increased the benchmark interest rate by a quarter-percentage point1. This rate now sits in a range between 3.75% and 4%1.

US President Donald Trump demanded a rate cut after the decision1. Trump said that interest rates in the United States should be 1% or less1.

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh defended the rate hike1. Warsh said that inflation is too high1. He said that inflation has stayed high for too long1.

The Federal Reserve targets an inflation rate of 2% or below2. But US inflation has remained above that target for more than five years2. US consumer price index inflation reached 3.4% in August 20263. The Federal Reserve also raised its inflation forecast for 20264. The central bank raised the forecast to 3.7%4. Its previous projection was 3.6%4.

White House senior deputy press secretary Kush Desai described the rate hike as unfortunate4. Senate Democratic leader Chuck Schumer also criticized the interest rate increase2. Schumer said the hike will make everything more expensive2. He blamed the economic management of Donald Trump2.

Commercial banks moved quickly to adjust their rates after the announcement. Major US banks JP Morgan, KeyCorp, and BNY raised their prime lending rate on Wednesday2. The banks pushed the rate to 7% from 6.75%2.

Yields on 10-year US Treasury bonds rose past the 5% threshold after the rate hike announcement3.

A majority of Federal Reserve policymakers expect interest rates to be hiked again2. They expect another increase before the end of 20262. They project the rate will end up between 4% and 4.25%2.

In other action, the US Congress passed new legislation1. Lawmakers passed the Lindsey O Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 in a 262 to 159 vote1.

The country can participate in the European Union's 150-billion-euro fund for joint defense purchases1. Canada spends 2% of its gross domestic product on defense2. The nation committed with NATO partners to raise total defense spending to 5% of GDP by 20352.

Ten of the 27 European Union member states have not yet ratified the free-trade agreement with Canada3.

Pakistani corvette collides with Indian warship in Arabian Sea, triggering protests

A Pakistani naval corvette collided with an Indian warship in the North Arabian Sea on September 15, 20261. The incident occurred in the evening involving Pakistan Navy vessel PNS Hunain1. The Pakistani ship hit a frontline Indian Navy warship1.

The collision caused different levels of damage to the two vessels. PNS Hunain sustained damage during the impact1. The Pakistani corvette returned to harbour after the collision1. The Indian vessel suffered minor damage1. It continued its routine surveillance mission1.

A source told the Times of India about the actions of the Indian commander2. The source said the captain of the Indian warship exercised extreme restraint to prevent a severe accident2.

Both countries lodged formal diplomatic protests following the collision. The Indian Ministry of External Affairs summoned Pakistan's Chargé d'Affaires in New Delhi1. India delivered a strong diplomatic protest over the incident1.

India claimed the Pakistani ship broke a long-standing military agreement1. The Ministry of External Affairs said the Pakistani vessel violated Article 10 of a 1991 pact1. That agreement is the Agreement on Advance Notice on Military Exercises, Manoeuvres and Troops Movements1. Article 10 mandates that naval ships and submarines maintain a distance of at least three nautical miles3. The rule aims to prevent accidents in international waters3.

Pakistan also lodged a diplomatic protest over the collision. Pakistan's foreign ministry summoned Indian Chargé d'Affaires Satyanjal Pandey late on Wednesday night3. Pakistan summoned the diplomat in Islamabad1. Pakistani officials called the collision a highly provocative action1. They said the incident occurred inside Pakistan's exclusive economic zone1.

PNS Hunain is a Yarmook-class offshore patrol vessel1. Damen Shipyards built the vessel in Romania1. The Pakistan Navy commissioned the ship in July 20241.

This collision marks the first reported military incident between the two nations since May 20253. That earlier period involved four days of hostilities following the Pahalgam terror attack3. The last recorded collision between warships of the two nations occurred in June 20113. During that incident, PNS Babur touched INS Godavari during an anti-piracy patrol in the Gulf of Aden3.

Indian government rules out rollback of 0.4% UPI merchant transaction charges

The Union government ruled out any rollback of new charges on digital payments1. A 0.4 per cent Merchant Discount Rate will apply to UPI merchant payments of ₹2,000 or more2. The government set the start date for October 15, 20261. The fee is capped at ₹300 for transactions of ₹75,000 or more2. Small merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh per month through UPI QR payments are excluded2. Merchant transactions up to Rs 2,000 and all person-to-person transfers remain free1. Capital market payments will face a 0.02% rate capped at ₹300, while specified essential sectors face a flat ₹5 charge2.

Opposition leader Rahul Gandhi criticized Prime Minister Narendra Modi over the policy1. Gandhi called the framework a UPI tax1. Five Congress MPs served on the parliamentary committee that recommended the framework1. The Parliamentary Standing Committee on Finance presented its report on August 12, 20261. The committee adopted the report without recorded dissent1.

Jammu and Kashmir Chief Minister Omar Abdullah backed the decision3. Abdullah said operating digital payment infrastructure is costly3. Abdullah noted person-to-person money transfers carry no charge3.

Legal and retail challenges have emerged. Advocate Anjan Datta filed a public interest litigation in the Supreme Court to stop the framework2. Retailers Association of India CEO Kumar Rajagopalan said small merchants will think twice about accepting cash or UPI4. The Clothing Manufacturers Association of India expressed concern over extra pressure during the festive season4.
