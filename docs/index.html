Pentagon Criticized Over Media Blackout as US-Iran War Escalates

Former United States defense secretary Leon Panetta has strongly criticized the Department of Defense for withholding information about American casualties in the ongoing war with Iran. The Pentagon has not held a press briefing in over two months and recently restricted mainstream media access, replacing traditional journalists with political commentators and requiring military escorts inside the building. While official records show at least 18 US troops killed and about 430 injured, critics accuse the administration of trying to hide the true human cost of the conflict ahead of upcoming elections. Tensions have further expanded across the region as Iran-backed Houthi militants in Yemen launched missile strikes against Saudi Arabia and targeted oil tankers in the Red Sea. The attacks prompted Saudi airstrikes on Yemeni ports and briefly drove global crude oil prices above 100 dollars a barrel. Amid the growing confrontation, fake videos created using flight simulator games have also spread on social media falsely claiming that Iranian missiles shot down an American B-52 bomber.

Massive Wildfires Force Hundreds of Thousands to Evacuate Across Europe

Uncontrolled wildfires fuelled by intense heat and shifting winds have forced more than 250,000 people to flee their homes and holiday destinations across France and Spain. In southwestern France, about 197,000 residents were evacuated from areas around Bordeaux and the Cap Ferret peninsula as fires burned record amounts of forest, prompting the government to deploy military forces and transport aircraft. In Spain, approximately 70,000 people fled villages west of Madrid, where heavy smoke prompted health authorities to issue warnings advising residents to wear protective masks outdoors. Meanwhile in Scotland, emergency services declared a major incident as an 11-day-old wildfire in the Cairngorms National Park spread toward the village of Nethy Bridge due to strengthening winds, forcing local residents to move to shelter centers. Extreme heatwaves across Europe and parts of Asia continue to heighten fire risks and create severe public safety challenges.

International Criminal Court Dismisses Chief Prosecutor Over Misconduct Findings

Member states of the International Criminal Court have voted by a large majority to remove chief prosecutor Karim Khan following an independent investigation into sexual misconduct allegations. The court's oversight assembly concluded that Khan engaged in serious misconduct and breach of duty involving a female aide and tried to stop her from pursuing a formal complaint. Khan, a British barrister who has strongly denied the claims, is the first chief prosecutor to be dismissed in the history of the tribunal. The decision comes at a time of severe political pressure on the international legal body, as the United States recently launched a campaign to dismantle the court after Khan sought arrest warrants for top Israeli leaders over the war in Gaza. Following Khan's dismissal, Venezuela announced its intention to withdraw from the court, citing accusations of bias against nations in the Global South.

Deadly Russian Strike Hits Ukrainian Drone Event as Kyiv Targets Retail Hubs

A Russian missile attack on a military training ground near Kyiv killed at least 10 people and injured around 100 others during an exhibition demonstrating drone technology. Ukrainian officials described the daytime strike as a war crime, while Moscow claimed the high-precision attack targeted Ukrainian military personnel and defense developers planning operations inside Russia. On the same day, Ukrainian forces launched drone strikes against warehouses belonging to Wildberries, Russia's largest online retailer, near St. Petersburg and in occupied Crimea. Ukrainian officials said targeting these commercial logistics centers aims to disrupt supplies used for military equipment. Meanwhile in neighboring Romania, a NATO fighter jet shot down a stray drone that breached its airspace, reflecting the continued risk of the war spilling over into neighboring European countries.

Deadly Clashes in West Bank Village Trigger Israeli Military Crackdown

The occupied West Bank has experienced its deadliest single confrontation in months after a clash in the village of Tal left four Palestinians and two Israelis dead. The violence began when local residents confronted a group of Israeli settlers who had entered the village. During the struggle, a Palestinian man grabbed a weapon from an Israeli guard and opened fire before being shot dead by arriving soldiers, who also killed three of his family members. Following the incident, Israeli military forces launched an extensive crackdown, raiding dozens of homes and detaining nearly 50 Palestinian villagers while severely restricting local movement. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu announced plans to expand unauthorized Israeli outposts in the West Bank, drawing strong criticism from human rights groups and international observers who warned that the move would further fuel violence against Palestinian communities.

India's Education Minister Resigns Following Massive Student Protests

Indian Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan has submitted his resignation after weeks of widespread student-led demonstrations over a paper leak scandal involving the national NEET-UG medical entrance examination. The protest movement, organized primarily by the youth campaign Cockroach Janta Party, gained national momentum after activist Sonam Wangchuk carried out a 26-day hunger strike to demand accountability. Demonstrators gathered in massive numbers at Delhi's Jantar Mantar despite police attempts to disperse crowds using batons and tear gas. Facing growing political pressure from opposition leaders and widespread public anger, Pradhan stepped down, taking moral responsibility for the exam irregularities. Following his resignation, the government agreed to drop criminal charges against student protesters and provide financial compensation to families of students affected by the crisis.
