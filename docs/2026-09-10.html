IAEA Board Reports Iran to UN Security Council Over Nuclear Obligations

The International Atomic Energy Agency Board of Governors reported Iran to the UN Security Council for nuclear non-compliance1. This resolution marks the first time in 20 years that the 35-member board has reported Iran1. The United Kingdom, France, Germany, and the United States submitted the resolution together2. Twenty-three countries voted in favor of the measure1. Russia, China, and Niger voted against it1.

The IAEA suspects Iran holds about 400 kilograms of highly enriched uranium1. That amount could produce 10 nuclear weapons if enriched further1. Senior US officials said Iran had about 440 kilograms of 60% enriched uranium when the latest conflict started2.

Iran's mission to the UN said the non-compliance was caused entirely by aggression from the US and Israel1. Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi said US and Israeli attacks disrupted nuclear inspections2. He said those attacks were then used as a pretext for the resolution2. Iran circulated an 11-page document warning Western nations not to turn the IAEA into a tool of war3. China, Russia, and Iran said the vote risks politicizing a technical matter3. US President Donald Trump said the US may soon hit the Pickaxe facility south of Natanz3.
