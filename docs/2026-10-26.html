Lula da Silva wins fourth term as Brazil president after defeating Flávio Bolsonaro

Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva won a fourth term as president of Brazil on Sunday1. He defeated Senator Flávio Bolsonaro in the election1. Flávio Bolsonaro is the eldest son of former President Jair Bolsonaro1. This presidential win is unprecedented in Brazilian history1.

Lula da Silva previously served as president of Brazil between 2003 and 20111. He was imprisoned from 2018 to 2019 on corruption charges1. Those charges were later overturned1. He is 80 years old2. During the vote count in São Paulo, leftist federal lawmaker Erika Hilton spoke about Lula da Silva1. She called him a statesman and a giant1.

Flávio Bolsonaro is 45 years old2. He finished first in the first round of the presidential election1. Former President Jair Bolsonaro led Lula da Silva by more than 2.2 million votes in that first round3. Brazil has nearly 160 million registered voters3. More than 33 million of those voters did not participate in the first round3.

Supreme Court Justice Cristiano Zanin issued an order on election day3. Voting was extended nationwide across Brazil by two hours after his order3. In Salvador, a court in Bahia ordered the arrest of a top transportation official4. The arrest order was based on allegations of reducing public buses on election day4.

When Reuters provided live coverage with over 78% of votes counted in the runoff, Lula da Silva led with 51.21%3. Flávio Bolsonaro held 48.79% at that point3. With nearly 99% of votes counted, Lula da Silva received 51.75% of the vote2. Flávio Bolsonaro received 48.25% of the vote2.

Pro-Bolsonaro congressman Nikolas Ferreira spoke to journalists during the election2. He said either they win or they win2. He added that people had been warned2.

Former President Jair Bolsonaro was sentenced to 27 years in prison for plotting a coup4. His political group remains powerful in parliament1. Bolsonaro's Liberal Party and allied parties could control over 280 seats in Brazil's lower house1. They could also control up to 50 seats in the Senate1.

Bay of Bengal deep depression threatens Andhra Pradesh and Odisha with heavy rains

A weather system over the Bay of Bengal intensified into a deep depression on Saturday1. The system was centered about 870 km east-southeast of Visakhapatnam at 11:30 a.m. on October 25, 20261. Forecasters expect the system to cross the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam1. It is forecast to cross as a very severe cyclonic storm by the forenoon of October 28, 20261. The cyclone is expected to bring maximum sustained wind speeds of 115 to 125 kmph1. Wind gusts could reach 140 kmph1.

Heavy rain forecasts cover several regions in India1. Extremely heavy rainfall is forecast over Coastal Andhra Pradesh on October 27 and 281. Heavy rain is also forecast for Odisha and Telangana on October 281. Chhattisgarh is forecast to receive extremely heavy rainfall on October 28 and 291.

In Mankulam in Idukki, heavy weather caused a tree to fall on two cardamom plantation workers1. Both workers were killed in the incident1.

State governments have taken emergency measures1. Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu ordered a high alert1. He prepared 10 National Disaster Response Force teams and 11 State Disaster Response Force teams for relief operations1. Andhra Pradesh Central Power Distribution Corporation Limited cancelled employee leaves2. The power company established district control rooms to handle cyclone damage2. District administrations in Visakhapatnam, Anakapalli, and Alluri Sitharama Raju set up 24-hour control rooms3. They also prepped relief centres3.

The Odisha government cancelled leaves for district collectors, district magistrates, and critical officers4. Odisha Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi said that the government's primary objective is ensuring zero casualties5. Majhi ordered all schools, colleges, and Anganwadi centres closed in five districts on October 28 and 294. Those districts are Ganjam, Gajapati, Rayagada, Koraput, and Malkangiri4. Odisha deployed or placed on high alert 60 ODRAF teams, 17 NDRF teams, and 225 Fire Services teams ahead of the storm4.

Cyclone warning signals were hoisted at nine ports4. These ports include Chennai, Nagapattinam, Cuddalore, Karaikal, Pamban, and Thoothukudi4.
