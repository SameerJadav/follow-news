French police make nearly 1,000 arrests during cost-of-living protests

French police arrested nearly 1,000 people across France during protests on Saturday1. French authorities deployed 70,000 police officers across several cities to manage the crowd1. People protested over the cost of living and school conditions1.

Attendance figures differed between the government and trade unions1. The French interior ministry estimated that 410,000 people protested on Saturday1. Trade unions reported about 800,000 participants on the same day1. In Paris, the main protest march drew between 110,000 and 250,000 attendees, according to estimates2.

French President Emmanuel Macron decreed that only demonstrations organised by recognised trade unions would be permitted1. French authorities also imposed partial curfews in certain urban zones of Paris and other cities2. Major Paris museums closed on Saturday ahead of scheduled protests2. These closures included the Louvre and the Musée d'Orsay2.

French Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez said two people were seriously injured during the protests1. The serious injuries happened in Toulouse and Clermont-Ferrand1. Nuñez reported a 20-year-old man had his fingers blown off in Toulouse2.

Around 550,000 people participated in demonstrations across France the previous weekend1. Those earlier protests resulted in more than 2,500 arrests1.

Protests also spread to other European nations on Saturday. Italian police arrested nine people across eight cities during protests3. Italian police also briefly detained 371 others in those cities3. Police said 110 of the people arrested or detained in Italy were minors3. The Italian government deployed 500 soldiers to Milan3. This deployment happened after looting occurred during protests the previous weekend3.

Riot police in Tirana, Albania, fired tear gas to disperse thousands of anti-government demonstrators on Saturday1.

Ukraine restores Starlink satellite speeds as military operations continue

Ukraine announced on Saturday that its Starlink and Starshield satellite systems returned to full speed1. The systems were restored to pre-restriction operating speeds1.

The Ukrainian defence ministry said connectivity remained stable at speeds over 400 kph on Saturday1. Another report from the ministry put the stable speed at more than 250 mph2. Previous restrictions capped Starlink speeds at about 100 mph2, or 160 kph1. Ukrainian officials said those speed caps hampered their interception of Russian drones1.

SpaceX CEO Elon Musk said on X that Ukraine violated terms by using Starlink for offensive drone strikes1. Ukrainian presidential adviser Serhiy Beskrestnov said Ukraine is physically unable to use Starlink for long and medium range attack drone operations1. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said US President Donald Trump has no role in decisions made by Starlink and Starshield3.

Ukrainian forces attacked a chemical plant in the Russian city of Tolyatti on Saturday2. A Russian drone struck a residential building in Kyiv's Obolon District on Saturday, causing a fire2. Trump announced a deal to buy Russian diesel while urging Kyiv to stop striking Russian oil refineries3. Russia agreed to resume three-way negotiations with Washington and Kyiv next month4. Moscow and Kyiv also agreed to a local ceasefire around Oleshky, brokered by US First Lady Melania Trump4.

Israel locks down West Bank and demolishes homes in Hebron after shooting

Israel placed the occupied West Bank on lockdown after a shooting attack near Hebron on Friday morning1. Two Palestinian gunmen killed two Israeli soldiers and injured three others at a checkpoint2. The gunmen were then killed2.

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu announced he reviewed plans for a major West Bank military operation2. He called it the largest operation in the West Bank since 19672. Israeli Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich called for a comprehensive campaign similar to operations in Gaza and Lebanon2. Smotrich filmed himself wearing body armour in front of a Palestinian building being demolished in Hebron3.

Israeli forces demolished homes in the Ein Deir Baha and Farsh al-Hawa neighbourhoods2. Hebron Mayor Yousef al-Ja'bari said 2,200 people were forced from their homes4. Ja'bari also stated more than 1,000 people were displaced by demolitions in those two neighbourhoods2. Israeli Defence Minister Israel Katz confirmed about 2,000 people were displaced in Hebron4. The Hebron municipality estimated 35 hectares were destroyed or marked for destruction in Ein Deir Baha3.

Israeli forces arrested about 70 Palestinians during raids across at least 15 areas of the West Bank4. Forces fired a stun grenade at Al Jazeera journalists near the Farsh al-Hawa checkpoint4.

Gunmen open fire at church funeral in Jordan, killing up to nine people

Gunmen attacked St George's Greek Orthodox church during a funeral in Hamoud, Jordan1. Nine people were killed in the shooting, according to initial reports1. Reuters later updated the death toll from the shooting to eight people killed2. Jordan's health minister reported that 40 people were injured1.

The church is located in Karak, about 90 kilometers south of Jordan's capital, Amman3. Security camera footage showed three attackers firing at the church entrance1. The attackers took cover behind trees and a low wall1.

Jordanian Prime Minister Jafar Hassan said on X that the attack resulted from a terrorist ideology and blind extremism1. King Abdullah II visited Karak after the attack1. King Abdullah promised the perpetrators would be punished with an iron fist1. Jordan declared three days of national mourning after the church attack3.

Indian External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar condemned the church attack2. Jaishankar expressed solidarity with the Jordanian government and people2.

Brazilian presidential candidates hold final rallies ahead of run-off vote

Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva addressed supporters at Copacabana Beach on the final day of campaigning1. City Hall estimated that about 1.1 million people attended the beach rally in Rio de Janeiro2. Lula also attended a large rally on Paulista Avenue in São Paulo on Saturday afternoon2.

Nearly 159 million Brazilians are eligible to vote in the presidential election run-off on Sunday2. Senator Flávio Bolsonaro defeated incumbent President Lula in the first round of the election on October 43. Flávio Bolsonaro received 47 percent of the vote in that round, compared to Lula's 45 percent1.

Flávio Bolsonaro is the son of imprisoned former president Jair Bolsonaro3. The elder Bolsonaro is serving a 27-year prison sentence for attempting a coup after losing the 2022 election1.

A survey released on Saturday showed Flávio Bolsonaro leading with 46.3 percent against Lula's 45 percent1. During campaign rallies, Flávio Bolsonaro said he will join Donald Trump's Shield of the Americas regional security initiative if elected4.

Bay of Bengal weather system threatens South India with heavy rain and landslides

The India Meteorological Department reported that a low-pressure area over the Bay of Bengal persisted on October 231. On Saturday evening, the depression lay centered about 510 km off the Andaman Islands2. It was also 820 km off the Visakhapatnam coast2.

The weather agency warned that the low-pressure area is expected to intensify into a cyclonic storm by October 263. The agency issued a red alert for 13 districts in Andhra Pradesh for October 27 and 282. Officials advised fisherfolk not to venture into the sea from October 26 onwards3. Last year's cyclone Montha was classified as a severe cyclonic storm1. A very severe cyclonic storm carries maximum sustained wind speeds between 118 km/h and 221 km/h2. During cyclone Montha, the wind speed recorded at Machilipatnam reached 65 km/h2.

Heavy rain triggered landslides and damage in southern India. Overnight rainfall caused landslides at more than 10 locations along the Coonoor-Mettupalayam National Highway4. A landslide struck near Barliyar on the Mettupalayam-Ooty road4. Government bus driver Janakaraj died, and conductor Kesavan was injured when mud and trees buried their bus near Burliar1. A house in Barliyar village was completely washed away by rainfall, but its occupants escaped unharmed4. A goods vehicle carrying chickens was swept into a gorge during a landslide, though its driver escaped unhurt4.

Local officials took emergency action across affected areas. Nilgiris District Collector Lakshmi Bhavya Tanneeru advised people to avoid traveling on affected roads unless necessary4. The Nilgiris district administration set up emergency helpline 1077 for residents4. Stranded bus passengers were evacuated to relief camps in Coonoor4. In Puliyampatti, heavy rains flooded a Sri Lankan Tamil refugee camp, inundating more than 100 houses1. District Collector S. Durga Moorthy visited the camp to assess the damage1.

The weather system is affecting multiple regions across India. The Northeast monsoon has set in over Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Coastal Andhra Pradesh, Kerala, and other southern regions3. The weather agency predicted moderate to heavy rainfall across Tamil Nadu and Puducherry until October 261. Coastal and western districts of West Bengal expect heavy rainfall starting October 283. Thunderstorms with wind gusts up to 40 km per hour are expected over south Bengal districts3. The Odisha government announced the closure of schools and colleges in five districts on October 28 and 291. Telangana is experiencing a rainfall deficit and drinking water shortages due to El Nino1.
